# Appendix D: Glossary of Terms

---

## A

**Action principle**: In physics, the principle that the evolution of a system follows the path that makes a quantity called the "action" stationary (usually a minimum). The framework derives its update rules from an action S[s, J].

**Alchemy**: The ancient art/science of transmutation, combining practical chemistry with spiritual philosophy. Operated for over two millennia until supplanted by modern chemistry.

**Alpha (α)**: See *fine structure constant*.

**Annihilation**: In the framework, the event when a +1 state and -1 state occupy adjacent positions and both return to the void (0 state).

**Anthropic principle**: The observation that the universe's constants must be compatible with observers, since we are here to observe them. The framework goes further: observers are *necessary* for self-consistency.

**Apkallu**: The Seven Sages of Mesopotamian tradition, who brought civilization after the Flood.

---

## B

**Bell's theorem**: The mathematical proof (1964) that no local hidden variable theory can reproduce all predictions of quantum mechanics. Bell inequality violations have been experimentally confirmed.

**Born rule**: The principle that the probability of measurement outcomes equals the squared amplitude of the wave function: P = |ψ|².

**Brahman**: In Hindu philosophy, the ultimate reality underlying all phenomena. Identified with Atman (individual self) in non-dual teachings.

---

## C

**Cathar**: Medieval dualist movement in southern France, destroyed by the Albigensian Crusade (1209-1229).

**CKM matrix**: The Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix describing quark flavor mixing.

**Color charge**: In QCD, the property of quarks and gluons analogous to electric charge. Quarks carry one of three colors; gluons carry color-anticolor combinations.

**Complex multiplication (CM)**: A special property of certain elliptic curves that gives them extra symmetry. The lemniscatic curve has CM by the Gaussian integers Z[i].

**Corpus Hermeticum**: Collection of Greco-Egyptian wisdom texts attributed to Hermes Trismegistus.

---

## D

**Demiurge**: In Gnostic cosmology, the ignorant or malevolent creator of the material world, distinct from the true God.

**Discrete spacetime**: The hypothesis that space and time are not continuous but composed of minimal units (like pixels).

**Divergence**: The mathematical operation measuring how much a vector field spreads out from a point: ∇·J.

---

## E

**Elliptic curve**: A curve defined by an equation of the form y² = x³ + ax + b. Central to modern number theory and cryptography.

**Elliptic integral**: Integrals arising from the arc length of ellipses and related curves. The lemniscatic constant involves K(1/√2).

**Emergence**: The arising of complex properties or behaviors from simpler underlying rules. "Weak emergence" involves predictable consequences; "strong emergence" involves novel causation.

**En Sof**: In Kabbalah, the infinite, unknowable aspect of God prior to all emanation.

---

## F

**Fibonacci sequence**: The sequence 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21... where each term is the sum of the two preceding terms.

**Fine structure constant (α)**: The fundamental constant governing electromagnetic interactions, approximately equal to 1/137.036. Dimensionless.

**Flux field**: In the framework, the vector field J(v, t) ∈ ℝ³ defined on each lattice site, encoding energy/momentum potential.

**Freemasonry**: Fraternal organization tracing its mythology to the builders of Solomon's Temple, institutionalized in 1717.

---

## G

**G***: The lemniscatic constant as used in the framework, G* = √2 × Γ(1/4)² / (2π) ≈ 2.9587.

**Gamma function (Γ)**: The extension of the factorial function to complex numbers.

**Gauss constraint**: A conservation law requiring that the divergence of the flux field equals the charge density.

**Genesis**: In the framework, the event of manifestation—a void voxel transitioning to +1 or -1 state.

**Gnosis**: Greek for "knowledge"—specifically, experiential or salvific knowledge rather than intellectual information.

**Golden ratio (φ)**: The ratio (1 + √5)/2 ≈ 1.618, appearing in Fibonacci ratios, pentagrams, and natural forms.

**Grand Unified Theory (GUT)**: Theories attempting to unify the strong, weak, and electromagnetic forces.

---

## H

**Hard problem of reference frame context**: David Chalmers' term for the question of why subjective experience exists at all.

**Hermetic tradition**: The wisdom tradition attributed to Hermes Trismegistus, emphasizing correspondence between levels of reality.

**Higgs VEV**: The vacuum expectation value of the Higgs field, setting the electroweak scale at ~246 GeV.

**Hilbert space**: The mathematical space of quantum states, a complete inner product space (usually over complex numbers).

---

## J

**j-invariant**: A quantity characterizing elliptic curves. The lemniscatic curve has j = 1728.

---

## K

**Kabbalah**: Jewish mystical tradition, systematizing the emanation of the ten Sefirot from En Sof.

**Koan**: In Zen Buddhism, a paradoxical statement or question used to provoke insight beyond rational thought.

---

## L

**Lemniscate**: The figure-eight curve, defined by (x² + y²)² = 2a²(x² - y²).

**Lemniscatic constant**: The constant arising from the arc length of the lemniscate. See G*.

**Lorentz invariance**: The property of physical laws being the same for all observers in relative motion. Fundamental to special relativity.

---

## M

**Manifestation**: In the framework, the transition of a voxel from state 0 to state ±1.

**Master quadratic**: The equation f(x) = x² - 16G*²x + 16G*³ = 0, whose roots encode α and N_c.

**Maya**: In Hindu philosophy, the cosmic illusion that veils the true nature of reality.

**Me**: In Sumerian cosmology, the divine decrees or principles governing civilization.

**Moore neighborhood**: In cellular automata, the set of 26 adjacent cells in a 3D grid (3×3×3 cube minus center).

---

## N

**N_base**: In the framework, = 4, the minimal vertex count for 3D structure.

**N_c**: In the framework, ≈ 3, the number of color charges. Corresponds to x₋.

**N_eff**: In the framework, = 13, the effective degrees of freedom.

**Neoplatonism**: The philosophical tradition beginning with Plotinus, developing Plato's ideas into a systematic metaphysics of emanation.

---

## O

**Observer**: In the framework, any manifested structure (s ≠ 0) capable of triggering collapse through flux interaction.

---

## P

**Perennial philosophy**: The hypothesis that the world's wisdom traditions share a common metaphysical core.

**PMNS matrix**: The Pontecorvo-Maki-Nakagawa-Sakata matrix describing neutrino flavor mixing.

**Planck scale**: The scale at which quantum gravity effects become significant: length ~10⁻³⁵ m, time ~10⁻⁴⁴ s, mass ~10¹⁹ GeV.

---

## Q

**Qualia**: The subjective, felt qualities of experience—what it is *like* to see red or feel pain.

**Quadratic formula**: The solution to ax² + bx + c = 0: x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac))/(2a).

**Quantum chromodynamics (QCD)**: The theory of the strong force, describing quark and gluon interactions.

---

## R

**Rosicrucian**: Relating to the secret brotherhood announced in 17th-century manifestos, claiming ancient wisdom.

---

## S

**Sefirot**: In Kabbalah, the ten emanations or attributes through which En Sof reveals itself and creates the world.

**sLoop**: In the framework, a self-referential loop where an observing system is part of the system being observed.

**Standard Model**: The current best theory of particle physics, describing electromagnetic, weak, and strong forces.

**Strong force**: The force binding quarks into protons and neutrons, and nucleons into nuclei. Mediated by gluons.

**Supersymmetry (SUSY)**: A proposed symmetry relating fermions and bosons. No superpartners have been observed.

---

## T

**Ternary**: Having three states. In the framework, each voxel is in one of three states: {-1, 0, +1}.

**Tetractys**: The Pythagorean sacred symbol: a triangular arrangement of 10 points (1+2+3+4).

**Theosophical Society**: Organization founded by Blavatsky and Olcott in 1875 to synthesize world wisdom traditions.

**Tick**: In the framework, one discrete time step.

**Triad**: In the framework, a stable three-particle configuration proposed as an analog of nucleons.

---

## U

**U(1)**: The gauge group of electromagnetism (circle symmetry).

**SU(2)**: The gauge group of the weak force (special unitary 2×2).

**SU(3)**: The gauge group of the strong force (special unitary 3×3, color).

---

## V

**Varpi (ϖ)**: The lemniscatic constant in its traditional form, ≈ 2.622. Related to G* by G* = √(2/π) × ϖ.

**Void**: In the framework, the state s = 0—neither positive nor negative manifestation, but the substrate from which both emerge.

**Voxel**: A single lattice site in the framework's discrete space.

---

## W

**Wave function**: In quantum mechanics, the mathematical object (usually complex-valued) whose squared magnitude gives probability. In the framework, ψ = J_x + iJ_y.

---

## Y

**Yuga**: In Hindu cosmology, one of four ages in a cycle. Kali Yuga = 432,000 years; Maha Yuga = 4,320,000 years.

**Yukawa potential**: A force that falls off exponentially with distance: V(r) ∝ e^(-mr)/r. Describes the short-range strong force.

---

## Z

**Zodiac**: The band of constellations along the ecliptic, divided into 12 signs.

---
