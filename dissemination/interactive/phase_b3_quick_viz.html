Cube-corner test failed: pairwise distance histograms

For 8 voxels at the corners of a cube of side d, the 28 pairwise distances² are EXACTLY: 12 × d² (edges) + 12 × 2d² (face diagonals) + 4 × 3d² (body diagonals) = 3 unique values.

Engine reality:

L=32: 14 unique distance² values (vs cube prediction: 3)
L=48: 18 unique distance² values
L=64: 22 unique distance² values

What the central cluster at L=32 actually is

The 4 voxels around lattice center at L=32 — (16,16,15), (16,16,17), (16,17,16), (17,16,16) — are 4 of the 6 face-adjacent neighbors of (16,16,16). The missing 2: (15,16,16) and (16,15,16). This is a partial SC face-axis orbit (4 of 6 face-centers). The "n=4 = N_base" finding from §5.6.10 was actually this partial-SC configuration, NOT the BCC tetrahedron we'd hoped for.
