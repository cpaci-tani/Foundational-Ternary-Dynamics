Lattice Ontology

Core Node (s-shell)
Capacity: 2. The origin. Represents pure temporal oscillation with zero spatial displacement. Provides the foundational radial stability of the atom.
Face Centers (p-shell)
Capacity: 6. The 6 orthogonal Cartesian vectors. Mediates direct electrostatic interactions and forms rigid, directional chemical bonds.
Transverse Edges (d-shell)
Capacity: 10. The face-diagonals. Allows flux to bypass the Cartesian singularity, facilitating complex electron screening, metallic bonding, and magnetic equilibrium.
Corner Nodes (f-shell)
Capacity: 14. The body-diagonals. Represents maximal 3D spatial displacement, supporting the deepest transverse phase loops for heavy elements.
