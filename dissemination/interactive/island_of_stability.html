FTD annotation: magic gap [THEOREM]

The gap between the observed proton magic numbers 50 and 82 is 82 − 50 = 32 = 2·Nbase² = 2·4².

Arithmetic identity at its corpus tag — the tag labels the identity, not a derivation of magic numbers (those come from spin–orbit-split shell structure).

Computed: B, S, Qα, log₁₀Tα, fission-barrier proxy, and beta-valley distance are evaluated exactly from those inputs on every cell; the island's location is an output, not an input.

Presentation: ring jitter/harmonics, colormap, scan animation, SF-band rendering, and beta-dominance fading are visual encodings, not additional physics. No quantitative TSF or Tβ is computed anywhere.

Open: island center is model-dependent (proton-closure selector); expected model error ΔQα ≈ ±0.5–2 MeV → Tα within ~1–7 orders of magnitude (see measured rows); spherical shell correction only — the deformed actinide/transactinide region (Z ≈ 94–108) is under-stabilized and its SF bands are too pessimistic (real deformed ground states are shell-stabilized). The tool's claim is the landscape's shape, not absolute half-lives.
