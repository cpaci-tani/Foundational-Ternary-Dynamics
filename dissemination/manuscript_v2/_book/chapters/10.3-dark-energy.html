57  Dark Energy

“The accelerating expansion”

NoteKey Revelation

The universe is not just expanding: it is accelerating. Something with negative pressure, filling all of space, is pushing the cosmos apart. We call it dark energy, but we do not know what it is. (For the FTD mechanism, see Ch. 22.)

57.1 Discovery

57.1.1 Type Ia Supernovae

Standard candles for measuring distances: - Same peak luminosity - Measure apparent brightness - Calculate distance

57.1.2 The Surprise (1998)

Distant supernovae were fainter than expected, meaning they were farther away than a decelerating universe would predict. This led to the discovery that the universe’s expansion is accelerating.

57.1.3 Nobel Prize 2011

Perlmutter, Schmidt, Riess for this discovery.

With dark energy: Age = 13.8 billion years (correct)

CMB photons gain/lose energy crossing voids/clusters. Effect only works with dark energy.
