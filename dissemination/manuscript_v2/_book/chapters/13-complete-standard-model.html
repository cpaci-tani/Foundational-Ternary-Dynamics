15  The Complete Standard Model

This chapter collects all 46 Standard Model observables computed by FTD into a single reference table. Every entry uses only the Planck mass \(m_P\), the bridge constant \(G^*\), and the framework integers \(\{3, 4, 7, 13\}\). Zero free parameters.

15.1 13.1 The observable table

The epistemic status of each entry is classified as:

  • D (Derived): follows from FTD axioms via the derivation chain
  • PI (Parametric Insertion): FTD values inserted into a standard QFT formula
  • E (External): standard physics formula used without FTD derivation

15.2 13.2 Honest accounting

Category Count Description
Genuine derivations (D) ~20 \(\alpha\), gauge groups, coupling constants, vacuum energy — from lattice geometry
Parametric insertions (PI) ~20 Masses, mixing angles — FTD values in standard QFT formulas
External physics (E) ~6 Decay rates, running couplings — standard physics unmodified
Total ~46

The distinction between D and PI is critical:

  • A derivation means: both the formula and the numbers come from FTD.
  • A parametric insertion means: the formula comes from standard QFT, FTD provides the numerical inputs.
  • An external entry means: standard physics is used without modification.

Parametric insertions are legitimate computations but must not be called “derivations.” The assumption ledger (Appendix) tracks every external formula used.

15.3 13.3 What is not computed

FTD does not currently compute:

  • Individual quark masses (scheme-dependent; only ratios and combinations are meaningful)
  • CP violation phase \(\delta_{CP}\) (qualitative mechanism exists but quantitative value is [OPEN])
  • Neutrino mass ordering (normal vs. inverted hierarchy not determined)
  • Strong CP parameter \(\bar{\theta}\) (set to zero by the lattice’s discrete symmetry, but the proof is incomplete)

These are research frontiers, not gaps in the framework.
