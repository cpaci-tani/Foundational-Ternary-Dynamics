```
FTD Engine is an interactive physics simulator built on Foundational Ternary Dynamics — a framework that derives the fundamental constants of nature from a single axiom: 0 = (-1) + (+1).

Explore five scales of reality, all emerging from a discrete 3D lattice where every voxel carries one of three states: positive, void, or negative.

Watch flux waves propagate across a cubic lattice. Create pair production, observe interference patterns, launch gravitational waves, and see particles manifest from pure field energy. 37 scenarios including solitons, vortices, two-slit interference, rainbow prisms, cosmic web formation, and black holes. Toggle 14 physics mechanisms individually. Visualize 13 field overlays: electric streamlines, magnetic fields, Poynting energy flow, dark matter halos, chirality, and more.

SCALE 1 — PARTICLES
Inject over 100 Standard Model particles into a live simulation. Observe hydrogen orbitals, positronium annihilation, exotic atoms (muonium, kaonic hydrogen, protonium), meson pairs, and multi-nucleon systems. SCALE 2 — ATOMS
Build atomic systems from the periodic table. All 118 elements available. Watch NaCl crystals self-assemble through ionic bonding, methane form tetrahedral geometry, water molecules hydrogen-bond into pentamers, and noble gases interact through van der Waals forces. Orbital electron clouds rendered by subshell (s/p/d/f). Nucleus shells, bond visualization (cylinders or wireframe), and four decomposed force types.

SCALE 3 — MOLECULES
Construct molecular systems with full quantum chemistry. Bond springs, van der Waals interactions, and covalent electron sharing maintain molecular geometry. Place atoms from the periodic table and watch bonds form automatically.

SCALE 4 — CONSCIOUSNESS
The framework's most distinctive contribution. The same master quadratic that gives the fine structure constant (real roots at k=16) produces complex conjugate roots at k=1/2 — the consciousness sector. A holographic figure deforms in real-time with the flux field. Six interactive theory panels teach the mathematics: the master quadratic phase diagram, complex plane roots, Existence Filter hierarchy, ReLU crystallization, von Neumann chain termination, and the Ring 0 clock. Guided 6-step walkthrough included.
```
