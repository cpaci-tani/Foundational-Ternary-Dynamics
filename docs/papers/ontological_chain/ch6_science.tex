\section*{The move this layer makes: state in advance what kills you}
% =============================================================================

A framework earns the name \emph{science} not by accumulating agreements but by
stating, before the experiment runs, what result would end it. The previous five
layers built a structure: a finite ternary lattice (Ontology), a deterministic
commutative logic that declines the quantum measurement import (Logic), a forced
bridge constant and a forced quadratic with a precisely located wall
(Mathematics), an observer layer that reads the lattice (Philosophy), and a
computational effective field theory that \emph{reproduces} infrared physics
while only \emph{matching} the one number everyone wants
(Physics). This final layer turns the whole edifice around to face the world and
asks: \emph{where, exactly, does it break?}

The answer is the \textbf{deviation spine} --- six structural deviations from the
textbook QM/SR formalism, each carrying a discriminating protocol and an explicit
kill condition. These are not parameter post-dictions; they are places where
FTD's native posture (a commutative substrate, declared by FC-1; a sector-scoped
emergent metric, declared by FC-2) predicts that experiment will disagree with
the textbook \emph{structure}, not merely with a number. Crucially, this layer
is also where the two framework declarations themselves are made killable: the
constitution stakes in advance what would refute FC-1 and FC-2.

Read this chapter as a registry, not a result. Every row inherits its source's
tag; \textbf{inclusion in the spine never promotes a claim}. The deviation ledger
adds discriminating protocols, scope caveats, and kill conditions --- it adds no
new physics. Two rows demand special honesty and are flagged as such below: the
lab-Bell burden (PL-2), the framework's highest-risk entry, and the
proton-stability sub-claim of the structural nulls (PL-6), which a later audit
(\ftdid{0301}) demoted from a forced theorem to a selection.

\section{The lab-Bell burden: PL-2 read honestly}\label{sec:bell-burden}
% =============================================================================

The single most important sentence in this chapter is that \textbf{loophole-free
laboratory Bell experiments already measure $S>2$.} PL-2 is therefore \emph{not}
a prediction that laboratory Bell tests will read $2.000$. It is two things at
once: a scoped substrate bound that is \etag{thm}-grade, and an accepted open
burden that is the framework's most exposed flank. Softening either half would be
dishonest; the boundary box states both at their canonical tags.

\begin{boundarybox}[PL-2 lab-Bell honesty block --- the highest-risk row]
\textbf{The scoped bound (forced).} Any correlation experiment fully
implementable inside the commutative substrate --- with no measurement-map $M$ ---
satisfies realism, locality, and statistical independence, so Bell's theorem
applies and bounds $S\le2$. This is \etag{thm}-grade via \ftdid{0243} plus Bell;
the engine measures $S\approx1.95\text{--}2.00$ across every tested
configuration. (The apparent $S\approx3.6$ at $\sim49\%$ detection efficiency is
the known detection loophole, an artifact, not a violation.)

\smallskip
\textbf{The open burden (accepted, not discharged).} Real laboratories measure
$S>2$. FTD must therefore produce the observed $S>2$ \emph{at its observer
layer}. The existing aggregate-correlation mechanism is \etag{sel}-grade, and the
complexification it relies on is \emph{exactly} an instance of the measurement
import $M$ that FC-1 \emph{declines}. So FTD's account of laboratory Bell
violations is \etag{opn}. This is the deviation spine's highest-risk entry and
the most likely place the framework breaks.

\smallskip
\textbf{Kill condition.} Either a substrate-native (M-free) $S>2$ beyond
numerical error --- which kills FC-1 directly --- or a proof that \emph{no}
observer-layer mechanism consistent with FC-1 can yield the laboratory
statistics, which turns the burden into a verdict against the framework's account
of established experiments. ($\ftdid{0258}$ registry; FC-1 declared by
$\ftdid{0255}$.)
\end{boundarybox}

\section{The proton-stability correction (FTD-0301)}\label{sec:proton-correction}
% =============================================================================

The structural-nulls row PL-6 stands at its source tag \etag{thm}: the four
absence claims are theorems of the ontology as recorded in the constitution and
the parametric catalog. But one of its four sub-claims --- proton stability,
$\tau_{\text{proton}}=\infty$ --- has since been audited directly, and the audit
\textbf{demotes that sub-claim}. The precedence rule applies without exception:
\textbf{the LEDGER wins over the constitution}, so the corrected status governs.

\begin{conditionalbox}[Proton stability: $\tau_p=\infty$ is a selection, not a forced theorem]
The forcedness audit \ftdid{0301} (\etag{meas}, UNFORCED-METASTABLE) attacked the
claim that the discrete ontology \emph{forces} $\tau_{\text{proton}}=\infty$. Its
target was a four-proposition argument: (a) exact charge conservation, (b) the
proton as a locked triad of three same-sign particles, (c) locked triads exempt
from evaporation, (d) weak transmutation preserving triad structure. Two
engine-free legs falsify the \emph{forcing}:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item FTD has exactly one exact vector charge (the $U(1)$ sum $\sum s$) and
  \emph{no} baryon or $B\!-\!L$ current. Baryon number is an emergent
  three-quark colour-singlet \emph{cluster label}; conservation of $\sum s$ does
  not forbid $p\to e^+ + \pi^0$. Propositions (b)\,--\,(d) smuggle in a baryon law
  the framework never derives, and they contradict FTD's own baryogenesis sector.
  \item Source fact: the triad-binding lock fires only on \emph{same-sign}
  triples, whereas the physical proton $uud=(+1,+1,-1)$ is mixed-sign. The lock
  therefore \emph{cannot fire on a real proton}, so the evaporation-exemption of
  (c) is structurally inapplicable to its target object.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
The run of record confirms the reading: a cold proton is metastable and never
lock-protected; under heating with the weak channel on, $46/48$ trials transmute
$uud\to uuu$ (changing the charge), while the control transmutes $0/48$ ---
falsifying proposition (d). \textbf{Net epistemic change:} the
$\tau_{\text{proton}}=\infty$ tag is corrected \etag{thm}\,$\to$\,\etag{sel}
(emergent-metastable). The discrete ontology does not determine baryon stability,
and its native dynamics \emph{license} proton decay --- a boundary in the spirit
of mapping what discreteness does not reach. The other three structural nulls
(monopoles, SUSY, extra dimensions) are \textbf{unaffected} and remain
\etag{thm} at PL-6.
\end{conditionalbox}

\noindent
This correction does not weaken the falsifiability of PL-6 --- it sharpens it. An
observed proton decay no longer would merely contradict a theorem; under the
corrected reading the framework's own weak channel already supplies a decay
mechanism, so the open empirical question is the \emph{rate}, and the structural
prediction $\tau_p=\infty$ is withdrawn as a forced statement.

\section{Kill conditions for the framework commitments}\label{sec:kill-conditions}
% =============================================================================

The deviation spine makes the \emph{predictions} killable; the constitution
(\S6.2) goes further and makes the two \etag{ax}-class \emph{declarations}
themselves killable, stated in advance. This is what distinguishes a framework
commitment from an unfalsifiable stipulation: FC-1 and FC-2 are choices the
theorem leaves open, but each comes with a published list of what would refute
it.

\paragraph{FC-1 (the commutative substrate declines the measurement import $M$)
--- \etag{ax}-class, \ftdid{0255} --- is killed by any of:}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item a substrate-native pair of observables with $[A,B]\neq0$ derived from
  P1\,--\,P5 (this would refute the commutativity-independence premise
  \ftdid{0243} and is the single most direct refutation of the constitution);
  \item a P1\,--\,P5 derivation of Born statistics (this would refute the
  binding-without-sharpness account and collapse the PL-1 deviation);
  \item a substrate-native, $M$-free correlation experiment exceeding $S=2$
  beyond numerical error (the PL-2 kill condition).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{FC-2 (native arrow; Lorentzian metric emergent-IR and sector-scoped;
space $\perp$ time fundamental) --- \etag{ax}-class, \ftdid{0256} --- is killed by
any of:}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item exact $\gamma$ (or exact Lorentz invariance) at finite $L$ off the IR
  limit --- a measured \emph{absence} of the predicted UV corrections where the
  lattice requires them (PL-4/PL-5 returning null at finite scale);
  \item a P1\,--\,P5 derivation of global reversibility (this would refute the
  \ftdid{0253} boundary);
  \item failure of the measured IR approach --- the $L^{-2}$ law breaking on
  $\langle100\rangle$, or the $k^4$ anisotropy decay reversing: emergence claims
  that stop emerging.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{FC-0 (the $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ reading) --- \etag{ax}-class,
\ftdid{0254}/\ftdid{0249} --- is killed by} an inequivalent reading of the
lattice symmetry that produces the same spine (uniqueness failure), or a spine
theorem that fails under FC-0 (soundness failure).

\noindent
A point of physical-scale honesty travels with the FC-2 conditions: under the
calibration register the PL-4 and PL-5 UV effects are Planck-suppressed, so their
laboratory accessibility is bounded by current Lorentz-violation searches. The
engine-native versions are testable now; the physical versions are conditional and
long-horizon. The ledger states both readings, and so does this chapter.

\section{Methodology as institution}\label{sec:methodology}
% =============================================================================

A list of kill conditions is only as good as the discipline that holds the
project to them. FTD's epistemics are operational, not aspirational
(\etag{syn}, constitution \S6.3). Four institutional mechanisms make the
falsification machinery binding rather than rhetorical:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,itemsep=4pt,topsep=2pt,font=\normalfont\itshape]
  \item[Pre-registration.] Every measurement campaign is hash-locked before it
  runs: a SHA-256 of the runner and the analysis, plus a git tag, recorded
  before any data exists (the manifest is the project's preregistration record).
  The verdict logic is frozen first; the run is the run of record. This is what
  prevents a near-miss from being retrofitted into a prediction --- the deviation
  spine's $L^{-2}$ blind extension and the time-dilation blind run are the recent
  exemplars.
  \item[The golden-tick gate.] A bit-exact engine hash guards every
  physics-bearing change. An edit that perturbs the deterministic engine trace is
  caught immediately, so an instrument cannot silently drift between the claim and
  its check. Observation-only campaign code is proven golden-neutral (bit-identical
  hash with and without the counters) before its measurements count.
  \item[The LEDGER.] A single source of truth with append-only tag history.
  Promotions happen only there, with evidence; no document --- including the
  constitution and this one --- may promote a claim. When a kill condition fires,
  the row is retagged in the LEDGER first, then annotated downstream. The
  precedence ordering \textsc{ledger} $>$ constitution $>$ prose makes the
  \ftdid{0301} proton correction (\S\ref{sec:proton-correction}) binding on this
  chapter.
  \item[Anti-target discipline.] No near-miss scans, no substitution identities,
  no labeling of a parametric insertion as a derivation. Honest negative results
  are first-class deliverables: the spine's PL-1 Born closure, the PL-2 open
  burden, and the FTD-0301 boundary are all recorded as such, not buried.
\end{description}

\noindent
Together these turn the chapter's promises into commitments. The deviation spine
says what disagreement would look like; the kill conditions say what would refute
the declarations; and the institutional machinery guarantees that, when an
experiment lands, the framework cannot quietly move the goalposts. This document
was produced under those rules: it contains no new measurement and no promotion.

\section{What this layer does not settle}\label{sec:science-open}
% =============================================================================

The science layer maps the boundary of the framework's accountability; it does
not pretend the boundary is everywhere closed.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{The lab-Bell account is open} (\etag{opn}, \ftdid{0258} /
  \ftdid{0255}). The substrate bound $S\le2$ is forced, but reproducing the
  measured laboratory $S>2$ at the observer layer without smuggling back the
  declined measurement-map $M$ is unsolved. This is the highest-risk entry, and
  it is genuinely unresolved, not merely awaiting data.
  \item \textbf{Several deviations are scoped, not yet universal.} PL-1 is
  measured in the 6-neighbour substrate; its canonical 26-neighbour reading is
  \etag{cnj} (testable in v2). PL-4 is scoped \etag{meas} on $\langle100\rangle$
  at moderate velocity, with the ultra-relativistic diagonals still unconverged
  (\etag{opn}). The structure is established where it is measured; its
  universality across the full configuration space is not.
  \item \textbf{The physical, as opposed to substrate-native, versions of
  PL-4/PL-5 are long-horizon.} Under the calibration register the UV
  corrections are Planck-suppressed; whether any laboratory can reach them is a
  question this layer poses but does not answer.
  \item \textbf{Kill conditions are stated, not yet exercised at the physical
  scale.} FC-1 and FC-2 carry published refutation lists, but most of those
  conditions can presently be checked only engine-natively. The framework has
  said what would end it; the experiments that could deliver the verdict, at the
  physical scale, are still to come.
\end{itemize}
