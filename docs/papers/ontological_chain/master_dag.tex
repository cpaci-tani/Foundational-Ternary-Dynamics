  \caption[The Master Chain DAG]{\textbf{The Master Chain DAG.}\enspace The six
    layers of the chain, top to bottom, in the constitution's order. Shape and
    rule encode epistemic status (never colour): a \emph{double-ringed} node is
    an axiom or a framework declaration (FC-0/1/2), a \emph{diamond} is the
    $G^{*}$ hub, a \emph{solid} box is theorem-grade, a \emph{thin-solid} box is
    a selection/synthesis, a \emph{dashed} box is below theorem grade (a
    strongly-motivated conjecture or a tiered result), and a \emph{double-ruled}
    box is a boundary---a mapped wall or an open kernel. Solid arrows read
    ``constructed from''; dashed arrows are weak links or ``accountable to''.
    The construction spine runs $\mathbb{Z}[i]\to G^{*}\to$ master quadratic
    $\to$ the nine-result spine $\to$ the IR predictions. The two double-ruled
    walls---the $\alpha$ readout boundary (MC-T4.3) and the lab-Bell burden
    (PL-2)---are exactly where the chain stops being forced: the dashed arrow
    from the $\alpha$ wall down to $x_+=1/\alpha$ is why that identification
    remains a strongly-motivated conjecture, not a theorem.}
