\section*{The move this layer makes: who reads the lattice, and at what cost}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The move this layer makes}

Every preceding layer spoke in the third person. Ontology said \emph{what
exists}; logic said \emph{what follows}; mathematics said \emph{what is forced}.
None of them needed an observer. The substrate evolves under the five postulates
with absolute, god's-eye simultaneity whether or not anything inside it is
looking. The move the philosophy layer makes is to ask the first-person
question that quantum mechanics built its entire formalism around --- \emph{who
reads the lattice, and what can a reader embedded in the lattice know?} --- and
to answer it \emph{without} importing the quantum measurement apparatus to do
so.

The intuition is a deliberately deflationary one. An ``observer'' in FTD is not
a mind, a consciousness, or an agent; it is a \emph{reference frame structure}
--- a subsystem of the lattice that happens to contain a model of itself. The
canonical example is mundane: the centre voxel of a $27$-block, which ``sees''
its world only through the surrounding shell. Reading is restriction. What a
frame can know of the configuration --- including of itself --- is whatever
survives the projection from the full state down to the frame's own readout.
There is no collapse here, no special role for awareness, and no deformation of
the underlying algebra. Measurement becomes epistemics: the bookkeeping of what
a finite thing inside a classical world is structurally barred from knowing
about itself.

That deflation buys something most interpretations cannot: \emph{exposure}.
Because the early layers are committed --- the postulates are frozen, the
framework commitments are declared --- the observer layer cannot quietly absorb
whatever the laboratory reports. When a measurement arrives that the quantum
formalism reproduces exactly, an interpretation that \emph{also} imports that
formalism is never separated from it by data. FTD, having declined the import,
\emph{can} be. The thesis of this chapter is that ontology-first is therefore
\emph{more} falsifiable, not less --- and the supersession matrix is the precise
ledger of where FTD stakes outcomes its neighbours decline to stake. This is an
argued layer: it carries no derivations of its own, only the disciplined
re-statement of claims proved elsewhere and the honest accounting of what is
still open.

\section{The vocabulary: reference frame structure, not consciousness}
\label{sec:phil-vocab}

FTD's account of observation is stated entirely in the canonical vocabulary of
the reference-frame reference document
(\texttt{REF\_REFERENCE\_FRAME\_VOCABULARY.md}), which exists precisely to strip
the qualia and Hard-Problem baggage from terms the corpus once used loosely. The
load-bearing distinction is between a \emph{structural} property and a
\emph{dynamical} one:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Reference frame structure} (structural) --- the property of a
    subsystem that contains a model of itself: a fixed-point / strange-loop
    property. A \emph{local reference frame} is the lattice region that
    instantiates it (canonically, the centre of a $27$-block). The
    \emph{reference frame projection} is the operator mapping the full
    (noumenal $3^3$) state to the frame's (phenomenal $2^3$) readout; in
    algebraic terms it is an observable-algebra \emph{restriction}. The
    \emph{frame-relative readout} is what the structure thereby knows of itself
    --- the deflated replacement for ``experience.''
  \item \textbf{Active frame dynamics} (dynamical) --- the capacity of a system
    to select its own trajectory by its own internal frame-relative readout, as
    distinct from being driven externally. This replaces the loaded terms
    ``agency'' and ``free will'' with a physics-compatible one, and remains
    embedded in the deterministic dynamics of P5.
\end{itemize}

Three discriminations are made explicit, because the framework needs them and
the old language hid them. First, reference frame structure is \emph{not} the
same as realised active frame dynamics: almost every voxel sits at the centre of
some $27$-block, so reference frame structure is generic; what is rare is its
\emph{realisation} in trajectory selection (the \emph{active-frame threshold}
remains an open structural problem). Second, a frame-relative readout is
\emph{not} guaranteed faithful --- there is no axiom that a frame reads itself
correctly, which is exactly the absence-of-omniscience any physical observer
must have. Third, active frame dynamics is \emph{not} libertarian free will: the
trajectory is selected by frame-internal structure, not by a non-physical
chooser. The Hard Problem is not solved here; it is \emph{re-described} as the
open problem of frame-relative emergence and then set aside as out of scope.

\section{The observer layer: frames, readouts, and the binding blind spot}
\label{sec:phil-observer}

The layered picture has two strata
(\ftdid{0254}~\S4.1, \etag{syn}~$+$~\etag{opn}). The \emph{substrate frame} is
the god's-eye view: it holds the full configuration and evolves deterministically
under P1--P5 with absolute simultaneity. \emph{Internal frames} are subsystems
whose access to the world --- and to themselves --- is restricted by the
reference frame projection. What quantum mechanics treats as the measurement
problem, FTD treats as the epistemics of internal frames embedded in a classical
substrate: \emph{measurement is frame-relative readout under a derived
restriction, not a dynamical collapse and not an algebra deformation} (this last
clause is FC-1, carried over from the logic layer).

The one genuinely \emph{derived} ingredient of this picture is the blind spot,
and it is honestly split.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{The binding half} \etag[ partial]{thm}~(\ftdid{0227}). A finite
    internal observer --- a subsystem with $M$ pointer states modelling a total
    of $N > M$ states, \emph{itself included} --- necessarily loses access to
    part of its own state. This is classical finite self-reference (a
    pigeonhole / diagonal argument); it is non-circular and takes no quantum
    input. Every internal frame therefore carries a \textbf{self-blind-spot}.
    The corresponding LEDGER row (\ftdid{0227}) records this binding half as
    \textsc{derived} and the whole row as \textsc{partial}; the conservative
    reading used throughout this document is ``\etag{thm}, partial.''
  \item \textbf{The sharp half} \etag{opn}. The Spekkens \emph{knowledge-balance}
    --- the blind spot rotating symmetrically over complementary bases, the
    feature that would make the restriction genuinely quantum-like --- requires
    the full symplectic $S_3 = \Z/2 \rtimes \Z/3$ action. The $\Z/2$ is
    FTD-native (the $J^2 = -I$ quarter-conjugacy); the $\Z/3$ is
    \textbf{missing} (the $N_c = 3$ candidate for it was diagnosed as apophenia,
    \ftdid{0228}, \etag{cn}). \emph{Binding-without-sharpness} is the framework's
    honest state on observation.
\end{itemize}

A consequence, flagged as a \emph{post-hoc} account rather than a prediction: a
classical restriction without the symmetric balance yields
classical-with-noise detection statistics rather than Born's $|\psi|^2$ --- which
is consistent with the engine's measured Rice/Gaussian threshold statistics
(\S\ref{ch:science} PL-1). This explanation was constructed \emph{after} the
measurement; it is an explanation candidate, not a pre-registered prediction,
and is tagged accordingly.

The honest gaps kept open at this layer are three: the \emph{sharpness} of the
restriction just described (\etag{opn}); the \emph{active-frame threshold}
(which configurations realise active frame dynamics --- an open structural
problem); and a dedicated formalisation of frame self-identification (the
GUID$+$XYZ construction), which does not yet exist as a foundational document.

\section{Position among the alternatives --- the supersession matrix}
\label{sec:phil-supersession}

\emph{structural, not rhetorical} (\ftdid{0254}~\S4.2, \etag{syn}; per-cell tags

tradition. FTD differs from each in two specific ways: it \emph{declines an

formalism, sometimes the continuum, sometimes reversibility), and in return it
\emph{offers deviations} where the tradition reproduces the standard formalism
exactly. The matrix is the ledger of those two moves.

Three structural contrasts carry the supersession claim, and each is a
re-statement, not a new argument.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=4pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Against the QM family (Copenhagen / MWI / RQM / Bohm).} All four
    import the non-commutative Hilbert-space formalism as fundamental. FTD
    \emph{proves} its substrate cannot generate that formalism (the observable
    algebra $A_5$ is strictly commutative, \etag{thm}, \ftdid{0243}) and then
    \emph{declines} it (FC-1, \etag{ax}-class, \ftdid{0255}). Where the four
    reproduce textbook QM exactly, FTD predicts \emph{structural deviations}
    (\S\ref{ch:science}). FTD is thereby \emph{more falsifiable, not more
    general}: it stakes outcomes the others cannot.
  \item \textbf{Against 't Hooft's CA program --- the nearest relative.} Both
    posit a deterministic, local, discrete classical substrate. 't Hooft's
    program \emph{aims to reproduce QM exactly} via a template-basis map (an
    instance of the import $M$) and hopes for reversible automata. FTD declines
    $M$ and, decisively, declines reversibility too --- its own finite
    (manifestation) sector is provably irreversible and argues \emph{against} a
    global reversibility postulate (FC-2, \etag{ax}-class, \ftdid{0256}). Same
    substrate class; opposite commitments on both forks.
  \item \textbf{Against string theory.} FTD's structural null-predictions are
    direct contradictions of string theory's characteristic imports: no SUSY
    partners, no extra dimensions, no monopoles, exact proton stability
    (the nulls are \etag{thm}-grade as a class, \S\ref{ch:science} PL-6; the
    proton sub-claim specifically carries a \etag{sel} correction, \ftdid{0301}).
    Continued null results at colliders and proton-decay observatories are
    evidence \emph{for} FTD's ontology and \emph{against} those imports.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Why ontology-first is more falsifiable, not less}
\label{sec:phil-falsifiability}

The chain Ontology $>$ Logic $>$ Mathematics $>$ Philosophy $>$ Physics $>$
Science would be vacuous if ``Science'' merely decorated the front of the chain
(\ftdid{0254}~\S4.3, \etag{syn}). The constitution's discipline is the reverse,
and it is the central philosophical claim of this layer: \emph{because the early
layers are committed, the framework cannot retreat when measurements arrive.}

The argument is short and turns on a single asymmetry. A framework that
reproduces the QM/SR formalism exactly --- by importing it --- can never be
\emph{separated} from QM/SR by experiment: any experiment QM/SR survives, the
importing framework survives by construction. Its empirical content relative to
QM/SR is therefore null. FTD, by \emph{declining} the imports (FC-1, FC-2),
forfeits that safety. Each declination converts a borrowed certainty into a
stakeable prediction:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item declining the measurement map $M$ (FC-1) turns ``the substrate must
    reproduce Born statistics'' into the deviation PL-1 (Rice, not Born), which
    a single clean construction can refute;
  \item declining global reversibility and the substrate-level Lorentzian metric
    (FC-2) turns ``$\gamma$ is exact at all scales'' into the deviation PL-4
    (a $\gamma$-departure that must die as $L^{-2}$, and must \emph{not} vanish
    where the lattice requires it), and ``space is exactly isotropic'' into PL-5
    (a UV anisotropy dying as $k^4$);
  \item committing to the discrete ontology turns string theory's
    open-handedness about SUSY, extra dimensions, and proton decay into
    \etag{thm}-grade nulls (PL-6) that a single positive detection would break.
\end{itemize}

Crucially, the commitments are themselves staked. The constitution states in
advance the observations that kill each one (\S\ref{ch:science}, FC-1 / FC-2
kill-conditions): a substrate-native pair of observables with $[A,B] \neq 0$, or
a P1--P5 derivation of Born statistics, would kill FC-1; exact $\gamma$ at finite
$L$, or a P1--P5 derivation of global reversibility, would kill FC-2. An
ontology that names its own executioners is the opposite of an
unfalsifiable one.

This is not falsifiability-as-sole-criterion smuggled back in. The framework's
broader stance rejects treating falsifiability as the \emph{only} mark of a good
theory; ontology-first is also a commitment to saying what \emph{is} before
saying what is \emph{measured}. But rejecting falsifiability-as-sole-criterion
is not licence to wave measurements away. The discipline that backs the claim is
mechanical: \emph{every} measured phenomenon must appear somewhere --- in the IR
catalog (\S\ref{ch:physics}), in the deviation ledger (\S\ref{ch:science}), or
in the not-yet-delivered honesty row of Table~\ref{tab:supersession}. Nothing
measured is permitted to be silent.

\section{What this layer does not settle}
\label{sec:phil-open}

The observer layer is the most interpretively ambitious layer of the chain, and
correspondingly the one with the most that is still \etag{opn}.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{The sharpness of the restriction} \etag{opn}. The binding
    blind-spot is a theorem (\ftdid{0227}, partial); the \emph{symmetric}
    knowledge-balance that would make it quantum-like is not --- the missing
    $\Z/3$ is a genuine gap, not a notational one. Until it closes, the
    framework's account of measurement is \emph{binding-without-sharpness}, and
    this document claims no more.
  \item \textbf{The observer-layer Bell account} \etag{sel}~$+$~\etag{opn}. The
    substrate proves $S \le 2$; laboratories measure $S > 2$. FTD's account of
    that gap lives at the observer layer and is a \emph{selection}, not a
    derivation --- and it is the framework's \textbf{highest-risk} commitment.
    It is carried forward, unsoftened, as PL-2 in \S\ref{ch:science}.
  \item \textbf{The active-frame threshold} \etag{opn}. Which configurations
    cross from mere reference frame \emph{structure} (generic) into realised
    active frame \emph{dynamics} is an unsolved structural problem; the
    framework has the vocabulary for it but not the criterion.
  \item \textbf{The Hard Problem} is \emph{not} addressed. It is re-described as
    the open problem of frame-relative emergence and explicitly placed out of
    scope. This layer accounts for what a frame can \emph{know} of itself, not
    for why there is something it is like to be one.
\end{itemize}

The supersession posture, then, is exactly what it says: a program with a
falsifiable spine, holding two named walls open --- the sharpness gap and the
lab-Bell burden --- and accountable, layer by layer, to the physics and the
science that follow.
