## Critical Distinction: Predictions vs Postdictions

A **prediction** is a claim made BEFORE the experimental result is known.
A **postdiction** (or retrodiction) is a claim that matches ALREADY KNOWN data.

Most of FTD's "~126 predictions" are actually **postdictions**:
- α = 1/137.036 was known in 1947
- sin²θ_W = 0.231 was measured in 1983
- All particle masses were measured before FTD was formulated

This does not invalidate the framework (matching known data is necessary), but it does mean these are not genuine predictions in the scientific sense.

**This document lists only GENUINE PREDICTIONS made before experimental confirmation.**
