# FTD-0762 — Moving-Dressing / Observer-Chart Discriminator

**Status:** [DERIVED TEST CRITERION; OUTCOME-AWARE FORENSIC]

## Defect isolated

FTD-0761 evolved a support-invariant compact core after a collective boost,
but its post-initial FTD-0754 observer and support-ladder gates all returned
false.  Those Boolean failures do not distinguish a physically non-comoving
field from an observer whose selected bound-field construction is undefined at
the evolved center.

The finite-support preparation used by both observers contains the domain
condition

\[
 \left\|\frac{x_+ + x_-}{2}
 -\operatorname{round}\!\left(\frac{x_+ + x_-}{2}\right)\right\|
 \le 10^{-12}.
\]

It therefore has exact integer-translation covariance, but it has no declared
fractional-translation chart.  A moving pair can leave that domain while its
relative state, exact common-action transaction, and support-invariant matter
predicate remain valid.

## Same-geometry discriminator

At the first failed FTD-0761 checkpoint, construct three read-only records.

1. **Evolved:** the actual evolved matter and field state.
2. **Same geometry:** a fresh finite-support preparation from the evolved
   constituent positions and momenta without moving them.
3. **Rigid recenter:** subtract only the centroid's fractional part from both
   constituents, preserving relative geometry and momenta, and freshly prepare
   the selected finite-support field.

The interpretation is fixed:

- same-geometry preparation valid but evolved observation invalid: physical
  dressing mismatch is exposed;
- same-geometry preparation invalid, rigid-recenter preparation valid, and the
  only failed preparation precondition is fractional centroid: the registered
  observer has an integer-center chart obstruction, so FTD-0761's observer
  failure cannot be used as evidence of physical incoherence;
- both preparations valid but both observations invalid: a downstream observer
  algebra defect is exposed;
- rigid recenter invalid: infrastructure is unresolved.

Rigid recentering is a diagnostic control, not a repair to the dynamics and not
evidence that the recentered field is the physical moving dressing.

## Consequence

An integer-center chart obstruction reopens the complete-moving-state question.
The next admissible construction must define a fractional-center bound-field
observer or derive a jointly moving matter/field preparation.  It may not
promote the raw moving core to a particle before that gate closes.
