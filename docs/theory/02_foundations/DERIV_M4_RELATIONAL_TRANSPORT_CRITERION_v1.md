## 1. Why site hopping is not motion

Let a complete selected matter--field state be

```text
X=(x_a,p_a,q_a; E,B),
```

where `x_a=anchor_a+remainder_a` is the effective position of constituent
record `a`. For the certified two-polarity family define

```text
C(X)=(x_+ + x_-)/2,
r(X)=x_- - x_+.
```

FTD-0760 records constituent anchor changes while the net matter momentum is
numerically zero. An anchor change can therefore be an internal chart event.
Conversely, `C` can move continuously before any anchor changes. Neither
manifested-anchor displacement nor `site_hops` alone defines object motion.

The state-only transport observable is the pair

```text
Delta C(t,t+T)=C(X_{t+T})-C(X_t),
I(X_t)=(r(X_t), p_-(t)-p_+(t), P_core(X_t)),
```

with the internal coordinates compared after removing `C`. A matter
worldline over a finite interval exists when:

1. `P_core(X_t)` remains true with strict graph and energy margins;
2. `C(X_t)` is causal and translates secularly;
3. the internal relational state remains bounded rather than dispersing;
4. the exact common-action, continuity, Gauss, energy, and local inversion
   gates remain satisfied.

This definition permits anchor turnover. It does not permit an object to be
declared mobile merely because constituents hop or because a fixed observer
chart changes.

## 2. The necessary two-stage experiment

There are two logically distinct questions.

### M4a — Does a moving member of the family exist?

Starting from the certified tick-160 parent, apply a declared collective
initial boost

```text

```

where `d` is a registered lattice direction. This preserves relative
momentum and injects the known external energy

```text

```

Thereafter the state evolves only through the unchanged common action. This
test answers whether the M3 family contains a coherent translating history.
It does not explain where the boost came from and cannot establish native
force, recoil, or autonomous motion.

### M4b — Can the field cause that motion?

Only after M4a succeeds, replace the boost with a separately evolved incoming
matched face/edge field packet. Compare three histories:

```text
full matter + packet,
resting matter baseline,
free packet control.
```

The nonlinear interaction field is the complete-state difference left after
the baseline and free driver are removed. Momentum transfer is causal only if
the matter histories agree before packet contact and diverge afterward. A
trailing wake is licensed only if baseline-subtracted field energy detaches
behind a still-coherent translated core.

This separation prevents a failed packet initializer from being mistaken for
absence of moving matter, and prevents an imposed boost from being presented
as derived electromagnetism.

## 3. Momentum accounting

The lattice does not possess exact continuous translation symmetry. Exact
continuum momentum conservation is therefore not imposed as a microscopic
identity. Nevertheless, every candidate must record

```text
P_candidate = sum_a p_a + P_spline(E,B)
```

and its per-step defect. A small defect supports field-balanced transport. A
nonzero defect is a measured substrate/Peierls reaction, not permission to
silently discard recoil. Without an explicit substrate momentum state, such a
history may establish selected translation but not isolated momentum closure.

This yields three distinct statuses:

- **coherent transport:** the relational core translates and all exact
  action/energy/inverse gates pass;
- **field-balanced transport:** coherent transport plus controlled total
  matter--field momentum;
- **substrate-reaction unresolved:** coherent transport with a non-negligible
  translation-momentum defect.

## 4. Dressing and wake

At each time, the complete electric field may be compared with the selected
state-determined bound projection `E_bound[X_t]`:

```text
E = E_bound[X_t] + R_t.
```

The bound projection is an observer, not a material membrane. Co-moving
dressing means that its centered profile follows `C(X_t)`. A wake is not the
entire residual `R_t`; it is the part of the interaction residual that remains
behind the translating core after subtracting both the resting-object and
free-driver controls. Outgoing field in a stationary history remains
radiation/background evidence, not a wake.
