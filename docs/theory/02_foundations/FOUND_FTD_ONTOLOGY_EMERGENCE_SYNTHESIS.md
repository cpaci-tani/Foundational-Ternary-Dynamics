## 2. The primitive ontology

### 2.1 Existence is local distinction, not occupation of a container

FTD does not place voxels inside a prior space. The adjacency structure is what spatial position means. A coordinate is a relational address in the cubic graph, not a location inside a larger continuous vessel. There is no ontic “outside” of the substrate in the model because an outside would be an additional containing type. `[AXIOM/SYNTHESIS]`

Likewise, “unbounded” must not be confused with a completed infinite set. At every specified site, adjacent sites are available; every realized configuration and every causal inquiry remains finite. Claims requiring `L -> infinity` must be replaced by finite-support, decay, or epsilon-versus-window statements. `[AXIOM — P1; infinity reframe]`

This gives a disciplined version of “the world can always continue without already existing as a completed totality.” It is a local generative rule, not a count of all sites.

### 2.2 State and disposition

The ternary state has three actual values:

```text
-1  negative manifestation
 0  no signed manifestation at this site
+1  positive manifestation
```

The values are not three substances. FTD's graded-monist reading is one substrate with different ontological grades:

- `J`: what the substrate is disposed to do;
- `s`: what is manifest now;
- stable patterns of `s`, current, and field: what we call matter and its properties.

The actual zero needs special care. A site with `s=0` is not automatically “nothing in every sense.” It may sit inside nonzero dispositional flux, a balanced current pattern, a potentiality support, or an environmental record. Conversely, zero total flux in a block can mean either empty support or exact cancellation of active contributions. A complete ontology therefore needs both a signed/resultant quantity and an activity/resource count.

### 2.3 Identity is a causal relation, not a permanent label

One voxel is not an electron, atom, observer, or clock. Current matter work supports a narrower candidate:

> **A matter candidate is a localized recurrent equivalence class of signed manifestation events, oriented currents between them, and their dynamically coupled field state.** `[FOUNDATIONAL SYNTHESIS — THEOREM-BOUNDED CANDIDATE]`

```math

where `I_n` is an oriented current on relations. The two endpoint snapshots determine only the divergence `D I_n`; they need not determine the cycle part of the current. Therefore identity cannot generally be read from still images alone. It resides in the complete state-generated transaction orbit: a causal world-tube modulo translations and consistent relabelings.

This sharpens the intuition that no two manifested events share exactly the same origin tick, place, and transaction history. That is a plausible **identity principle**. It is not yet the Pauli exclusion principle. Fermionic antisymmetry, spin-statistics, and a Fock-space or equivalent native structure remain separate physical burdens. `[CONJECTURE; fermion recovery OPEN]`

### 2.4 Birth, persistence, and expiry

Every physical instantiation in FTD occupies a tick interval. A localized pattern forms, persists while its recurrence conditions hold, and ends when that recurrence no longer closes. Its existence interval can be represented as

```math

where `n_end` may be unknown to a local observer and need not be fixed in advance. `[CONJECTURE]`

This does **not** imply that every abstract mathematical set literally has a clock. It says that every physically instantiated token, computation, record, and observer is a process in the substrate's global order. The abstract set is a description; its physical inscription has a birth, maintenance history, and possible expiry.

Identity after a constituent change is therefore neither numerical sameness of voxels nor an immaterial essence. It is continued membership in a dynamically stable relational class. Matter is closer to a maintained whirlpool than to a labeled bead, while still being fully objective and deterministic at the complete-state level.

## 3. Time: global order, local clocks, and the arrow

### 3.1 The global tick

FTD already has a fundamental clock in the minimal sense: the successor relation

```math
n \longmapsto n+1.
```

This is one shared update order for the substrate. It provides absolute simultaneity at the ontic level and a privileged foliation. Local systems do not create separate time dimensions; they undergo different amounts of internal change across the same number of substrate ticks. `[AXIOM — P2]`

The “server and clients” analogy is useful if read carefully. The server clock is the global update index. A client clock is a maintained periodic process that counts or phases those updates. Clients may run at different effective rates because motion, interactions, or local latency change the number of internal recurrence events completed per global tick. They need not display the same reading even though they share the same update order.

The clean ontological statement is:

```math

The native Scale-0 closure was shown unable to force the required strictly convex `L^2` form; the law is folded into FC-2 as an axiom-class metric commitment. `[AXIOM-CLASS — FTD-1000; prior derivation route CLOSED NEGATIVE]`

### 3.3 Why invariant light speed does not prove a global clock

The same measured vacuum light speed in inertial laboratories does not logically prove a preferred global time. In standard relativity it is compatible with the absence of a preferred foliation. In FTD the global tick is known because P2 postulates it; empirical light-speed invariance becomes a recovery condition the substrate must satisfy.

### 3.4 The clock is not multiplication by a cube root of unity

The attractive notation `T=e^{2 pi i/3}` cannot, by multiplication, implement

```text

```

because multiplication maps `0` to `0`. A three-cycle of the three symbols can be represented by an abstract permutation operator `P` with `P^3=I`, but it is not ordinary complex multiplication on the numerical set `{-1,0,+1}`.

FTD's canonical phase clock is instead the order-four quadrature cycle

### 3.5 Arrow, irreversibility, and the fold

Determinism means the update is a function:

```math
X_{n+1}=F(X_n).
```

Reversibility additionally requires `F` to be one-to-one. FTD explicitly declines that requirement. The current engine has a full-state non-injectivity witness: distinct admissible states can map to the same later state. The “fold” is this many-to-one structure. Once two distinguishable precursors share one successor, the present no longer contains a unique inverse history. `[THEOREM on the registered engine domain — FTD-0395; FC-2 SYNTHESIS]`

1. order from the tick;
2. orientation from the non-injective fold, given FC-2;
3. duration from counted recurrence along a local world-tube.

## 4. Records, information, memory, and thermodynamic cost

### 4.1 Ontic history is not a stored replay tape

The complete present may determine the next state without containing a literal copy of every earlier state. A past event survives only through present consequences: altered matter, outgoing radiation, environmental correlations, inscriptions, or other stable records. If no present discriminator remains, the historical distinction has expired from the current ontic state even though the event had consequences while it existed. `[SYNTHESIS/CONJECTURE]`

This is compatible with deterministic dynamics. Forward determinacy does not imply recoverability. It also avoids treating “information” as a conserved fluid. Information is a distinction available relative to a physical discriminator and a decoding context.

### 4.2 Memory kernels

Eliminating environmental or field variables from a complete local description generally leaves an effective memory kernel for the retained subsystem. In FTD's relational-matter work, eliminating the field yields a matter-memory kernel even when the full matter-field state remains Markovian. Thus “memory” has at least three levels:

- **dynamical memory:** present environmental state mediates a past interaction;
- **record memory:** a stable physical structure can be decoded as evidence of a past distinction;
- **biological recollection:** an organism actively maintains, compresses, retrieves, and reinterprets records.

The first two are already physical notions. The third is not recovered by the current ontology and should not be projected backward as a primitive. Biology is a high-level record-maintenance process whose emergence remains open. `[OPEN — biological memory]`

### 4.3 Landauer without reifying deletion

The ontological intuition “heat comes from the work, not from annihilating an abstract substance called information” is largely compatible with a careful physical reading of Landauer's principle. A logical many-to-one reset is implemented by a real device coupled to a reservoir; the heat belongs to the physical protocol and its constrained state-space compression, not to an immaterial bit disappearing.

FTD's sharper conjecture is:

> **No heat is produced by abstract loss as such. Heat is the ledgered energy transfer required by the actual reset, stabilization, transport, and reservoir coupling that realize a fold.** `[CONJECTURE]`

This is not yet a refutation of Landauer's quantitative bound. To become one, FTD would need an explicit cyclic memory device, a complete reserve current, controlled bath assumptions, and a measured work/heat balance that violates the bound's own premises. The conditional clock-resource theorems show why a physical reserve ledger is necessary; they do not by themselves settle Landauer.

## 10. What would make the ontology physically compelling

A genuine ontology should do more than redescribe known equations. The strict tests are:

1. **State completeness.** One finite complete state must determine every allowed next transaction without an unrecorded replay tape or target-dependent selector.
2. **Native action.** Genesis, evaporation, current, field work, recoil, motion, and reserve flow must arise from one local update/action architecture or an explicitly proven compatible decomposition.
3. **Continuum recovery.** Maxwell, relativistic matter, quantum statistics, and gravity must be recovered by finite-domain epsilon bounds, with lattice corrections calculated rather than hidden.
4. **Matter spectrum.** Stable localized solutions must produce mass, spin, statistics, reactions, and particle poles without inserting the Standard Model catalog.
5. **Coupling readout.** `alpha` and other dimensionless couplings must arise as target-blind operational limits of the native dynamics, not substitutions into borrowed formulas.
6. **Bell/Born closure.** The preparation, context, selector, records, and long-run frequencies must reproduce experiment while satisfying the branch's declared measurement-independence and no-signalling commitments.
7. **Gravity closure.** The same native energy-momentum that moves matter must source the clock/geometry sector; light must respond correctly; the spin-2 or alternative tensor structure must be identified.
8. **Preferred-frame envelope.** The theory must predict where the global substrate frame becomes observable and survive existing bounds without post-hoc suppression.
9. **Thermodynamic implementation.** Reset, memory, heat, and reserve current must be modeled in a complete cyclic device before claims about information erasure are promoted.
10. **Novel prediction.** At least one pre-registered, non-parametric result must differ from standard physics and survive external test.

These are not “unaddressed gaps” in the sense of forgotten topics. The ledger has adjudicated them into theorems, adoptions, scoped measurements, no-go results, and bounded successor programs. They remain the places where the ontology must earn physical realization.
