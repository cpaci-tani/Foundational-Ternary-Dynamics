## 0 · Verdict (layered, all categories pre-registered)

The contradiction in canon dissolves once three different objects are separated. The arrow's direction is **not** a residual free choice of the substrate:

| object | direction status | basis |
|---|---|---|
| **substrate / causal arrow** (the update-map direction) | **forced-given-FC-2** | the current complete update `F` is non-injective by FTD-0395's evaporation witness; given FC-2's adoption of that irreversible sector, no single-valued inverse dynamics exists. No `ℤ/2` to choose. |
| **thermodynamic arrow** | **direction = the causal one** (forced-given-FC-2); **gradient = imposed** | entropy increases *along* the many-to-one (information-losing) direction; that a gradient *exists* needs a low-entropy past (a boundary condition). |
| **reversible-wave `±ω` orientation** | **free orientation, but arrow-less** | the flux/wave sector is time-reversible (`∂_t`, not `∂_t^{1/2}`); `±ω` is a genuine free `ℤ/2`, but it carries **no** arrow. |

**Headline:** "which way is forward" is **fixed by the non-invertibility of the adopted update sector, given FC-2** — not chosen. FTD-0322/0315 tagged the direction a "free orientation (`±ω`)"; that was a **mislabel** — `±ω` belongs to the *reversible wave* sector, which carries no arrow. FTD-0394's lossy readout `R` is not itself the complete dynamics; FTD-0395 supplies the separate full-map witness. The arrow lives in the adopted *irreversible* sector, whose direction has no free `ℤ/2`.

The honest grade is **forced-given-FC-2**, not forced-from-P1–P5-alone: FTD-0253 proves P1–P5 are reversibility-silent, so category "forced-structural" is empty. The forcing rides on FC-2's commitment that the irreversible sector is fundamental, with current-engine non-injectivity witnessed by FTD-0395.
