## 1. Purpose

This charter pauses the rapid construction sequence and fixes the question the
matter program is trying to answer:

> What is the smallest explicit FTD state and local law for which a material
> object exists, persists, moves, composes, forms, decays, and admits a physical
> infrared interpretation?

The target is an ontological foundation, not another favorable trajectory and
not a particle-name assignment. The charter organizes future work into gates
whose failures have declared consequences. It introduces no theorem, engine
result, primitive, production toggle, particle, or Standard Model claim.

## 2. Preliminary thesis

The live candidate is:

> **Matter is a localized, metastable, state-identifiable relational process
> in the complete manifestation--constituent--field state. A ternary site is an
> instantaneous manifestation of that process, not the enduring object.**

Write the selected research state as

\[
X_n=(s_n,C_n,F_n),\qquad
F_n=(E^{\rm face}_n,B^{\rm edge}_{n-1/2}),\qquad X_{n+1}=T(X_n).
\]

Here `s` is ternary manifestation/polarity, `C` is the currently selected
finite constituent phase-space record, and `F` is the matched face/edge field.
A candidate object is a localized invariant or metastable family in this
complete state, modulo integer translation, proper cubic transformations, and
polarity-preserving constituent relabelling. Its recurring transaction is

```text
relational configuration
    -> subcell motion and exact event current
    -> face/edge field transport
    -> reciprocal constituent impulse and work
    -> continued or transformed relational configuration.
```

This is a selected working definition. The five postulates do not presently
force `C`, the compact interaction, or the face/edge action.

## 4. Object criterion

A history may be called a **selected classical matter object** only over a
declared sector and horizon, and only when all of the following are measured
or proven:

1. **localized core:** constituent support and excess relational energy define
   a computable centre and bounded core;
2. **energetic integrity:** the core occupies a negative or otherwise derived
   stable energy sector with a stated margin;
3. **complete-state identity:** membership and observables are functions of
   the instantaneous complete state, not an unrecorded route label;
4. **reciprocity:** motion, current, field update, impulse, binding work, and
   energy are one atomic transaction;
5. **environmental decomposition:** bound dressing is separated from outgoing
   radiation/tail and dispersing background by operational observers;
6. **persistence:** the family survives a registered open neighborhood and a
   horizon preceding any boundary or environmental return;
7. **covariance:** polarity conjugation, integer translation, constituent
   relabelling, and proper cubic transformations preserve its class;
8. **composability:** two distant instances retain approximately separable
   state identities and vanishing interaction defect with separation;
9. **formation and decay:** local rules enter and leave the family with exact
   event, energy, and invariant ledgers;
10. **physical spectrum:** a positive-residue pole or controlled resonance and
    an empirically admissible infrared dispersion exist.

Criteria 1--7 can establish a selected classical matter pattern. Criteria
8--10 are required before physical particle language is adopted.

### Negative energy is a witness, not the ontology

For the selected compact pair, `E_core<0` relative to the separated-pair
threshold is a sharp formation classifier. It is not a universal definition
of matter. A different qualified action could possess a stable recurrent
family without that sign convention, and shifting an energy zero cannot
create an object. The invariant content is occupation of a dynamically
isolated or metastable family with a finite escape/decay cost and a closed
complete-energy ledger. Negative pair energy is the registered witness of that
content in the present two-body branch.

## 5. Identity is not a voxel and not hidden history

The occupied site set may change while the object persists. The identity
candidate is the relational organization of the complete state, not permanent
occupation of a substrate cell.

An event path can matter because equal endpoint densities can differ by a
divergence-free current cycle and thereby generate different transverse field
states (FTD-0719). That does not license an unrecorded history variable. The
common action must select the transaction from the committed state, or the
missing selector must be admitted explicitly as state. A successful reverse
map must reconstruct from the later state without replaying a saved forward
route.

Four objects must therefore be kept distinct:

1. the **manifestation snapshot** `s_n`, which is not state-complete;
2. the **complete instantaneous state** `X_n=(s_n,C_n,F_n)`;
3. the **transaction** `(X_n,I_n,X_{n+1})`, selected by the common action;
4. the **orbit** generated by repeated application of that action.

When `T` is single-valued and state-only invertible on a candidate family, one
complete state determines its transaction and its complete two-sided orbit.
The phrase “history-level identity” then means an orbit equivalence class, not
a separately stored history tape:

\[
\operatorname{Orb}_T(X)=\{T^kX:k\in\mathbb Z\},\qquad
\mathcal O(X)=[\operatorname{Orb}_T(X)]/\mathcal G,
\]

where `G` contains the declared translation, proper-cubic, and consistent
relabeling equivalences. If forward selection or reverse reconstruction is not
unique, the committed state is incomplete. The exact trilemma is then: derive
one action-selected current, store an oriented current/internal phase, or
carry mathematically equivalent connection/holonomy information.

### The constituent record is not yet declared fundamental

`C` currently has three live interpretations:

1. fundamental finite constituents;
2. a reversible coordinate chart for persistent structures already present
   in `(s,J)`;
3. resolution cells in an effective collective description.

Track A can establish the sufficiency of `C` without choosing among these.
Track B must distinguish them by reconstruction, refinement, and state-
completeness tests. Quantities that change merely because the same object is
represented with more constituent records cannot be called physical mass,
charge, size, or species data.

## 6. Boundary, dressing, wake, and decay

The candidate has no hard container boundary. Its operational boundary is the
region across which correlations and energy transport cease to remain locked
to the recurring core.

For a declared centre and causal buffer, the field must be decomposed as

\[
F=F_{\rm bound}+F_{\rm outgoing}+F_{\rm background}
\]

by measurements rather than labels:

- `bound` means translation-covariant or phase-locked near-field persistence;
- `outgoing` means causal shell arrival with net outward regional transport
  and no sustaining return over the declared horizon;
- `background` means ordinary source-free dispersion not state-identifiable
  with the object.

Solidity is collective persistence of such relations. Decay is exit from one
metastable family into other relational families plus outgoing field
excitation. Fundamental irreversibility does not follow from an observed
coarse-grained decay rate when the complete transaction remains invertible.

## 7. Dual research track

The program now separates two questions that earlier work sometimes mixed.

### Track A — sufficiency and existence

Does the explicit selected state `(s,C,F)` and common action support a robust,
uncontained, composable, moving matter pattern? A positive result establishes
that these ingredients are sufficient for a classical matter ontology. It
does not show that the ingredients follow from the five postulates.

### Track B — native reduction and necessity

Can `C`, the compact interaction, and the connection representation be
reconstructed from native `(s,J)` histories or shown to be mathematically
necessary? This track decides whether matter is emergent in the original
ontology, an honest selected effective ontology, or evidence for a new
primitive.

Track A must not be presented as Track B. Track B must not obstruct learning
whether Track A is internally coherent.

The experimental dependency spine is

```text
M1 existence -> M2 environmental closure -> M3 identity/stability
             -> M4 motion -> M5 composability -> M6 formation/decay
             -> M8 charge/connection -> M9 physical interpretation.
```

M7 native reduction begins as an analytic track after M0 and runs in parallel,
but it must converge with M1--M6 before the word **emergent** is used for the
matter ontology.

## 8. Gate-ordered roadmap

### Gate M0 — charter and vocabulary

Freeze this thesis, the object criteria, equivalence relations, allowed repair
policy, evidence hierarchy, and escalation rules. No new campaign runs before
the charter is indexed and internally reconciled.

**Evidence required:** canonical charter, ledger row, index links, and an
explicit evidence boundary. This requirement was satisfied before the later
FTD-0739 run.

### Gate M1 — finite-support causal existence

FTD-0739 is complete after a non-semantic runner/output audit. Starting from an
exact compact-support neutral Gauss preparation, a negative core forms while
energy crosses an outer shell before causal self-contact on every registered
unbound ray.

**Pass:** exact preparation/current/action/regional ledgers, durable core on
every registered ray, outgoing tail, bound control, polarity agreement, and
state-only inversion.  
**Failure:** preserve the locked negative result. It closes that preparation,
not all relational matter. A broader candidate is allowed only as a new
versioned hypothesis with an independently stated reason.

### Gate M2 — environmental closure

Repeat a successful M1 construction over increasing causal buffers and
horizons. Establish a volume-stable bound near field, causal shell-arrival law,
and an outgoing component that does not return during the claimed persistence
interval.

**Pass:** a localized core plus operationally separated dressing/tail exists.  
**Failure:** the candidate is a finite environmental reservoir history, not a
self-maintaining object.

### Gate M3 — invariant or metastable family

Replace pointwise favorable trajectories with a state-only family classifier.
Measure whether finite-time open neighborhoods retain nonzero margins as
volume and horizon increase.

The classifier contract and anti-circularity rules are fixed in
[`SCOPE_METASTABLE_MATTER_OBJECT_PREDICATE_v1.md`](SCOPE_METASTABLE_MATTER_OBJECT_PREDICATE_v1.md)
(FTD-0743). Its empirical scales remain deferred until M1/M2 identify the
finite-support core and bound-field component.

**Pass:** one complete-state metastable family with a quantified lifetime and
basin.  
**Failure:** if membership depends on unrecorded phase/history, price explicit
internal phase; if every neighborhood collapses with horizon, close the
current object candidate.

### Gate M4 — autonomous complete motion

Prepare motion by a causal acceleration/formation history rather than boosting
the core and leaving its dressing static. Permit recurrent internal gaits and
polarity-preserving constituent permutations.

**Pass:** the entire core--field identity translates under the same common
action with controlled radiation and no legacy force.  
**Failure:** repeated state-completeness failure prices an internal phase;
field-only failure motivates the connection gate, not a force multiplier.

### Gate M5 — two-object composability

Construct two independently identifiable distant objects. Require cluster
decomposition of energy/action, vanishing cross-defect with separation, and
continued individual identity before, during, and after weak interaction.

**Pass:** multi-object ontology and scattering work are licensed.  
**Failure:** the purported object is inseparable from one global environmental
state and cannot yet serve as matter.

### Gate M6 — formation, decay, and count change

Begin without a preassembled object. Use local event rules to enter and leave
the M3 family while accounting for every energy and polarity transfer.

**Pass:** formation and decay are dynamical transitions between identifiable
families.  
**Failure:** the selected object is a prepared solution only; native matter
emergence remains open.

### Gate M7 — native reduction

Attempt to derive or eliminate each selected price:

1. reconstruct constituent phase space from persistent native `(s,J)`
   histories or prove an injective equivalent is necessary;
2. derive the binding interaction from the same local field action, or label it
   an imposed effective law;
3. prove transaction selection from instantaneous state, or admit the missing
   phase/connection data;
4. derive field normalization without fitting a physical constant.

**Pass:** the candidate moves toward emergence from the original ontology.  
**Failure with a coherent Track-A object:** explicit constituent phase space
becomes an honest additional ontology rather than a hidden sidecar.

### Gate M8 — charge and electromagnetic ontology

Only after count-changing events exist, solve the exact transition graph for a
nontrivial additive invariant and derive its current. Primitive `+/-` remains
polarity until this closes.

If local phase comparison, Wilson/Aharonov--Bohm information, gauge-covariant
canonical momentum, or holonomy is required, adopt a link connection or
mathematically equivalent state explicitly. `E/B` field strengths alone do
not encode that information.

### Gate M9 — physical infrared interpretation

Only after M1--M8 establish a composable object, extract:

- poles or controlled resonances and positive spectral weight;
- dispersion, inertial mass, lifetime, and radiation rate;
- preferred-frame and cubic-anisotropy coefficients;
- spin/statistics candidates and scattering observables.

Exact microscopic Lorentz symmetry is not required. Uncontrolled marginal
preferred-frame effects are not acceptable. Particle names, mass formulas,
and Standard Model identifications remain outside the program until this gate.

## 9. Escalation ladder

The least-price ordering is:

```text
existing state and a new selected common action
    -> joint trajectory/current/field solution
    -> explicit constituent internal phase or injective equivalent
    -> noncompact link connection for local phase/holonomy
    -> compact/constrained connection or order parameter for protected sectors
    -> closure of the candidate if no robust localized family remains.
```

One failed numerical arm cannot force a new primitive. An enlargement is
licensed only by a repeated state-completeness, uniqueness, conservation, or
representation failure that survives a registered class of admissible
repairs.

The exact reconstruction/behavioral-congruence discriminator and every branch
transition are frozen in
[`SCOPE_MATTER_ONTOLOGY_BRANCH_DECISION_MATRIX_v1.md`](SCOPE_MATTER_ONTOLOGY_BRANCH_DECISION_MATRIX_v1.md)
(FTD-0744).

## 10. Research-repair policy

Constructive suggestions and changes are welcome. The ontology is not frozen
to a single voxel, a particular compact well, or the first attempted action.
The following discipline applies:

1. every change of state type, equation, interaction, timing, normalization,
   tolerance, or ontology is a fresh versioned candidate;
2. any candidate that will be measured is locked before its result is read;
3. a failed predecessor remains failed at its registered scope;
4. repairs must state which defect they address and what new price they add;
5. no post-hoc force amplification, tolerance relaxation, favorable-phase
   selection, or physical-constant targeting is admissible;
6. production defaults and the five postulates remain unchanged until a
   separate owner adoption decision.

## 12. Preliminary conclusion

The present work supports one precise research hypothesis: a material object
may be a localized recurrent relation whose manifestations, event current,
and field dressing continually reconstruct one another. It does not yet show
that such an object survives an uncontained environment, composes with another
object, forms from native rules, carries conserved charge, or has a particle
pole. Those are the roadmap rather than conclusions.
