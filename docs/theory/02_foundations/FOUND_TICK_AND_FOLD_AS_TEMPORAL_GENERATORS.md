## 0 · The thesis, in one line

> Time is not a primitive ingredient. It is manufactured by exactly two generators, each of which is — on its own — **not itself temporal**: a **tick** (a bare, reversal-invariant order) and a **fold** (a non-invertible, many-to-one update map). Nearly the entire temporal vocabulary — direction, change, duration, simultaneity, the present, tense — derives from these two. What survives every reduction is a single genuinely irreducible *temporal* primitive: not "flow," but the **designation** that a particular oriented order **is** time.

This document consolidates a maximally-precise lexical reduction of the time-vocabulary attempted this session, tying it to FTD's own established mechanisms so the result is checkable against canon rather than free-floating philosophy.

## 1 · The two generators `[established FTD, restated]`

**The tick — bare ORDER.** The substrate's tick gives a **reversal-invariant betweenness relation** `B(x,y,z)` on positions: McTaggart's C-series. `[THEOREM/SYNTHESIS, FTD-0253]` P1–P5 alone are **arrow-silent** — a deterministic update can be many-to-one, so determinism does not by itself supply a direction. As a mathematical object, bare order is **not temporal**: it is pure reversal-invariant relation-theory, indistinguishable in form from the ordering of integers or the pages of a book.

**The fold — DIRECTION.** The substrate's manifestation map `M: J → s` (flux to ternary state) is **many-to-one, hence lossy, hence irreversible** — its inverse is one-to-many (a relation, not a function), so "run `M` backward" is not a defined dynamics. `[established FTD, FTD-0324]` This non-invertibility is what *manufactures* the single traversal sense: because there is no inverse, "backward" names nothing, so there is no ℤ/2 to choose and exactly one direction is forced. The operator content is `∂_t^{1/2} = √∂_t`, a Volterra integral over the entire past, the `G*` ratio-branch of the Euler reflection. `[THEOREM, FTD-0323]` As a mathematical object, the fold is **also not temporal**: non-invertibility (`|preimage| > 1`) is pure function-theory.

**The keystone.** The fold is the load-bearing generator precisely *because* it carries no tense of its own — a temporal primitive could never manufacture direction without circularity (defining time with time), but a non-temporal one can. This is the square-root-as-act reading (FTD-0340) applied to the tick: the fold is a **squaring** (a 2-to-1 forgetting, the same shape as `v²`, `|ψ|²`, the Born rule), done for free; direction is what remains once that forgetting forecloses the reverse.

These are eliminated from the ontology, not left as unexplained residue.

## 3 · The one irreducible temporal primitive — "which order is time" `[SYNTHESIS]`

Order's *form* reduces fully to non-temporal betweenness. Direction reduces fully to the fold's non-invertibility. But one thing does **not** reduce: the **designation** that this particular oriented betweenness — this tick-order, carrying this fold's direction — **is** physical time, as opposed to being merely an abstract order (like the integers, or a queue).

This is exactly FC-2's `[AXIOM]`-class identification: "the mathematical face of the declaration, not a derivation" (`FOUND_ARROW_AS_SQUARE_ROOT.md` §1). And it is exactly the mechanism type-priority names (FTD-0339, `FOUND_TYPE_PRIORITY_PRINCIPLE.md`): **content cannot bootstrap context.** The tokens of the order (its positions, its steps) cannot supply the type-level fact that this order *is* time — that designation must be set, not derived, precisely as the Framework Commitments are precondition-types rather than theorems. Order's *interpretation*, not its *form*, is where the genuine, unpaid, irreducibly temporal debt sits.

## 4 · Persistence — the fold read on the identity axis `[CONJECTURE]`

The fold that manufactures direction may do double duty: the same many-to-one map that forecloses "backward" may also be what makes **"is the same X as"** hold across successive tick-stages — trans-tick re-identification. On this reading there are not three independent generators for order/direction/persistence but **two generators producing four phenomena**: order (tick), direction (fold), becoming (indexical now + tick realizer, §2 — not a fourth thing, on pain of McTaggart's regress), and persistence (fold, read on the identity axis).

This is explicitly the **weakest joint** in the whole reduction. It is not derived, only proposed as the natural reading given that the same non-invertibility already does the work for direction. It is recorded here as a flagged conjecture, not asserted as settled.

## 5 · Applications — pointers only, not new derivations `[established FTD, cross-linked]`

The same single fact — the fold's non-invertibility — reappears in three places already canonical elsewhere in the corpus. This section **points**, it does not re-derive:

- **Memory.** The lattice holds only the present slice; there is no stored past. A "memory of an event" is the fold's **retained** (kernel/image) half — a present-state correlation an earlier fold deposited and later folds did not erase. Records of the past exist but not of the future, because the fold is one-way (the record/fork asymmetry). This is a statement about *physical records*; it says nothing about felt recollection (§6).
- **The container / "beyond."** FTD's undefined-boundary ontology (`FOUND_AXIOM_ZERO.md`; `AUDIT_INFINITY_REFRAME.md`, FTD-0079) already states that the substrate is specified pointwise — always "more lattice" locally — with no completed totality and no edge. Read through §3's lens, "is there a container?" is exactly a **type-level question** (an ambient embedding is a type, not a token) that the modulus/argument frontier (FTD-0336) already identifies as an imported-or-declined argument-half structure, not derivable from the substrate's own tokens. No new claim is made here beyond this cross-link.
- **Expansion vs. fractalization** `[SPECULATION]`. The fold is a **local compression** (many-to-one, inward, forgetting); genesis is a **local creation** (new excitations, outward) — two opposite-signed local operations, neither a global claim. FTD-0059 (`[THEOREM]`) proves no native length is expressible from Axiom-Zero invariants, so there is no native metric and no native cosmic expansion; FTD-0331 predicts `Λ = 0` as a *dissolution*, not a derived value. Reading recursive folding as "fractalization within" is `[SPECULATION]`, and the engine's scale hierarchy (scales 0–11) is a modeling convenience, not a physical fractal claim. This paragraph asserts nothing beyond what FTD-0059/FTD-0331 already establish.
