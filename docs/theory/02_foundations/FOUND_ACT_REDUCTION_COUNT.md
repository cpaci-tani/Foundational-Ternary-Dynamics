## 2 · The decisive findings

### 2.1 The i-act is unique — and it is housed twice
Rows 3 and 6 are the **same** `√(−1)`. P3's state values *are* `{i², 0, |i²|}` (FTD-0128 `[SYNTHESIS]`, adopted), and FC-0 is the `ℤ[i]` reading. The constitution lists them in two registers (a postulate's content and a commitment), but they collapse to **one generative act**. This is exactly the redundancy FTD-0128 named: *"the framework's axiomatic footprint shrinks by one … because the three state values now follow from Axiom 0 (`i` exists)."* So **H is correct that there is a single generative act**, and it is the `i`-act.

### 2.2 α is the category-6 breaker — proven, with `i` already native
The cleanest refutation of *full* closure is **internal to the i-native calculus**. FTD-0244 §1 makes `J` = "multiplication by `i`" (`J² = −I`) an **explicit native generator** of the operator calculus `𝔠` over `V_complex ≅ ℤ[i]²`. Yet (§2–§4):
- all native invariants lie in `ℚ(G*)`;
- the master-quadratic splitting field `ℚ(G*)(√(G*(4G*−1)))` is **degree 2** over `ℚ(G*)` (`G*` transcendental, Chudnovsky);
- `Gal ≅ ℤ/2` swaps `x₊ ↔ x₋`, and every native invariant is **blind** to the swap.

So **even with the i-act native, α's `δ` and the `k=1` selection are provably unreachable** — the selection `W` is "logically independent of the generating set." α belongs to category 6, not to {i-act, orientation, refusal}. This is `[THEOREM]`-grade and is the load-bearing reason the verdict is PARTIAL, not FULL. (It is also why MC-T4.3 is a *foundational* obstruction: the obstruction is a square root the i-native substrate cannot take.)

### 2.3 FC-3 is the category-5 breaker
Scale-ratio-covariance (FTD-0304) declares only dimensionless internal ratios physical — a covariance principle, structurally distinct from the i-act, an orientation, or a refusal. It is a genuine, separate `[AXIOM]`-class commitment that **H** does not absorb.
