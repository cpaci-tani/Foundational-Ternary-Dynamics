## The shortest defensible ontology

Matter is not a voxel and is not a collection of permanently labeled little
objects.  The present evidence supports a narrower statement:

> A matter candidate is a localized, recurrent equivalence class of signed
> manifestation events, oriented currents between those events, and the field
> state with which those currents exchange energy and momentum.

The word *equivalence class* matters.  Integer translation and relabeling of
indistinguishable same-polarity constituents do not change the candidate.
Different causal correspondences between the same snapshots can change the
current and field, however, so histories are not equivalent merely because
their endpoint pictures look the same.

**FTD-0740 state/history reconciliation:** “history” here means the orbit and
transaction structure generated by the complete state, not an automatically
new history primitive. A ternary or density snapshot is insufficient. If the
common action is single-valued and state-only invertible on the relevant
family, one complete instantaneous manifestation--constituent--field state
determines the current and the two-sided orbit. If it does not, the state must
be enlarged by an oriented current/internal phase or mathematically equivalent
connection information. This is exactly the FTD-0719 trilemma, not a choice to
store an unbounded replay tape.

FTD-0736--0738 show why this history language is necessary. The same pair can
be graph-connected yet still have positive internal energy. Only after a
finite current--field exchange does it enter the negative sector. Matter-like
formation is therefore a history-level energetic event, not a property that a
snapshot graph label can carry by itself.

FTD-0739 strengthens the locality part of this candidate: compactly supported

## Three levels

### 1. Manifestation events

At tick `t`, ternary site values identify actual signed manifestations.  The
quadratic coat derived from subcell position supplies a coupling density
`rho_t`; it does not fractionalize the primitive ternary state.

An instantaneous snapshot says *where polarity is manifested now*.  It does
not say how manifestations at adjacent ticks are connected.

### 2. Oriented transactions

The face current `I_t` is an oriented relation between successive events:

\[
\rho_{t+1}-\rho_t+D I_t=0.
\]

FTD-0719 proves that `rho_t` and `rho_{t+1}` determine only `D I_t`.  The cycle
part of `I_t` can change while both snapshots remain identical.  That cycle
part carries curl and changes the field.

Thus persistence resides in an event network or world-tube, not in a sequence
of still images.  A site hop is one boundary event of that network, not the
translation of an indivisible object.

### 3. Joint matter–field recurrence

Currents source face/edge fields; those fields must generate the constituent
impulses that produced the currents.  A qualified matter state must therefore
be recurrent under one atomic common-action map:

\[
(\rho_t,p_t,E_t,B_{t-1/2})
\longmapsto
(I_t,\rho_{t+1},p_{t+1},E_{t+1},B_{t+1/2}).
\]

The transaction must solve motion, current, field update, recoil, work, and
inversion together.  FTD-0718 shows why these cannot generally be assembled
afterward from independently selected solutions.

## Binding, inertia, radiation, and decay

These are interpretations to test, not derived results.

- **Binding `[CONJECTURE]`:** a self-consistent closed circulation of field

- **Inertia `[CONJECTURE]`:** changing velocity may require rephasing the

material strands.

## Minimal completeness trilemma

A deterministic Markov formulation must choose one route:

1. **Derived transaction:** the common action uniquely solves the next current
   and state from the present complete matter–field state.
2. **History state:** the oriented current/correspondence is retained as an
   explicit state variable.
3. **Connection state:** a link phase, holonomy, or equivalent variable stores
   the cycle information and determines the current.

Route 1 introduces no new primitive and is constructive for free distinct
endpoints (FTD-0503) and numerically supported in one interacting basin
(FTD-0720). Routes 2 and 3 become necessary only if the implicit transaction
is non-unique or cannot be inverted in a required sector.

## Open foundation gaps

The ontology is not complete until the following are derived or closed:

1. global existence and uniqueness of the implicit common-action transaction,
   especially at collisions and topology changes;
2. a stable localized orbit selected by that transaction;
3. the admissible current-cycle or connection rule;
4. causal formation and reaction rules for the recurrent complex;
5. a positive energy and spectral measure;
6. infrared particle poles, mass, spin, statistics, and common-cone behavior;
7. operational identity under interaction with clocks, rods, and detectors.

No formula for a measured constant can substitute for these gates.
