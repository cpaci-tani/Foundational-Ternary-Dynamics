## 2. Why the object must be relational

Four established facts jointly exclude the single-voxel picture inside the
selected branch:

1. Exact motion changes site anchors while preserving a composite's internal
   organization. A fixed site therefore cannot be the bearer of identity.
2. Stable rest and motion require several signed constituents and their
   binding relations; internal orientation and strain change later motion.
3. The same worldline segment generates face current, field update, recoil,
   and work. The state-selected constraint relation is therefore part of the
   object's constitutive dynamics, although the complete residual field need
   not be part of its identity.
4. Eliminating the field produces the exact matter-memory kernel
   `K_j=B D^j C`. A matter-only instantaneous state is therefore generally not
   Markov-complete even when the full state is reversible. This need for
   environmental state to predict evolution does not make the environment the
   object.

Identity must consequently be carried by the preserved relational-core history
under complete-state evolution, not by permanent occupation of particular
substrate sites or by annexing the complete environment to the object.
