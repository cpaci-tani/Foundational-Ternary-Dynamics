
## Four distinct stages

FTD-0736--0738 force a separation that earlier “capture at re-entry” language
blurred:

1. **encounter:** localized manifestations approach;
2. **relational entry:** their separation activates the selected compact
   interaction;
3. **field-mediated relaxation:** event current transfers energy between the
   constituent core and the face/edge field;
4. **energetic formation:** the connected core reaches negative internal
   energy and remains there over the tested horizon.

For the selected potential, stage 2 generically precedes stage 4 by theorem:
the potential and force vanish at the graph cutoff while a moving pair still
has positive kinetic energy. In the certified three-ray instance, stage 4
begins 15 ticks after the third graph entry. That number is measured, not
derived.

The exact complete-energy ledger gives a sharper interpretation. If `t_e` is
the graph-entry time, then for every later reaction-free selected-action state

```text
E_pair(t) = E_pair(t_e) - [E_field(t)-E_field(t_e)].
```

Energetic binding is therefore a first-passage event: the field's cumulative
gain must exceed the pair's positive entry energy (plus the stated negative-
margin threshold). The measured 15 ticks should be attacked as an integrated
work/relaxation time, not promoted to a fundamental integer. A parameter
campaign can now predict delay from the independently accumulated field-work
curve before reading the energy-sign crossing.

FTD-0739 adds a fifth, spatially testable stage:

5. **causal separation:** after formation, source-free field energy crosses an
   outer shell while the current support remains local and the core stays

This stage passes for the registered face, edge, and both body-diagonal
