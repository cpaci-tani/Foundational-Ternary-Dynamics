## 0 · The hypothesis under test

> **H.** FTD's entire irreducible-choice content reduces to a single primitive *act of intent* — **"`i` exists" = taking `√(−1)`** — with every other `[AXIOM]`-class element being (a) that act re-appearing, (b) an **orientation** (a field-trivial sign `ℤ/2`), or (c) a **refusal** (declining an import).

## 1 · The act-taxonomy (locked categories)

Every inventoried element is assigned **exactly one**:

1. **generative-structural** — defines the dynamics; introduces no `ℤ/2`/√/branch (e.g. "a lattice," "a tick," "a function").
2. **the i-act** — taking `√(−1) = i` (the `ℤ[i]` reading; the `ℤ/2` of `i` vs `−i`).
3. **orientation** — a sign `ℤ/2` on a real quantity; field-trivial (adds no irrational; e.g. an arrow direction, a matter/antimatter label).
4. **refusal** — declining to import a structure (adds nothing; e.g. declining the measurement map `M`).
5. **covariance** — a dimensionless-invariance declaration (e.g. only internal ratios physical).
6. **independent algebraic selection** — adjoins an irrational of **degree > 1 over `ℚ(G*)` not contained in `ℚ(G*)(i)`**, OR selects among admissible operators a value the native calculus does not force.
7. **calibration** — a unit/gauge anchor; the separate `[DECLARED]` register (theorem-non-derivable per FTD-0059).

**H is the claim that categories 5, 6, and 7 are empty (or vacuously reducible).**

## 3 · Admissible verdicts (mechanical criteria — one must be chosen)

- **PARTIAL** — the i-act is the **unique** category-2 element, the algebraic spine chain reduces to it (per the blind chain), **AND** at least one element ∈ {covariance (5), independent-algebraic-selection (6)}. (H holds only as the dominant through-line, not as a full collapse.)

## 4 · Mechanical adjudication of category 6 (the decisive one)

A constant/selection is **independent algebraic selection** iff, by **reuse of FTD-0244 §2–§4**:
- its minimal polynomial over `ℚ(G*)` has degree > 1, and
- it is not in `ℚ(G*)(i)` (note: `i = J` is a **native generator** of the calculus `𝔠`, per FTD-0244 §1 — so "i-native" is the calculus, and category 6 is what the calculus provably cannot reach).

FTD-0244 establishes this for α's `δ = √(G*(4G*−1))` (splitting field degree 2 over `ℚ(G*)`; `Gal ≅ ℤ/2`; invariants blind to the root swap). **This document does not re-derive that; it cites it.**
