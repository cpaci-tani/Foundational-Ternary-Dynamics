ontology consistent with the constructive common-action results is a
self-maintaining, spatially localized pattern in the joint configuration of
ternary manifestation, constituent subcell positions and momenta, and the

the permanent identity of a particular occupied site. Motion is therefore
successive local reconstitution of the pattern with a matched current and
recoil transaction—not a solid voxel sliding through an empty container.

This is a selected research ontology supported by constructive examples. FTD
does not yet derive a physical electron, atom, or generic matter spectrum from

## 3. Matter-language dictionary

### Identity

Identity is an equivalence class of configurations connected by time
evolution, constituent permutation, and integer translation while preserving
the object's internal relational pattern. A useful identity criterion must be
computable from state alone; it cannot depend on an unrecorded trajectory.

### Motion

Motion is a reversible local transaction that simultaneously changes
constituent position and momentum, deposits the corresponding face current,
updates the field, and balances work. FTD-0600/0601 construct this transaction
for selected composite matter. The resulting motion is evidence that a
single-voxel surrogate was the wrong carrier, not evidence that the selected
trimer is a physical particle.

### Polarity and charge

`+` and `-` are primitive polarity labels. A conserved additive charge and
electromagnetic `U(1)` do not follow from the labels alone. In a reaction-free
sector, transported signed polarity supports an exact continuity ledger. A
full theory still must show which reaction rules preserve an emergent charge,
why that charge is universal, and how local gauge redundancy or an equivalent
observable structure arises.

### Mass and inertia

Mass cannot be equated with voxel count by definition. Ontologically, it is
expected to be the energy/action cost of accelerating and deforming a stable
dressed mode. This collective-coordinate reduction is open. Until that
calculation closes, constituent rest energy and selected binding coefficients
are model inputs, not an emergent mass theorem.

### Solidity, stability, and decay

Solidity is a regime of pattern stability, not an absolute ontic substance.
A stable object must occupy a local dynamical basin or recurrent orbit whose
internal restoration survives environmental perturbations. Decay is the loss
of that self-maintaining organization into other allowed patterns and outgoing
field excitation. This interpretation is consistent with the user's flame,
membrane, pressure, and wake intuitions, but the engine has not yet derived the
relevant attractors or decay rates.
