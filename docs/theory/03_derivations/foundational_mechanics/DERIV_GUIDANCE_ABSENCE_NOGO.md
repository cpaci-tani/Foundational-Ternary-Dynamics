The de Broglie–Bohm reading then requires the **guidance equation**: the particle

## 1 · The complete engine force law (enumeration)

`phase_forces.cpp` computes, per manifested voxel, the velocity update from exactly
four channels (all written to `force_diag_`, summed at lines 192–193):

| Term | Expression | Generating object |
|---|---|---|
| `f_em` (mode: emergent_forces) | `G_C · s · ∇|J|` (tier-2 stencil) | flux **magnitude** |
| `f_em` (mode: poisson_coulomb) | `−α · s · ∇φ`, `∇²φ = div J − …` | Coulomb **potential** (sourced by div J / charge) |
| `f_em` (mode: default) | `−α · s · ∇(div J)` | flux **divergence** |
| `f_grav` | `G_N · ∇ρ` (tier-2) | energy **density** |
| `f_lorentz` | `α · s · (v × ∇×J)` | flux **curl**, ⊥ v |
| `f_color` | `α_s(r) · cf(c_i,c_j) · r̂` | pairwise **position** |

Cluster transport (`cluster_inertia`, FTD-0250) sums exactly these diagnostics
(`F_cluster = f_coulomb + f_gravity + f_strong + f_magnetic`, line 319). The movement
phase adds no further coupling — it integrates the velocity these terms produce.

**By inspection:** every term is a functional of `{|J|, div J, φ[sources], ρ, ∇×J,
positions, s, v}`. **No term is a functional of the envelope phase `S`**, and no term
carries the Bohmian structure `∇S` with a magnitude-independent coefficient. In
particular, on a configuration of *uniform* flux magnitude the magnitude- and
density-gradient channels vanish identically (`∇|J| = 0`, `∇ρ = 0`), while the
guidance velocity `(c²/ω₀)·∇S` is nonzero — the two laws are structurally disjoint.

## 2 · The dynamic null (closes the divergence/curl loophole)

Inspection alone leaves a loophole: a pure-phase wave `J = A(cos kx, sin kx, 0)` has
`|J| = A` uniform but **nonzero** `div J = −Ak·sin kx` and `∇×J`, so the
divergence-sourced and Lorentz channels are not pointwise zero — could their net
effect *mimic* guidance? FTD-0271 Phase E measured exactly this
(`campaign_de_broglie_guidance.cpp`): a manifested cluster at rest in the pure-phase
pilot wave (re-imposed each tick; `forces + poisson_coulomb + gauss_projection +
lorentz_force + coupling + de_broglie_clock` ALL enabled; movement off so the
acquired velocity integrates), swept over k, against a live-force control:

- guidance prediction: `v_pred = (c²/ω₀)·k` (grows with k);
- measured: `|v_meas| ≈ 10⁻¹⁹` (machine noise) at every k, no k-correlation;
- control (offset Coulomb pair, same readout): `v = 1.4×10⁻³` — the force path is live.

**Verdict GUIDANCE-ABSENT `[MEASURED]`:** the only channels that are even nonzero on
a pure-phase wave produce no cluster response; the null is real, not a dead engine.

## 3 · The no-go statement

> **No-go (engine level).** The FTD force law contains no phase-gradient coupling:
> every velocity-update term is generated by flux magnitude, divergence, potential,
> density, curl, or pairwise position — never by the envelope phase `S` — and the
> measured cluster response to a pure phase gradient is null at machine precision
> while the force path is demonstrably live. **Matter in FTD moves by
> magnitude-derived forces; the wave does not guide the particle.** A de Broglie–Bohm
> reading of FTD therefore requires ADDING the guidance equation `v = (c²/ω₀)·∇S` as
> a **sixth-postulate-class input** — it is not emergent from, and is structurally
> disjoint from, the existing five postulates' dynamics.

Scope: "engine level" means the theorem quantifies over the implemented force law
(the §1 enumeration), not over all conceivable FTD-compatible dynamics. It is robust
to recombination of existing terms (none carries `∇S`); it would be invalidated only
by adding a new phase-coupled term — which is precisely the sixth-postulate-class
move the statement identifies.

## 4 · Why this is the load-bearing result of the pilot-wave arc
