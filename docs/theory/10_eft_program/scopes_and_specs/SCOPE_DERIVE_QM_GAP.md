QM's non-commutativity is **Bell-violating** (Tsirelson `S=2√2`). **Bell's theorem:** local + deterministic + **measurement independence** ⟹ no Bell violation. FTD is local (Postulate 4) + deterministic (Postulate 5). Therefore **FTD cannot derive Bell-violating QM** unless it adds one of:

| Door | Cost | FTD status |
|---|---|---|
| **Superdeterminism** (drop measurement independence) | a 6th postulate; denies "free choice" of settings | natural for a deterministic CA ('t Hooft route); but an *import*, not a derivation |
| **Non-locality** (drop Postulate 4) | breaks a core axiom | not available without rewriting FTD |
| **Retrocausality** | exotic; a 6th postulate | not in FTD |

**None of these is a derivation from the 5 postulates.** This is the honest boundary: the Bell-violating layer needs an added postulate.
