## 3 · Mutual wells (why big and small bodies both “pull”)

If gravity is not a force carrier, “pull” is the wrong verb. Manifestation sources a **shared clock well**. Every manifested voxel sources \(\rho = K_B\cdot n\) (`SPEC_FTD_LAGRANGIAN.md` §4.2). There is no mass threshold: a one-voxel cluster and a planet are the same type of source, different \(N\) (FTD-0110). The discrete Green’s function is symmetric, so A’s well at B equals B’s well at A. Both worldlines respond to the joint \(\mathcal{L}\).

The small body *moves more in the lab* because the well is dominated by the large \(N\) and because inertial response scales with \(N\). That is mutual geometry plus inertia, not a one-way graviton wind from heavy to light. Equivalence-principle universality of free fall remains `[OPEN]` (`SPEC_OPEN_MATH_BY_SECTOR.md` §12-EP).
