## 2. What is actually forced by the existing results

### 2.1 A new reciprocal law is forced; a new variable is not

FTD-0582 proves that frozen reaction-free production contains

```text
(s, velocity) -> (J,W)
```

but no field-to-matter momentum write when selected forces are disabled. A
reciprocal mover therefore requires a **new selected atomic transaction**. The
same theorem does not show that the transaction needs a new persistent
coordinate.

FTD-0584 explicitly leaves an active nonlinear same-variable core open. Thus
no current theorem supports the unconditional sentence “mobile matter requires
a new ontic variable.”

### 2.2 Frozen raw-chart reversibility conditionally forces unbounded history

FTD-0498 proves that effective position and exact face current factor through

\[
X=\operatorname{site}+\operatorname{remainder},
\]

while frozen source and collision rules do not. FTD-0499 proves that a fixed
finite hidden chart label cannot make the unchanged many-to-one raw transition
reversible under repeated merges.

The consequence is conditional:

> If the frozen raw projection is retained and exact state-only reversal is
> demanded, unbounded branch history or an exported environmental record is
> necessary.

Changing the transition to an injective atomic map avoids that lower bound.
History is therefore rejected as the primary mobile-matter repair.

### 2.3 A complete multibody ontology conditionally forces constituents

The aggregate polarity coat and face current lose constituent information.
Distinct configurations can have the same aggregate density/current data and
later differ in collision or binding behavior. Therefore a generic multibody
theory must carry either constituent phase-space records or an injective field
representation with equivalent information.

This necessity does not apply to the first isolated one-carrier experiment.

### 2.4 Local microscopic `U(1)` conditionally forces connection information

Matched `E/B` variables can support a selected classical energy-preserving
field-strength update. They do not determine flat connection/holonomy sectors,
charged parallel transport, Wilson phases, or gauge-covariant canonical
momentum.

Therefore:

> If “electromagnetic” includes local phase comparison, microscopic gauge
> redundancy, Wilson/Aharonov--Bohm data, or compact flux sectors, a persistent
> link connection or a mathematically equivalent holonomy type is necessary.

This is the existing `IMP-S4` carrier choice, not a new fifth selected-type
row. A noncompact real connection is enough for the smallest classical local
gauge model. Compactness is an additional price if integer flux sectors or
bundle topology are claimed.

### 2.5 Topological matter requires more than ordinary real fields

FTD-0583/0584 prove that the current ordinary-real field fibres are affine and
contractible after fixed source/Gauss/harmonic data. A localized protected
sector therefore requires a compact/constrained/singular target or a genuinely
active nonlinear solution. Compactness alone does not stabilize a particle,
set charge magnitude, or guarantee mobility.

## 3. Gate R0 — smallest no-new-variable experiment

The round distinguishes “no new persistent type” from “no new dynamics.” Gate
R0 changes the update law and is therefore a `[SELECTED DYNAMICS]` experiment.

### 3.1 Persistent state

Use only information already present in the isolated sector:

```text
matter: manifested site s, velocity/remainder (mechanical p is derived)
field:  J and W
```

Mechanical momentum may replace velocity as a coordinate because the frozen
causal dispersion gives a bijection between them. It must not be described as
already derived ontology outside that restricted sector.

Within-tick chords, temporal occupations, and oriented currents are solver
unknowns and are discarded after commit. They are not history ontology.

### 3.2 Atomic transaction requirement

One transaction must solve, rather than phase-compose:

1. matter momentum before/after;
2. one locally selected Moore displacement or stationary endpoint;
3. the complete carrier move as an injective local event;
4. the source current and native field update;
5. field recoil and matter impulse from one interaction;
6. the exact declared energy and momentum ledgers;
7. the state-only inverse.

The current `phase_forces` followed by `phase_movement` ordering is excluded.
The frozen remainder reset/carry is excluded. No legacy force, prescribed
trajectory, projector, external reset, or post-hoc self-force subtraction may
enter.

### 3.3 R0 acceptance gates

R0 fails closed unless all of the following hold under a fresh hash lock:

- stationary plus all 26 signed Moore directions solve uniquely;
- current, locality, cubic covariance, and the declared energy and
  field-plus-matter momentum identities close below the locked tolerance;
- reversing from the final state alone recovers the complete initial state;
- dynamic-state size is constant with tick count;
- repeated traversal returns any active field energy after every complete
  hop without an imposed clock or reset;
- a localized core and dressing survive increasing duration and volume;
- the Peierls/depinning curve is measured before tuning and retained as a
  prediction if nonzero;
- no electromagnetic, Coulomb, photon, Lorentz, or particle claim is made from
  a successful hop alone.

R0 is the smallest decisive experiment because it can prove that a new
persistent type is unnecessary for scoped site mobility. Failure does not by
itself prove which larger ontology is uniquely necessary.

\[

experiment**, not a gapless mobile law.

Required new tests are state-only reversal, root uniqueness, long traversal,
momentum balance, localization, and the preregistered Peierls threshold. A

\[

\[

R2 is a `[SELECTED ONTOLOGY CANDIDATE]`, not an adoption in this document.

## 8. Round-table reconciliation

The three roles initially disagreed because they optimized different targets:

- the engine-realization position proposed `(X,p,E,B)` as the smallest
  constructed one-step candidate;
- the hostile-physics position required constituent phase space plus a
  connection before accepting electromagnetic charged matter;
- the constructive-physics position objected that no theorem yet forces a new
  persistent variable for one site-ontic reciprocal hop.

After cross-review and the FTD-0600 execution, the common denominator is:

1. the registered one-voxel R0 map is closed negative at energy;
2. a single manifested voxel is a carrier surrogate, not the asserted
   ontology of physical matter;
3. generic matter needs constituent-complete phase space or an injective
   equivalent;
4. FTD-0600 constructs one selected three-constituent common-action state with
   repeated bound site-crossing motion and state-only inversion;
5. that construction does not close isolated matched pseudomomentum and does
   not derive its constituent number, charges, or binding law;
6. microscopic local `U(1)` needs a connection/holonomy type or its exact
   mathematical equivalent unless the field-strength branch first supplies
   the missing closed-system momentum channel;
7. no current candidate supplies a gapless freely translating localized pole;
8. persistent history is not part of the minimum for the registered FTD-0600
   reaction-free transaction.

FTD-0599 resolves the R0 question for its registered map: the one-event root
is unique, but native energy fails. FTD-0600 resolves the first R2 existence
question constructively: explicit constituents plus a selected binding law
close energy, inversion, and repeated mobility. Whether an `E/B`-only closed
system can close total momentum without connection information remains open.
