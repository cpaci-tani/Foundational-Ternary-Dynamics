# PRE-REGISTRATION — the dual test: fix the relation, vary the Γ-constant (v1)

**Date:** 2026-09-04   **Status:** LOCK, uncommitted. Runner SHA printed at run time.
**Question:** The master quadratic in one-integer form is `u² = 16·c·(u−1)` with
`u = α⁻¹/c`, equivalently `16c²α(1−cα) = 1`. Given CODATA α alone, this *defines* a
constant `c*`. `G* = Γ(1/4)/Γ(3/4)` lies `0.62 ppm` from `c*`. **Among Γ-structured
constants generated by a declared rule, at what complexity does a hit this good become
expected?**

This is the dual of FTD-0319 / OT-3.3, which fixed `G*` and varied the polynomial. It is
the corrected form of the 2026-09-03 bridge test, which was invalid because its grid
was hand-built around the winner. Here the catalog is rule-generated, `G*` is one member
with no special status, and the target never sees `G*`.

## D0 — Binding declarations

- **D0.1** `c*` is computed from CODATA-2022 `α⁻¹ = 137.035999177` only, by solving
  `16c²α(1−cα) = 1` for the root near 3. No `G*` input.
- **D0.2** Tolerance `ε* := |G*/c* − 1|`, computed in-run. The statistic counts catalog
  values with `|c/c* − 1| ≤ ε*` — "at least as good as `G*`."
- **D0.3** Catalogs are generated by the rules in §1 and **deduplicated by value** at
  relative `1e-10` (Γ-identities produce many expressions for one number; counting
  expressions would inflate density). Values restricted to `[1, 10]`.
- **D0.4** No catalog is edited after a result. The ladder is fixed at C1–C5.
- **D0.5** The deliverable is a **threshold**, not a verdict: the complexity level at
  which the expected hit count crosses 1. Outcome labels below are for citation only.

## §1 — Catalog ladder (declared before generation)

`F(B)` := reduced fractions `a/b` with `1 ≤ a < b ≤ B`. `|F(8)| = 21`, `|F(12)| = 45`.

| id | rule | complexity |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | `Γ(p)/Γ(q)`, `p ≠ q ∈ F(8)` | single ratio, simple denominators |
| **C2** | `Γ(p)/Γ(q)`, `p ≠ q ∈ F(12)` | single ratio, finer denominators |
| **C3** | C1 × `π^{k/2}` × `m/l`, `k ∈ {−2..2}`, `m,l ∈ {1..4}` | single ratio, dressed by the factors Γ-identities produce |
| **C4** | `Γ(p)Γ(q)/(Γ(r)Γ(s))`, `p,q,r,s ∈ F(8)` | two-term ratio, bare |
| **C5** | C4 × `π^{k/2}` × `m/l` | two-term ratio, dressed |

`G* = Γ(1/4)/Γ(3/4)` is a member of C1 (and, by reflection, of every level) with no
distinguished status.

## §2 — Statistics, per catalog

- `N_k` — distinct values in `[1,10]`
- `hits_k` — values with `|c/c* − 1| ≤ ε*`; the best member's expression is reported
- `E_k` — expected hits under a smooth-density null: count in `c*(1 ± 10⁻²)` times `ε*/10⁻²`
- `p_k` — sliding-target null: fraction of `2×10⁵` log-uniform targets in `[1,10]` with a
  member within `ε*`
- `rank_k` — position of `G*` when members are sorted by `|c/c* − 1|`

## §3 — Secondary: the integer is also a choice

The relation carries one integer, `16`. To avoid hiding a factor-64 look-elsewhere,
repeat the primary with `n ∈ [1, 64]` replacing `16`: for each `n` solve
`n·c²α(1−cα) = 1` for `c*(n)`, count `(n, c)` pairs hitting at `ε*` in each catalog, and
compare with `64·E_k`. Report whether `(16, G*)` is the unique C1 hit across all `n`.

## §4 — Outcomes (citation labels for the threshold)

- **Structure-at-level-k**: `hits = 1` (G\* only) and `E_k < 0.1` for all levels `≤ k`.
- **Chance-at-level-k**: `E_k ≥ 1` — the Γ-space at that complexity is dense enough
  that a 0.62-ppm hit is expected.
- The finding is the pair (highest structure level, lowest chance level). If C1 is
  already chance, the coincidence carries no weight. If C1–C3 are structure and C4–C5
  chance, `G*` is special among *simple* Γ-ratios and unremarkable among compound ones;
  whether "simple Γ-ratio" is the natural family is then the declared judgment.

## §5 — What this cannot do

It cannot promote `x₊ = 1/α`. The relation is excluded as an identity by ~4×10³ times
the measurement precision (§0 of the response record); this test asks only whether the
near-miss is rare in its Γ-family. A structure result at C1 would license one sentence:
"among single Γ-ratios with denominators ≤ 8, the one α selects is `G*`, at a chance
rate of `p₁`." Nothing more.
