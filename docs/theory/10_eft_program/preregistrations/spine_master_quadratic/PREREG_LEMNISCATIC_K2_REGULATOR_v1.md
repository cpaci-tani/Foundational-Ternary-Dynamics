## §3 — Definitions & Formalisms

*   **Definition D1 (Lemniscatic Curve):** The elliptic curve $E\colon y^2 = x^3 - x$ defined over $\mathbb{Q}$, with conductor $N = 32$ and complex multiplication by $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ (LMFDB curve 32.a3).
*   **Definition D2 (L-series coefficients):** The Fourier coefficients $a_n$ of the weight-two modular newform $f(\tau) = \eta(4\tau)^2 \eta(8\tau)^2 = \sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n q^n$ in $S_2(\Gamma_0(32))$.
*   **Definition D3 (Accelerated L-value series):** The rapidly-convergent series for $L(E, 2)$ derived from the functional equation using the Dokchitser Mellin-transform split (with sign $w = -1$, i.e. terms add):
    $$L(E, 2) = \frac{\pi^2}{8} \sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n \left[ \left(\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{2\pi n}\right)^2 (x_n + 1) e^{-x_n} + E_1(x_n) \right]$$
    where $x_n = \frac{\pi n}{2\sqrt{2}}$ and $E_1(x) = \int_x ^ \infty t^{-1} e^{-t} dt$.
*   **Definition D4 (PSLQ Search Basis):** The 10-dimensional basis of transcendental periods:
    $$\mathcal{B} = \{1, G, \pi^2, G^*, G^{*2}, \pi, \pi G, \frac{G}{\pi}, \frac{G}{\pi^2}, \log 2, \pi \log 2\}$$

## §4 — Admissible Search Space

The PSLQ search is strictly confined to:

*   No introducing arbitrary coefficients or transcendental parameters outside the frozen basis.

## §5 — Three Pre-Blessed Outcomes

*   **Outcome A (FOUND):** A tight integer relation is found between $L(E, 2)$ and the basis elements with a residual deviation $< 10^{-50}$. The $K_2$-regulator is successfully closed-form derived.
*   **Outcome B (UNDERDETERMINED):** A relation is found but only at lower precision or with very large coefficients ($> 10^8$), suggesting a near-miss or degenerate basis.

## §6 — Falsifiers

*   **F-a (Precision failure):** The series does not converge to 60+ digits at $n=200$ terms, or has a different value from the direct-sum control.
*   **F-b (Identity miss):** The PSLQ search returns `None` for the basis, proving the absence of a simple modular period relation.
*   **F-c (High coefficient):** The integer coefficients are large and lack any structural or prime-factorization meaning.
