## Question

Does the field record generated by a small internal excitation separate into
an ordered outward component plus a persistent localized component, or does
the complete excitation remain broadly nonseparable over the largest causal
window yet tested?

This tests the excitation relative to the same dressed rest control.  A
nonzero near plateau would be excitation-associated dressing; the baseline
rest dressing cancels and is not being re-proved.

## Frozen observations

At every tick `0..112`, for each sign, record:

- canonical target energy and target ratio with the explicit contract
  `target_energy=initial_target*target_ratio`;
- the FTD-0677/0680 translation/boost-quotiented internal position, momentum,
  and phase metrics relative to the simultaneous control;
- the FTD-0683 component-aware radial difference-field bins, cumulative
  values at radii `R={8,16,24,32,40,48}`, total norm, mean/RMS radius, and
  `r50,r90,r99`;
- the FTD-0671 signed regional ledger at the same six radii, applied to the
  exact excited-minus-control field step;
- current-source support, complete-energy drift, common-action residual,
  sector/fibre signature, and observer residuals.

For each radius define cumulative outward and inward source-free transport

```text
O_R(t) = sum_{u=1..t} max(-T_R(u),0),
I_R(t) = sum_{u=1..t} max(+T_R(u),0),
```

normalized by the sign arm's tick-zero canonical target energy.  Define the
arrival tick `tau_R` as the first tick with `O_R>=0.001`; if absent, record
`tau_R=-1`.  No peak search or post-hoc threshold is permitted.

Fit `tau_R=a+R/v_shell` by ordinary least squares over all arrived radii among
`{16,24,32,40,48}`.  Record `v_shell`, `R^2`, and the complete arrival list;
do not compare `v_shell` to a continuum constant in this campaign.

On the fixed late window `88..112`, normalize the radius-eight cumulative
profile and internal phase metric by their tick-zero internal phase metric.
For each series record mean, coefficient of variation, linear slope, and
relative slope `abs(slope)/max(abs(mean),1e-300)`.

## Factorized outcome classes

Spatial class:

- `ORDERED_OUTWARD_PACKET` if every registered radius arrives, arrival ticks
  increase strictly with radius, the five-radius fit has `R^2>=0.98`, and
  `v_shell>0`;
- `LOCALIZED_EXCITATION_FIELD` if radius 48 never arrives and final `r90<=16`;
- `DISTRIBUTED_FIELD_MIXED` otherwise.

Late local class:

- `EXCITATION_BOUND_PLATEAU` if both late internal-phase and radius-eight
  means are at least `0.01`, both coefficients of variation are at most
  `0.10`, and both relative slopes are at most `0.001` per tick;
- `CONTROL_REST_RECOVERED` if both final normalized internal phase and final
  normalized radius-eight field are at most `0.01`;
- `CONTINUING_EXCITATION_TRANSFER` otherwise.

The registered verdict is the ordered pair of these two classes.  A possible
`ORDERED_OUTWARD_PACKET + EXCITATION_BOUND_PLATEAU` result is only a finite-
window dressing-plus-packet candidate.  `ORDERED_OUTWARD_PACKET +
CONTROL_REST_RECOVERED` is only a finite-window metastable-relaxation
candidate.  No class is called radiation, decay, lifetime, photon, particle,
or asymptotic basin.
