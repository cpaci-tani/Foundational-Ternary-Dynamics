## 1. Question

If the complete `J/W` history generated by the manifested polarity is
translated one face step with the polarity, does the FTD-0459 kinematic barrier
disappear, and is that event local enough to be an admissible lattice update?

## 2. Exact component protocol

Use the frozen `L=33`, 48-tick FTD-0459 initial state and evolve three parallel
histories with the reversible snapshot tick:

1. full packet + dressing + moving polarity;
2. source-free packet + dressing (`external`);
3. zero-field moving polarity (`source history`).

Require `full=external+source_history` in `J/W` at every tick. At the same 42
attempt ticks, construct:

- the FTD-0461 partial production source-site `J` carry;
- a rigid event that translates every source-history `J/W` value by `+x`,
  translates the polarity, and leaves the external history fixed.

No amplitude, phase, radius, speed, or tolerance is scanned.

## 3. Energy identities

With the FTD-0452 observer `H=H_wave-G_C s div J`, record:

- isolated source-history translation residual;
- external endpoint work `W_ext`;
- wave cross energy `X=H_wave(full)-H_wave(ext)-H_wave(source)` before and
  after translation;

- required partial-carry work from FTD-0461;

All full endpoint works must reproduce FTD-0459 to `1e-12`.

## 4. Locality estimator

Let `D` be the rigid event's total `J/W` difference from the pre-event full
history. Report the squared-norm fraction of `D` outside the 36-site union of
the radius-one Moore cubes around source and target. The rigid event is local
only if this fraction is `<=1e-12` at every attempt. This tests event support,
not the support of the pre-existing field itself.
