## 1. Question

FTD-0601 used an exact deterministic dipole-path field. Gauss fixes only the
longitudinal equivalence class; a routed representative can also contain a
divergence-free component and therefore an immediate magnetic/transverse
relaxation. The registered resting opposite-composite fixture moved outward
on its first step.

This protocol asks whether replacing only the initial field by the unique
zero-mean minimum-quadratic-energy representative at the same fractional
density restores the inward force sign.

## 2. Locked electrostatic initializer

For the unchanged aggregate quadratic-coat density `rho`, solve on the
periodic zero-mean scalar subspace

\[
(D D^T)\phi=\rho,
\qquad E_{\min}=D^T\phi.
\]

Use deterministic conjugate gradients initialized at zero, tolerance
`1e-13`, and at most `20 L` iterations. No warm start, path field, amplitude,
normalization, fit, or source smoothing is admitted. Require

```text
max|D E_min-rho| <= 1e-12,
max|C^T E_min| <= 1e-12,
mean(phi) convention independent,
B_half = 0.
```

As an independent minimum-energy control, add the fixed transverse challenge
`C A` generated by `make_transverse_challenge(L,1e-3)`. It must preserve Gauss
below `1e-12`, have positive norm, and strictly increase quadratic field
energy. This is a structural check, not a fitted perturbation.

## 3. Matter and transaction freeze

Reuse the exact FTD-0601 six-constituent state, charges, two independent
quartic trimer bindings, face currents, production dispersion, orbit gather,
18-component forward solve, and state-only inverse. There is no stationary
compensator or direct cross-trimer potential.

Use the FTD-0601 rest, co-moving, and counter-moving velocity pairs. For each,
run the base, integer-translated, cyclically rotated, and global
charge-conjugated/composite-exchanged copies: 12 forward and 12 reverse arms.

Every common-action, symmetry, and inverse gate remains `1e-12`/`1e-10` as in
FTD-0601. If these pass, run the resting base for 16 forward steps and 16
state-only reverse steps with the unchanged repeated gates.

## 4. Locked discriminators

For the initially resting base fixture retain the FTD-0601 definitions

\[
I_{\rm inward}=\tfrac12[(\Delta P_A)\cdot n
                         -(\Delta P_B)\cdot n],
\]

and `R_P=|Delta P_total|` including matched field pseudomomentum.

- electrostatic sign passes iff `I_inward>1e-10` and the center separation
  decreases on the first step;
- isolated momentum passes iff `R_P<=1e-12` on every arm and repeated step;
- later motion cannot retroactively repair a failed first-step static sign;
- no continuum `1/r^2` or particle claim follows from one separation.

## 5. Verdicts

- sign, common-action, repeated, and momentum gates pass:
  `MINIMUM_ENERGY_NEUTRAL_PAIR_CLOSED_DYNAMICS_CONSTRUCTIVE`;
- sign/common-action/repeated pass but momentum fails:
  `MINIMUM_ENERGY_ATTRACTION_RESTORED_MOMENTUM_CHANNEL_MISSING`;
- minimum-energy controls and common action pass but the sign remains outward:
  `MINIMUM_ENERGY_FIELD_DOES_NOT_REPAIR_FORCE_SIGN`;
- common action passes but repeated/inverse fails:
  `MINIMUM_ENERGY_NEUTRAL_PAIR_ONE_STEP_ONLY`;
- initializer or one-step physical identities fail:
  `MINIMUM_ENERGY_NEUTRAL_PAIR_CLOSED_NEGATIVE`;
- conditioning prevents classification:
  `MINIMUM_ENERGY_NEUTRAL_PAIR_UNRESOLVED`.

If the sign remains wrong, the next admissible branch is a locked self/cross
force decomposition of the common interaction. Connection/holonomy data are
not adopted merely to repair a sign without locating its origin.
