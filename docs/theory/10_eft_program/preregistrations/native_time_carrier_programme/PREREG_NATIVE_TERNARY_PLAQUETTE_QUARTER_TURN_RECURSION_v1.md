## 3. Frozen construction

Use the cardinal square in the `xy` plane,

\[
r_0=(1,0,0),\quad r_1=(0,1,0),\quad
r_2=(-1,0,0),\quad r_3=(0,-1,0),
\]

with the neutral ternary word

\[
s^{(0)}=(+1,0,-1,0).
\]

Let `S` be the forward cyclic shift and `S^{-1}` its reverse. Define

\[
s^{(k)}=S^k s^{(0)},\qquad
d_k=\sum_{j=0}^3 s_j^{(k)}r_j.
\]

The forward and reverse transition bivectors are

\[
L_k^+=d_k\times d_{k+1},\qquad
L_k^-=d_k\times d_{k-1}.
\]

The coordinate-free successor reconstructed from a nonzero transition is

\[
\mathcal R_L(d)=\frac{L\times d}{|d|^2}.
\]

No complex number, phase field, elapsed-time oracle, target outcome, or
coupling constant is inserted into these definitions.

## 4. Frozen exact claims

The certificate and compiled witness must establish all of the following.

1. Every `s^(k)` is ternary, neutral, and has exactly one `+1` and one `-1`.
2. `S^4=I`; on the two-dimensional alternating subspace generated by
   `a=(1,0,-1,0)` and `b=(0,1,0,-1)`, `S^2=-I`.
3. The induced matrix is the real complex structure
   `R=[[0,-1],[1,0]]`, while reverse transport induces `R^{-1}=-R`.
4. The four dipoles are nonzero, equal-norm, mutually quarter-turned, and
   close after four steps.
5. `L_k^+` is the same nonzero axial vector at every forward step;
   `L_k^-=-L_k^+` at every reverse step.
6. Reversing a transition changes the sign of `L`; `L` is therefore time odd.
7. For every step, `R_L(d_k)` reconstructs the next dipole exactly.
8. The radial and tangential channels are orthogonal and have equal norm, so
   their quadratic energy splits equally and recurs exactly.
9. Every signed cubic transformation obeys

   \[
   d\mapsto Qd,\qquad L\mapsto\det(Q)QL,
   \qquad \mathcal R_L(d)\mapsto Q\mathcal R_L(d).
   \]

10. `Sym^2(R)=Sym^2(R^{-1})`; the ordered bivector distinguishes the two
    transports while the symmetric square does not.
11. The forward and reverse cycles visit the same four instantaneous ternary
    words. No function of one instantaneous word can identify the direction;
    an ordered transition, retained bivector, or equivalent momentum is
    necessary.
12. A simple cardinal cycle in the cubic graph has even length, and four is
    the minimum nontrivial length. A two-site neutral pair supplies only an
    order-two exchange; a cardinal triangle does not exist.
13. In the ordinary real field lift, the homotopy `(d,L)->(t d,t^2 L)` reaches
    zero continuously with energy proportional to `t^2`. Hence recurrence is
    not topological protection in the current noncompact variables.
14. The ternary word can also leave the four-word orbit through the existing
    production event graph. Orbit membership is not a proven production
    invariant.
15. Exact protection would require a derived invariant region such as
    `|L|>=L_min>0`, or an explicitly selected compact/singular branch with its
    admissibility and work ledger. Neither is claimed here.
16. `G*`, `gamma`, absolute mass, Born weights, Bell settings, and measurement
    outcomes are absent from the construction.
