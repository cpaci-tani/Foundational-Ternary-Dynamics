## 1. Question

FTD-0929 derived the unique quasilocal companion

\[
 Q=-(2I-K)^{-1}U
\]

for the selected `C4` source, but did not supply a positive causal formation
mechanism. FTD-0931 then proved that the native `(J,W)` field pair forms a
fixed static halo by retarded dispersal, while leaving time-dependent tracking
and moving-source recoil open.

The present discriminator asks two deliberately separate questions:

1. Does the same native field pair form and locally track the fixed-center
   `C4` companion because the quarter-turn phase `z=i` lies in a spectral gap?
2. Does a primitive translating compact source have the same property, or
   does its Doppler phase intersect the native wave band and obstruct a
   generic finite-energy co-moving halo?

Success on the first question must not be promoted into success on the
second. Neither result supplies the source's formation energy, a reciprocal
translational recoil law, or production behavior.

## 4. Frozen fixed-center C4 source

\[
 \boxed{f_{n+2}=-f_n.}                                  \tag{6}
\]

This is the real two-arm form of temporal multiplication by `i`. It may be
generated by the target-blind FTD-0927 record/current recurrence; the field

profile registered below.

\[
 A=2I-K.                                                 \tag{7}

\[
 {2\over9}I\le A\le2I,
 \qquad

\[

\[

## 7. Frozen primitive-translation discriminator

Now freeze a different source:

\[
 f_n(x)=f_0(x-nu),
 \qquad u\in\mathbb Z^3\setminus\{0\},
 \qquad u_i\in\{-1,0,1\}.                              \tag{23}
\]

A co-moving profile `q_n(x)=q_0(x-nu)` would require

\[
 \boxed{
 D_u(k)\widehat q_0(k)=\widehat f_0(k),
 \qquad
 D_u(k)=\kappa(k)-4\sin^2(k\cdot u/2).}                \tag{24}
\]

The quadratic part at the origin is

\[
 D_u(k)={|k|^2\over3}-(k\cdot u)^2+O(|k|^4),           \tag{25}
\]

whose Hessian has signature `(+, +, -)` for every nonzero Moore step:

\[
 \operatorname{spec}\!\left({1\over3}I-uu^T\right)
 =\left\{{1\over3},{1\over3},{1\over3}-|u|^2\right\}. \tag{26}
\]

The certificate must verify the sign change along directions parallel and
perpendicular to each face, edge, and corner representative and the regular
cone furnished by equations (25)--(26). Therefore zeros of `D_u` form smooth
codimension-one sheets accumulating at `k=0`.

For a generic compact source whose Fourier numerator does not vanish on
those sheets—certainly for a compact source with nonzero total in a
sufficiently small neighborhood—`fhat/D_u` has a simple normal pole. It is
not locally `L2`, so there is no generic finite-energy exact co-moving halo.
The retarded solution may carry a wake, but no radiated-power law is
registered. Neutrality alone is not promoted into cancellation; exact
numerator divisibility by the resonance surface is a separate exceptional
condition.

This is scoped to a primitive one-site-per-tick translation. It does not
contradict FTD-0558's positive no-resonance floor for sufficiently slow smooth
drives, nor does it prove a no-go for every subvoxel, deforming, neutral, or
nonrigid source.
