## 3. Registered local seed class

Let `x` denote the local source coordinates with conjugate momenta `pi`, and
let `(Q,P)` be an already existing Cartesian clock pair. Let

\[
 U(x,Q)>0                                                     \tag{1}
\]

be the net local energy released by the membrane update after onsite and
other preregistered loads. `U` must be smooth on the admitted compliance
domain and computable inside the event's Moore cone.

For `sigma in {-1,+1}`, define the coordinate generating scalar

\[
 \mathcal S_\sigma(x,Q)
 =\sigma\int_0^Q\sqrt{2U(x,\xi)}\,d\xi.                    \tag{2}
\]

The candidate Cartesian seed is the momentum shear

\[
 x'=x,\qquad Q'=Q,
\]

\[
 \boxed{\pi'=\pi+\partial_x\mathcal S_\sigma,
 \qquad P'=P+\partial_Q\mathcal S_\sigma.}               \tag{3}
\]

On the zero-action seam

\[
 Q=0,\qquad P=0,                                           \tag{4}
\]

equation (3) reduces to

\[
 \pi'=\pi,qquad
 P'=\sigma\sqrt{2U(x,0)}.                                 \tag{5}
\]

If the clock frequency is `Omega>0`, equation (5) must give

\[
 H_{\rm clock}'=U,qquad
 I'={U\over\Omega},qquad
 \theta'=-\sigma{\pi\over2}\pmod{2\pi}                  \tag{6}
\]

under the registered convention
`Q=sqrt(2I/Omega) cos(theta)`,
`P=-sqrt(2 Omega I) sin(theta)`.

The inverse is generated by `-mathcal S_sigma=mathcal S_{-sigma}`. The
orientation record must therefore be retained until reversal.

## 4. Registered extended-body and growth tests

For a connected `N`-site body, its exact normalized uniform pair is

\[
 Q_N={1\over\sqrt N}\sum_{i=1}^Nq_i,qquad
 P_N={1\over\sqrt N}\sum_{i=1}^Np_i.                    \tag{7}
\]

A one-tick change of `P_N` by a local event changes every site momentum by
the same `1/sqrt(N)` share. The discriminator must test the resulting dense
projector against Moore locality.

For one adjacent prospective site `(q,p)`, define the exact join chart

\[
 \begin{pmatrix}Q_{N+1}\\R\end{pmatrix}
 ={1\over\sqrt{N+1}}
 \begin{pmatrix}\sqrt N&1\\1&-\sqrt N\end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}Q_N\\q\end{pmatrix},                    \tag{8}
\]

with the same orthogonal matrix acting on `(P_N,p)`. The joined body is in a
pure uniform mode exactly when

\[
 q={Q_N\over\sqrt N},\qquad p={P_N\over\sqrt N}.        \tag{9}
\]

Equation (9) is a phase-and-amplitude compatibility condition, not a license
to clone an arbitrary canonical pair.

   Jacobian dependence at every body site;

4. from a seed site `x_0`, any causal phase preparation requires at least

\[

### Z5 — canonical no-cloning and exact join chart

4. a phase-matched new site carries exactly `1/N` of the old uniform-mode

payment, or an explicit phase-complete machine. It cannot be free copying.
