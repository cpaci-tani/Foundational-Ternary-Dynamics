## 1. Question

FTD-0437 established nonzero momentum of the isolated two-particle subsystem.
That is not yet a violation of total momentum balance because the flux field
could recoil. FTD-0438 asks the narrower question:

> Does the periodic central-difference translation generator carried by the
> production flux variables compensate the particle momentum generated by the
> selected `G_C s grad|J|` force?

The campaign does not declare this candidate to be a unique continuum physical
momentum. A cubic lattice has only discrete translations. It tests the local
pseudomomentum candidate selected by the exact derivative used in the native
state-to-flux source.

## 2. Frozen operator

Let `J` be `voxel.flux`, let `W` be `voxel.wave_vel`, and let

$$
(D_iJ)(x)=\frac{J(x+\hat i)-J(x-\hat i)}{2}
$$

with periodic boundaries. The only field-momentum candidate tested is

$$
P_i^{\rm field}=-\sum_x W(x)\cdot(D_iJ)(x).
$$

The particle term is the existing production diagnostic

$$
P^{\rm particle}=\sum_a \gamma_a M_{\rm inertial}v_a,
$$

and the tested total is their unscaled sum. No normalization or fitted
coefficient may be introduced after execution.

For a source-free periodic tick `W'=W-KJ`, `J'=J+W'`, the central derivative is
skew-adjoint and commutes with the translation-invariant `K`. Therefore

$$
-\langle W',D_iJ'\rangle+\langle W,D_iJ\rangle
=\langle KJ,D_iJ\rangle-\langle W',D_iW'\rangle=0.
$$

The source-free arm tests this identity in the production implementation.

## 4. Locked estimators

At every tick boundary,

$$
P^{\rm total}=P^{\rm particle}+P^{\rm field}.
$$

The source-free control reports the maximum absolute and relative drift from
its initial nonzero field momentum. The pair arms report

$$
R_{\rm abs}=\max_t|P^{\rm total}(t)-P^{\rm total}(0)|,
$$

$$
R_{\rm rel}=\frac{R_{\rm abs}}
{\max_t|P^{\rm particle}|+\max_t|P^{\rm field}|}.
$$

An opposing field recoil is resolved when its final axial component has the
opposite sign and at least `0.10` times the magnitude of the particle component.
