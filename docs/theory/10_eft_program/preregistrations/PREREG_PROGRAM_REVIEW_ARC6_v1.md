> **Purpose.** "Consumption is a direction, not an achievability claim" has a shadow: a direction can be walked forever, with every arc on-track by its own metrics, because the audited floor is attackable and its openness regenerates work. This lock freezes, **before any arc runs**, the review that decides whether the walk continues — so CONTINUE must be *earned*, not defaulted into by sunk cost. A program without a scheduled falsification window is a priced line never fired at.

## §1 · Execution window and executor (LOCK-STD point 10)

The review executes at the close of **Arc 6** of the roadmap of record (the plan approved 2026-07-12), or **6 calendar months** from this lock, whichever comes first. Executor: the owner, with an adversarial AI panel whose composition mirrors the six-chair round table of 2026-07-12 (number theory / lattice / logic / methodology / ontology / strategy); the panel drafts, the owner rules. An unexecuted review past window auto-books an open F10 row and **blocks all further lock-cutting** until executed.

## §2 · Frozen inputs (no execution-time definitions; LOCK-STD point 7)

The review adjudicates on exactly these, as they stand at window close: (i) the census state and F10 ratio history (locks executed ÷ locks cut, per arc); (ii) the arc verdicts of Arcs 1–6 (meta-F10: each must exist); (iii) the [PARAMETRIC]-row movement count **with distinct gap-classes named** (the anti-gaming counter); (iv) the terminal states of the flagship attacks (δ-IND v2a/v2b, IMP-S4 battery, Damerell h≥2, D-IND, S2-a, β-coefficients, P5 confinement); (v) the external-review status (commissioned? report committed?); (vi) the P6C register state (candidates processed through the pipeline vs stalled at intake).

## §3 · The frozen outcome map (partition; LOCK-STD points 1–3)

Exactly one outcome fires; precedence order as listed; tie-breaks stated inline:

1. **RE-SCOPE** fires iff ≥2 of the four fronts have produced **zero** booked terminal states (RETIRED / NO-GO / SHARPENED / STANDS / IMPROPER / confirms-free-choice) across Arcs 2–6, OR the F10 ratio fell below 0.5 in ≥2 consecutive arcs. The re-scoped program continues only on fronts with ≥1 booked terminal state; the others are archived with their residue booked. *(Precedence: RE-SCOPE is checked first because it is the narrowest factual predicate.)*
2. **ARCHIVE-AS-MAPPED-BOUNDARY** fires iff RE-SCOPE does not fire AND every flagship attack in §2(iv) that executed returned a wall-confirming terminal (no retirement, no adoption-worthy candidate, no IMPROPER-reopening) AND the owner rules that the residual doors (as then named) do not justify a re-charter. This outcome is **a win under the goal's boundary clause**: the program ends by declaring the consumed-vs-priced map complete at its floor, and the corpus pivots to external exposure of that map. *(Tie-break vs CONTINUE: requires the owner's affirmative ruling; absent a ruling, CONTINUE-conditions are evaluated.)*
3. **CONTINUE (re-chartered)** fires otherwise — but never on momentum: the re-charter must name, per front kept, the specific doors that justify the next tranche of arcs, and re-freeze metrics + the next review window (this lock's successor, v2, cut before Arc 7). An unexamined CONTINUE is not an outcome of this map; failing to produce the re-charter document within the window books the review INCOMPLETE and blocks lock-cutting (meta-F10).

**Vacuity witnesses (LOCK-STD point 5):** each outcome can fire on admissible histories — RE-SCOPE on a two-dead-front history; ARCHIVE on an all-walls-confirmed history; CONTINUE on any history with a live retirement or IMPROPER-reopening. No outcome passes on the whole space.
