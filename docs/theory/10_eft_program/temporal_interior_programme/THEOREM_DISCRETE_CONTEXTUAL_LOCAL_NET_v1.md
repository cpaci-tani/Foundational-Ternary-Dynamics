## 1. Statement

Let `Lambda` be a finite specified set of lattice sites and let
`S={-1,0,+1}`.

1. The algebra of state-record functions is
   `D_Lambda = C(S^Lambda) ~= C^(3^|Lambda|)`. It is commutative and has
   `3^|Lambda|` minimal central projections.
2. The selected qutrit potentiality algebra is
   `A_pot^ref(Lambda)=tensor_Lambda M_3(C) ~= M_(3^|Lambda|)(C)`. It is a
   finite Type-I factor and is noncommutative for nonzero `Lambda`.
3. The diagonal embedding maps `D_Lambda` into a maximal abelian subalgebra
   of `A_pot^ref(Lambda)`.
4. If `Lambda subset Gamma`, tensoring by identities gives an injective
   unital embedding `A_pot^ref(Lambda) -> A_pot^ref(Gamma)`, proving isotony.
5. Algebras supported on disjoint tensor factors commute.
6. Compatible density operators restrict consistently by partial trace.
7. The norm-inductive limit over finite regions is the selected qutrit UHF
   C*-algebra. A von Neumann factor type is a property of a GNS closure for a
   specified state, not of finite level spacing.

Items 1–7 are standard finite-dimensional/operator-algebra results inside
the selected reference construction. The identification of this construction
with physical potentiality is an adopted v2 type, not a theorem of P1–P5.

## 2. Correction of the superseded construction

The archived predecessor treated “three possible classical values” as enough
to identify the actual algebra with `M_3(C)`. That step was invalid: classical
observables on a three-point sample space form `C^3`, the diagonal algebra.
`M_3(C)` becomes meaningful only after a separate noncommutative potentiality
type is adopted.

The retired modular-spectrum script also inferred Type III_1 from dense
finite spectra and random-matrix spacing. Such statistics do not determine a
Murray–von Neumann factor type. No output of that script remains
proof-of-record.
