A *tracker* is a subsystem whose internal state carries information about
the **dispositional layer** — sub-threshold `J` — rather than about the
manifest layer `s`. Representation is the third purchased structure of the
temporal interior: it converts potentiality into content a system can hold.
The ladder has three rungs; each has criteria fixed here, before any
construction; **no rung's language may be used for a candidate until every
lower rung has closed positive under lock.**

## Rung a — the sensor (correlation with present potentiality)

**a1.** A declared subsystem `S` with fixed support, and a declared internal
observable `Q_S` that is not a closing linear functional of the field.
**a2.** A declared family of sub-threshold environment patterns
`{J_i}` (all `|J| < K_GENESIS` on `S`'s support horizon — no manifestation
mediates the coupling; the channel is the native sub-threshold dynamics
itself, e.g. the phase_read pathway).
**a3.** Measured mutual information / declared discrimination statistic
between `Q_S` and the pattern index `i`, above a pre-registered floor, at a
pre-registered horizon; look-elsewhere controlled.
**a4.** Licensing and coupled-escape checks as in R8/R9.

*Ceiling:* rung a establishes only correlation with **present** dispositions
— a thermometer for potentiality. Tag ceiling `[ENGINE FACT — MEASURED,
profile-scoped]`.

## Rung b — memory of potential (retention of absent potentiality)

**b1.** All of rung a, plus: after the pattern `J_i` is removed (declared
removal protocol), `Q_S` retains discrimination above the floor for at
least `K_ret` cycles of a companion clock.
**b2.** **Gate: requires a register.** Rung b is *blocked by front T2* — a
candidate must embed an R1–R9-qualified register (or jointly qualify one);
retention claims without a qualified register repeat the FTD-0777 error in
new vocabulary.
**b3.** The retained state must be about the *removed* pattern, verified by
a declared decoding protocol on held-out patterns.

*Ceiling:* a system holding content about potentiality that is no longer
present — the minimal "was-able." Tag ceiling `[ENGINE FACT — MEASURED]` +
the register's own price.

## Rung c — offline traversal (QUARANTINED LANGUAGE)

**c1.** All of rung b, plus: a declared *decoupling* condition — mutual
information between `Q_S`'s trajectory and the concurrent environment below
a floor over the traversal window — while
**c2.** the internal trajectory visits at least `M` distinct stored-state
configurations under the system's own dynamics (declared enumeration,
declared `M`), and
**c3.** the visited sequence is readable by the declared decoder (the
traversal is content-bearing, not noise).

*Ceiling:* internal dynamics over representations of absent potentiality —
the structural minimum the charter's spine calls traversal. **The words
"imagination," "simulation," or any experiential gloss are quarantined**:
they may appear only in an identification claim, separately tagged
`[CONJECTURE — INTERPRETIVE]`, and only after c1–c3 close positive under
lock. Rung c makes no appearance in any candidate document until rungs a
and b are closed.

## Standing relations

- The ladder does **not** presuppose the Born statistical layer (front T3);
  a tracker reads dispositions dynamically, not probabilistically. If T3's
  programme later grounds a native measure, rung-a discrimination statistics
  can be restated in it — a strengthening, not a dependency.
- The whole ladder is downstream of the charter's ceiling sentence: at
  total success it delivers priced constructions, and the step from
  scaffold to interior is an identification, tagged as one.
