# THEOREM — Quadratic-coat composite Peierls obstruction

**Identifier:** `FTD-0553`  
**Status:** `[THEOREM — RIGID INTEGER-OFFSET COMPOSITE PEIERLS LAW] +
[NUMERICAL FACT — LOCKED FINITE-VOLUME VERIFICATION] + [CLOSED NEGATIVE —
RIGID NEUTRAL-COMPOSITE SELF-FORCE CURE]`  
**Scope:** finite periodic matched face complex, FTD-0541 compact quadratic
coat, minimum-energy longitudinal dressing, rigid integer-offset constituents.

## 1. Composite source

Let distinct existing ternary sites carry `q_a in {-1,+1}` at integer offsets
`n_a`, with `sum q_a=0`. Preserve the constituent decomposition required by
FTD-0501. A rigid subcell translation along axis `i` deposits

```text
rho_f(n)=sum_a q_a W(n-R-n_a-f e_i).              (1)
```

The base-site structure factor is

```text
A(k)=sum_a q_a exp(-i k dot n_a).                 (2)
```

No aggregate-worldline assumption is needed: (1) is explicitly the sum of
the individual signed coats.

## 2. Exact quadratic-coat Fourier identity

Inside one centered cell, `-1/2 <= f <= 1/2`, the one-dimensional quadratic
B-spline has the three nonzero samples

```text
w_-=(1/2)(1/2-f)^2,
w_0=3/4-f^2,
w_+=(1/2)(1/2+f)^2.                              (3)
```

Writing `c=cos k`, direct expansion gives

```text
B(k,f)=w_- exp(+ik)+w_0+w_+ exp(-ik),
|B(k,f)|^2=((3+c)/4)^2+(1-c)^2(f^4-f^2/2).       (4)
```

This is an identity, not a small-`f` expansion. It isolates all continuous
subcell dependence into the single quartic

```text
Q(f)=f^4-f^2/2.                                   (5)
```

## 3. Exact finite-volume energy law

For positive-face divergence `D`, let `E_f` be the unique zero-harmonic,
minimum-norm solution of `D E_f=rho_f`. On a periodic `L^3` lattice the scalar
Laplacian eigenvalue is

```text
lambda(k)=2 sum_j (1-cos k_j).                    (6)
```

Parseval and (2)--(4) give the physical field energy

```text
U(f)=beta ||E_f||^2/2=U(0)+C_i Q(f),              (7)

C_i=beta/(2L^3) sum_{k != 0}
    |A(k)|^2 (1-cos k_i)^2
    prod_{j != i} ((3+cos k_j)/4)^2 / lambda(k).  (8)
```

Every summand in (8) is nonnegative, and every transverse coat factor is
strictly positive. Therefore

```text
C_i=0  iff  A(k)=0 for every mode with k_i != 0.  (9)
```

The right side of (9) is exactly the Fourier criterion that the integer base
source be invariant under one-site translation along `i`. A nonzero localized
source is not invariant along every axis. Consequently every nonzero localized
rigid composite has `C_i>0` on at least one translation axis. For the locked
finite structures, it is positive on all three.

Equation (7) supplies the exact barrier and force:

```text
U(0)-U(1/2)=C_i/16>0,
dU/df=C_i f(4f^2-1).                              (10)
```

Thus integer-centered configurations are maxima and half-cell configurations
are minima inside the centered chart. The pinning seen in FTD-0552 is the
stationarity of these extrema, not restored continuous translation symmetry.

## 4. Exact current-work theorem

Translate every constituent along the same straight segment and sum its exact
FTD-0541 current into `K`. Exact continuity gives

```text
rho_1-rho_0+D K=0.                                (11)
```

Both endpoint fields are longitudinal. Hence `E_1-E_0+K` is divergence-free
and is orthogonal to `Ebar=(E_0+E_1)/2`. Therefore

```text
U_1-U_0
=beta <Ebar,E_1-E_0>
=-beta <Ebar,K>.                                  (12)
```

FTD-0550 makes the final pairing the summed constituent orbit work. The
composite therefore experiences the conservative center-of-mass impulse
generated by (10). Exact energy conservation proves the self-force is real
within the selected lattice dynamics; it does not cancel it.

## 5. What neutrality does and does not cancel

Neutrality removes the zero Fourier mode because `A(0)=0`. It does not remove
the nonzero alias-sensitive modes in (8). Exact cancellation occurs only for:

1. the zero deposited source, including a coincident opposite pair that is
   indistinguishable from vacuum in the signed coupling variables and is not
   an allowed distinct-site primitive state; or
2. a source invariant along the translation axis, which is spatially extended
   rather than a localized particle.

A rigid localized neutral carrier therefore cannot repair the FTD-0552
self-force while retaining the compact quadratic coat and minimum-energy face
dressing.

## 6. Boundary of the theorem

This theorem does not exclude a carrier whose internal separation changes with
subcell phase, a globally supported band-limited coat, a physical Peierls
medium interpreted as fundamental, or a new binding variable. Each changes a
locked premise. In particular, an internally deforming carrier would need a
derived internal schedule that keeps the full configuration energy and
constituent continuity reciprocal; neutrality alone supplies no such law.
