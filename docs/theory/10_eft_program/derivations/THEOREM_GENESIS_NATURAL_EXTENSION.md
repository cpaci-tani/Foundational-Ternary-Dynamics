## 1. Finite-dimensional is not finite-information

FTD-0569 proves that repeated discarded branch outcomes require an unbounded
history record. That statement has two inequivalent realizations:

1. a finite-precision record must grow without bound;
2. a fixed number of exact real coordinates can store the same unbounded
   symbolic history in successively finer digits.

The second option is mathematically constructive. It is not available to the
binary64 production engine and is not automatically a physical ontology.

Let `(u,v)` lie in the unit square and let `0<p<1`. Define

\[
B_p(u,v)=
\begin{cases}
(u/p,pv),&u<p,\\
((u-p)/(1-p),p+(1-p)v),&u\ge p.
\end{cases}
\]

The accepted branch maps onto the strip `0<=V<p`; the rejected branch maps
onto `p<=V<1`. Hence the future `V` coordinate supplies the inverse branch:

\[
B_p^{-1}(U,V)=
\begin{cases}
(pU,V/p),&V<p,\\
(p+(1-p)U,(V-p)/(1-p)),&V\ge p.
\end{cases}
\]

Both branch Jacobians have determinant one:

\[
\det\!\begin{pmatrix}1/p&0\\0&p\end{pmatrix}=1,
\qquad
\det\!\begin{pmatrix}1/(1-p)&0\\0&1-p\end{pmatrix}=1.
\]

The two strips partition the square, so the map is bijective up to the usual
measure-zero boundary convention and preserves Lebesgue area. The locked 48
floating-point arms invert below `2.23e-16`; the independent rational proof
inverts a 100-step variable-probability sequence exactly without retaining a
separate branch word.

Therefore the correct refinement of FTD-0569 is:

> the finite-capacity obstruction excludes a finite-information local
> reservoir, not a finite-dimensional reservoir made of exact reals.

## 2. The raw genesis drain is not canonical on `(J,W)`

Consider the accepted canonical single-substrate map

\[
J'=\left(1-\frac{k_g}{r}\right)J,
\qquad
W'=aW,
\qquad
r=|J|=k_g+x,
\qquad a=1-d.
\]

Let `n=J/r` and `t=x/(x+k_g)`. The flux Jacobian is

\[
A=\frac{\partial J'}{\partial J}
=tI+(1-t)nn^{\mathsf T}.
\]

It has radial eigenvalue one and two tangential eigenvalues `t`. If `J` and
`W` were a canonical coordinate--momentum pair, the block map would be
symplectic only if

\[
A^{\mathsf T}(aI)=I.
\]

The radial and tangential eigenvalue defects are

\[
\delta_{\parallel}=a-1=-d,
\qquad
\delta_{\perp}=at-1<0.
\]

The second defect is strictly nonzero for every finite supercritical input,
including `d=0`. The six-dimensional volume Jacobian is

\[
\det DF_{\rm raw}=t^2a^3<1.
\]

Across the 2,160 registered accepted arms, the smallest magnitude of the
tangential defect is `0.44444444444444431` and the largest volume Jacobian is
`0.30864197530864212`. Thus the raw genesis drain cannot be generated by a
canonical transformation on the frozen `(J,W)` pair.

This is stronger than information loss: even the conditionally invertible
accepted branch is dissipative in the proposed canonical variables.

## 3. A branchwise symplectic lift exists after enlarging the ontology

The strongest constructive result treats all six entries

\[
q=(J,W)
\]

as configuration coordinates and adds six conjugates `pi`. It also adds the
environmental phase pair `(u,v)` and a time--reservoir-energy pair `(tau,R)`.
This adds ten continuous variables to the existing six. It is a sufficient
construction, not a proof of minimality.

For accepted and rejected branches define

\[
S_1(u,V;p)=\frac{uV}{p},
\qquad
S_0(u,V;p)=\frac{(u-p)(V-p)}{1-p}.
\]

The identities `v=partial S_b/partial u` and
`U=partial S_b/partial V` reproduce the generalized baker map. On the realized
branches,

\[
g_1=\frac{\partial S_1}{\partial p}=-\frac{uv}{p},
\qquad
g_0=\frac{\partial S_0}{\partial p}=Uv-U-v.
\]

Let `F_1(q)` be accepted genesis, `F_0(q)=q`, and

\[
D_b(q)=\frac{|q|^2-|F_b(q)|^2}{2}.
\]

The type-2 generator

\[
\mathcal F_{2,b}
=F_b(q)\!\cdot\!\Pi+S_b(u,V;p(q))
+\tau\,[R'-D_b(q)]
\]

gives

\[
Q=F_b(q),\qquad
R'=R+D_b(q),\qquad
\tau'=\tau,
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\Pi=DF_b(q)^{-\mathsf T}
\left[\pi-g_b\nabla p+\tau\nabla D_b\right].}
\]

Because these equations come from one generating function, the enlarged map
is branchwise symplectic. It also conserves

\[

\]

exactly, since `R` receives the quadratic field withdrawal. All 4,320 locked
arms invert below `1.89e-15`; the extended energy closes below `8.89e-16`; and
the generating equations close below the registered tolerance.

The `-g_b grad p` term is essential. It is the conjugate backreaction caused
by the state dependence of the acceptance probability. A direct product of a
fixed-`p` baker map with genesis would miss this term and would not be a
derived common action.

## 4. Why the construction is not a production rescue

The lift changes the ontology in three independent ways:

1. `W` is reclassified as part of a six-dimensional configuration and receives
   a new conjugate rather than serving as the conjugate of `J`;
2. the phase pair `(u,v)` replaces the stateless production RNG with a
   dynamically updated exact-real environment;
3. `(tau,R)` supplies a continuous energy reservoir absent from the engine.

None of these variables is derived from the five postulates or the frozen
tick. Their initialization, locality across events, transport between sites,
and behavior under evaporation remain unspecified.

Binary64 also fails as the proposed exact history carrier. At `p=1/2`, two
64-event histories differing in their oldest branch and sharing 63 subsequent
upper branches are distinct rational numbers, yet both round to `1.0`. The
inverse branch is then unrecoverable and the nominal `[0,1)` invariant is
lost. Exact-real reversibility is therefore an infinite-information ideal,
not an engine implementation.

## 5. Projected absolute irreversibility survives

For the raw production pair `a->b`, accepted genesis has positive forward
probability while the exact reverse event is absent:

\[
P(a\!\to\!b)>0,
\qquad
P(b\!\to\!a)=0.
\]

The projected pathwise log forward/reverse ratio is consequently

\[
\log\frac{P(a\to b)}{P(b\to a)}=+\infty.
\]

The natural extension removes this singularity only on the enlarged state
space where the environment coordinates and conjugates are retained. It does
not make the projected production kernel equilibrium or detailed-balanced.
