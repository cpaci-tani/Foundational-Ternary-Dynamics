## 0 · The question

MC-T4.3 is the central foundational obstruction: α's value rides on selecting the master-quadratic root `x₊ = 8G*² + 4G*·δ`, with `δ = √(G*(4G*−1))`. FTD-0244 proved — **as a `[THEOREM]`, scoped to the fixed 5-generator native operator calculus 𝔠** — that no `T ∈ 𝔠` forces the assembly: all invariants of 𝔠 lie in `ℚ(G*)`, while the root-selection lives in the degree-2 Galois extension `ℚ(G*)(δ)`, and the Galois `ℤ/2` (σ: δ ↦ −δ) fixes every `ℚ(G*)`-invariant. FTD-0242's *broader* boundary is only a `[STRONGLY MOTIVATED CONJECTURE no-go]` ("route-invariant"), because routes cannot be exhaustively enumerated.

This test attacks the **most natural unexamined loophole**: could one of FTD's **native ℤ/2 symmetries** — an unforced sign-choice the substrate already carries — supply the missing δ-selection when adjoined to 𝔠? FTD-0317 already proved `i ⊥ δ` (the FC-0 act is Galois-independent of the α act). This test extends that single check to **all** the native ℤ/2's, and returns **PERMANENT-EXTENDED** (none can) or **NEW-EXIT** (one can — named).

A **native ℤ/2** here means an *involution the substrate carries intrinsically* (a sign/orientation/conjugation symmetry of the lattice ontology), as opposed to a positivity-forced magnitude (one admissible branch) or the Galois root-swap itself (which is the act under test, not a native operator).

## 1 · The inventory of native ℤ/2's (LOCKED — no post-hoc add/drop)

The classification must cover **exactly** these five:

1. **i-conjugation** — complex conjugation on `ℤ[i]` (FC-0's reading); the involution `J ↦ −J` / `c: a+bi ↦ a−bi`. Already implicit in 𝔠 via `J`.
2. **±ω reversible-wave orientation** — the free, arrow-less `ℤ/2` of the flux/wave sector (FTD-0316): the sign of a frequency mode (`e^{+iωt}` vs `e^{−iωt}`).
3. **matter/antimatter charge sign** — the ternary state involution `s ↦ −s` (swap `+1 ↔ −1`), the C-symmetry of the state field.
4. **cubic-lattice parity / inversion** — the `O_h` center (spatial inversion `x ↦ −x`).
5. **time-reversal T** — the linear part of the motion-reversing involution (sign on momentum / `∂_t`).

**Explicitly EXCLUDED and flagged (not native free ℤ/2's):**
- the positivity-forced sign choices `±√3` (`C_SPEED = 1/√3`) and `±√α` (`G_C = √α`) — **forced magnitudes** with one admissible branch (a positive speed / positive coupling), not free `ℤ/2`'s, per the FTD-0317 criterion;
- the Galois root-swap `σ: δ ↦ −δ` itself — **this is the act under test**, the thing we are asking whether any *native* operator can realize; it is not itself a native lattice symmetry.

## 2 · The field-extension taxonomy (locked; reuse FTD-0244)

Each native `ℤ/2` generator `g`, adjoined to 𝔠, is classified by the field generated by the invariants (traces, determinants) of the algebra `⟨𝔠, g⟩` acting on the readout module `V_complex ≅ ℤ[i]²`:

- **BLIND** — every invariant of `⟨𝔠, g⟩` lies in `ℚ(G*)`. Then `g` is fixed by the Galois `ℤ/2` and **cannot supply δ**.
- **REACHES-δ** — some invariant of `⟨𝔠, g⟩` lies in `ℚ(G*)(δ) \ ℚ(G*)` (degree 2 over `ℚ(G*)`, the element `δ` up to `ℚ(G*)`-scale). Then `g` **supplies the missing selection** — a third, non-closed-negative exit.

## 3 · Mechanical criterion (reuse FTD-0244 §2–§3 — do NOT re-derive)

For each `g`:
1. Write a matrix representation of `g` on `V_complex` (or the relevant sector); record its entry-field.
2. By FTD-0244 Lemma 1: any operator whose **entries lie in `ℚ(G*)`** has `Tr, Det ∈ ℚ(G*)`. So if `g`'s representation has entries in `ℚ(G*)` (a fortiori in `ℚ`), then `⟨𝔠, g⟩` — closed under sum/product/`ℚ`-scaling of `ℚ(G*)`-entry matrices — has **all** matrix entries in `ℚ(G*)`, hence all invariants in `ℚ(G*)` ⇒ **BLIND**.
3. `g` is **REACHES-δ** only if its representation genuinely forces an entry outside `ℚ(G*)` equal to `δ` up to `ℚ(G*)`-scale — checked by the squarefree/transcendence test (Chudnovsky: `G*` transcendental ⇒ `G*(4G*−1)` is a non-square in `ℚ(G*)`, so `δ` has minimal polynomial `y² − G*(4G*−1)` of degree 2; FTD-0244 Lemma 2).
