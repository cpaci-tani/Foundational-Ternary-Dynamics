## 1. Result

The oriented ternary transfer proved in FTD-0872 has an exact continuous
Hamiltonian lift. Embed its latch/output pair into one canonical carrier mode,

\[
 (p,q)=a(s,o),\qquad a>0,
 \qquad A=\frac{p^2+q^2}{2}.                  \tag{1}
\]

Add one independent clock pair `(theta,I)`. For frozen eligibility
`e in {0,1}` and orientation `sigma in {-1,+1}`, the imposed onsite law

\[
 H_{e,\sigma}
 =\Omega I+\nu A
 +e\sigma\kappa(1-\cos\theta)A               \tag{2}
\]

with

\[
 \nu=\Omega,qquad \kappa=\frac{\Omega}{4}   \tag{3}
\]

gives after one complete clock cycle

\[
 e=0:\quad(s,o)\mapsto(s,o),                 \tag{4}
\]

\[
 e=1,\sigma=+1:\quad(s,o)\mapsto(-o,s),     \tag{5}
\]

\[
 e=1,\sigma=-1:\quad(s,o)\mapsto(o,-s).     \tag{6}
\]

Thus the actual-layer quarter-turn, its inverse, and hold are all restrictions
of one orientation-preserving Hamiltonian flow. This closes the reference
actuator trajectory and its energy ledger. It does not derive the Hamiltonian,
its scale, or its one-shot controller from production.

## 2. Why four phase-space dimensions are sufficient and minimum here

The ordered carrier pair `(p,q)` is one complete canonical mode. Hamilton's
equations from (2) are

\[
 \dot p=-\omega_{e,\sigma}(\theta)q,
 \qquad
 \dot q=\omega_{e,\sigma}(\theta)p,           \tag{7}
\]

where

\[
 \omega_{e,\sigma}(\theta)
 =\nu+e\sigma\kappa(1-\cos\theta).           \tag{8}
\]

Equation (7) is generated by the same matrix

\[
 R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}    \tag{9}
\]

that appears in FTD-0872. It preserves `A` exactly.

An autonomous phase gate requires an independent phase coordinate and its
conjugate action. Therefore the registered class needs one two-dimensional
carrier pair plus one two-dimensional clock pair: four continuous
phase-space dimensions. This is a minimum **inside that class**, not a theorem
that every conceivable discrete actuator must use four real coordinates.

The prior FTD-0865 construction required six dimensions because it lifted a
full two-mode matter/signal swap. The present task is smaller: the two actual
registers are the two quadratures of one oriented carrier mode, so a second
carrier mode is unnecessary.

## 3. Exact winding

Starting at `theta=0`, the clock obeys

\[
 \dot\theta=\Omega,
 \qquad T=\frac{2\pi}{\Omega}.                \tag{10}
\]

The carrier angle accumulated in one cycle is

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Phi_{e,\sigma}
 &=\int_0^T\omega_{e,\sigma}(\theta(t))dt\\
 &=2\pi\frac{\nu}{\Omega}
 +e\sigma 2\pi\frac{\kappa}{\Omega}\\
 &=2\pi+e\sigma\frac{\pi}{2}.               \tag{11}
 \end{aligned}
\]

The base winding is an exact identity. The active excess is the required
quarter-turn. Equation (11), restricted through (1), proves (4)--(6) on all
nine ternary pairs.

In particular,

\[
 (s,0)\xrightarrow{\sigma=+1}(0,s),
 \qquad
 (0,s)\xrightarrow{\sigma=-1}(s,0).          \tag{12}
\]

## 4. Physical scale and exact energy ledger

The endpoint carrier energy is

\[
 E_{\rm carrier}=\nu A
 =\frac{\nu a^2}{2}(s^2+o^2).                \tag{13}
\]

This makes the label norm dimensional only after the amplitude `a` and
frequency `nu` are imposed. The coefficient

\[
 \epsilon_{\rm rec}=\frac{\nu a^2}{2}        \tag{14}
\]

is therefore an input scale, not an FTD substrate prediction.

The clock action evolves as

\[
 I(\theta)=I_0
 -e\sigma\frac{\kappa}{\Omega}
 A(1-\cos\theta).                             \tag{15}
\]

Substitution into (2) gives the phase-independent total

\[
 H_{e,\sigma}=\Omega I_0+\nu A.              \tag{16}
\]

At the minimum winding,

\[
 |\Delta I|_{\max}=e\frac A2,
 \qquad
 |\Delta E_{\rm ref}|_{\max}
 =|E_{\rm int}|_{\max}
 =e\frac{\Omega A}{2}.                       \tag{17}
\]

A conservative reserve supporting either orientation is

\[
 I_0>\frac A2.                                \tag{18}
\]

At the endpoint, `I(T)=I_0`, the interaction vanishes, and

\[
 \Delta E_{\rm total}=0.                     \tag{19}
\]

Equation (19) means zero **net endpoint** work for an uninterrupted ideal
cycle. It does not mean that actuation is work-free: equation (17) is the
nonzero transient exchange the clock must support.

## 5. Switching work

Changing the signed control `e sigma` at fixed phase changes the Hamiltonian
by

\[
 \Delta H_{\rm switch}
 =\Delta(e\sigma)\kappa(1-\cos\theta)A.       \tag{20}
\]

At a preregistered gate zero, equation (20) vanishes. At `theta=pi`, its
magnitude for a unit control change is

\[
 |\Delta H_{\rm switch}|=2\kappa A
 =\frac{\Omega A}{2}.                         \tag{21}
\]

Thus phase-correct switching is an energetic condition, not merely a software
convention. Acquisition and release of the control still require a dynamic
physical realization.

## 6. Exact one-shot boundary

The Hamiltonian cycle is autonomous once `e` and `sigma` are fixed, but those
controls are frozen during the registered cycle. Repeating the forward active
cycle gives

\[
 R^2=-I,                                      \tag{22}
\]

not hold. Nor can invariant carrier norm stop the next cycle: a ready event
and its emitted endpoint both have the same nonzero `A`.

Therefore neither “repeat while nonzero” nor a norm-only clutch is a one-shot
scheduler. Exact recursion still requires one of the already-booked options:

1. gate-zero acquisition and release tied to signal completion;
2. immediate reciprocal handoff that returns the local carrier to zero before
   the next actuator cycle;
3. a retained stroke/controller state with its own inverse and work ledger; or
4. a declared external schedule.

This is not an information-theoretic demand for a new acknowledgement bit.
FTD-0871 already proves that a retained oriented signal can uncompute the
actual latch. It is a dynamical requirement that the operations occur in a
reversible order.

## 7. Separation from the quartic G* clock

The actuator in (2) is harmonic and isochronous. That is why its pulse angle
(11) is independent of carrier load. FTD-0865 proves that a strictly convex
quartic action clock has a load-dependent pulse area in the analogous minimal
class. Consequently the honest architecture remains split:

- the harmonic pair supplies orientation-preserving actuation;
- the selected quartic clock may supply a separate eligibility calendar; and
- the synchronization gearbox identifying their phase crossings remains
  open.

`G*` does not appear in equations (1)--(21). FTD-0836's exact
`sqrt(pi) G*` traversal remains the conditional quartic-calendar law, not the
energy source or transfer angle of this actuator.
