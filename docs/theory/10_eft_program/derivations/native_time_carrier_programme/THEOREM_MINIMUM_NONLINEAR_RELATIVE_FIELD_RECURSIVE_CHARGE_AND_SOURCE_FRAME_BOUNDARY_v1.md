## 2. Why the sextic is the minimum honest potential

Register the class of radial polynomials in `y=r^2` that are:

- even in the field;
- nonnegative;
- zero at the relative vacuum `r=0`; and
- zero again at a degenerate nonzero ring `r=A`.

A polynomial of degree four in `r` has the forced form

\[
 c\,r^2(r^2-A^2).                                               \tag{4}
\]

It changes sign between and beyond its two zeros, regardless of the sign of
`c`. It cannot be a nonnegative energy.

At degree six, nonnegativity makes the nonzero zero a double root. The three
conditions `P(0)=P(A^2)=P'(A^2)=0` uniquely give, up to a positive scale,

\[
 \boxed{V(r)=\beta r^2(r^2-A^2)^2},
 \qquad \beta>0.                                               \tag{5}
\]

Its extrema are

\[
 r=0,qquad r={A\over\sqrt3},qquad r=A,                        \tag{6}
\]

with barrier

\[
 V\!\left({A\over\sqrt3}\right)={4\beta A^6\over27}.          \tag{7}
\]

The exact force is

\[
 \boxed{-\nabla V
 =-2\beta q(|q|^2-A^2)(3|q|^2-A^2).}                            \tag{8}
\]

Equation (5) is the radial two-component counterpart of the selected FTD-0848
ternary latch. It is a perfect-square factorization, not gauge-theoretic
self-duality and not a Bogomolny construction.

Written in the conventional sextic form

\[
 V(r)={m^2\over2}r^2-{g\over4}r^4+{h\over6}r^6,                 \tag{9}

\[
 m^2=2\beta A^4,
 \qquad g=8\beta A^2,

The last equality is the exact degeneracy condition. It is imposed by the

## 7. The finite-tick boundary

The natural local symmetric update is kick--drift--kick:

\[
 p_{n+1/2}=p_n-{\Delta n\over2}\nabla U(q_n),                   \tag{28}
\]

\[
 q_{n+1}=q_n+\Delta n\,p_{n+1/2},                              \tag{29}
\]

\[
 p_{n+1}=p_{n+1/2}-{\Delta n\over2}\nabla U(q_{n+1}).             \tag{30}
\]

Every kick is a canonical shear generated by the local `C18` plus onsite
potential. The drift is an onsite canonical shear. Therefore the composed map
is:

- finite-range local per substep;
- symplectic;
- exactly inverted by the same composition with `Delta n -> -Delta n`;
- time symmetric; and
- exactly charge preserving, because each potential kick has zero total
  axial torque and the drift adds only `p cross p=0` to (3).

It is not exactly energy preserving. Even the one-dimensional harmonic
witness beginning at `(q,p)=(1,0)` has

\[

\]

which is nonzero at generic finite step. The candidate tick is therefore an
excellent reversible charge-preserving reference integrator, but it cannot be
installed as the exact energy law of the substrate without an additional
energy account or a different update.

Sampling the exact Hamiltonian flow would preserve both `H_e` and `Q_e`, but
that mathematical map is not thereby a finite-range computable production
tick. This distinction remains live.

## 8. Why gamma does not come from i

\[

\]

\[

\]

The two coefficients play categorically different roles:

- `omega J_e` is antisymmetric and rotates without changing the quadratic
  norm;
- `-gamma Pi_e` is symmetric and contracts the norm.

With momentum damping,

\[
 \dot p=\text{Hamiltonian force}-\gamma p,
\]

the charge obeys

\[
 \boxed{\dot Q_e=-\gamma Q_e.}                                 \tag{34}
\]

Therefore `i` does not yield `gamma`. The conservative recursive mechanism
sets `gamma=0`. A nonzero `gamma` is a separate dissipative adoption and must
name a bath or feedback channel that receives the energy and information it

\[

\[

\]

pseudoscalar handedness datum, or an explicit restriction to proper rotations.
