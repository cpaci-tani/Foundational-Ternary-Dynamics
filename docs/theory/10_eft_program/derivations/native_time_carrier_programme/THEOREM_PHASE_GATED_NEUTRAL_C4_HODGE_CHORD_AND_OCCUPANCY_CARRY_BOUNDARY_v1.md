## 2. Why `s^2` is forced in the registered class

Let

\[
 a(s)=a_0+a_1s+a_2s^2,
 \qquad s\in\{-1,0,+1\}.                              \tag{5}
\]

Require

\[
 a(0)=0,
 \qquad a(-s)=a(s),
 \qquad a(1)=1.                                      \tag{6}
\]

The first condition gives `a_0=0`; evenness gives `a_1=0`; normalization
gives `a_2=1`. Therefore equation (1) is unique.

This is a scoped theorem. Removing normalization leaves a scalar multiple;
allowing neighborhood dependence, histories, or additional fields enlarges
the class. Equation (1) assigns neither mass nor energy. It is a bounded
function of the existing actual record:

\[
 s=0\mapsto\nu=0,
 \qquad s=\pm1\mapsto\nu=1.                           \tag{7}
\]

The actual algebra therefore contains a natural signed/even pair:

\[
 \rho_s=s,
 \qquad \rho_{\rm occ}=s^2.                          \tag{8}
\]

Charge conjugation reverses the first and preserves the second.

## 6. Why one-hop occupancy is not persistent carry

The occupancy current owns the ordered crossing during one registered hop.
The endpoint record does not retain the number of earlier closed crossings.

On any finite periodic computational quotient, compare:

1. a history that remains at its initial site; and
2. a history that traverses one complete cycle and returns.

Their endpoint ternary and occupancy records are identical, while their
winding differs by one. Repeating the second history produces any integer
winding with the same endpoint record. Hence

\[
 \boxed{
 (s_n,s_n^2)\ \text{at one tick}
 \not\Longrightarrow W_\nu.}                         \tag{27}
\]

Persistent Markovian carry requires at least one of:

- retained local face-crossing history;
- a body/worldline identity that integrates those crossings;
- an unwrapped source coordinate; or
- a separately adopted link/carry state.

The certificate adopts none of them. It identifies the transient owner and
the exact information that is lost when the crossing record is discarded.
