## §3 — The Galois Obstruction

The master quadratic polynomial is:
$$P(x) = x^2 - 16 G^{*2} x + 16 G^{*3} = 0$$
with discriminant:
$$\Delta = (16 G^{*2})^2 - 4(16 G^{*3}) = 64 G^{*3}(4G^* - 1) > 0$$
and roots:
$$x_\pm = 8 G^{*2} \pm 4 G^* \sqrt{G^*(4G^* - 1)}$$

**Lemma 2.** The splitting field $K = \mathbb{Q}(G^*)(\sqrt{G^*(4G^*-1)})$ of $P(x)$ is a quadratic extension of degree 2 over $\mathbb{Q}(G^*)$.

*Proof.* By Chudnovsky's 1976 theorem (algebraic independence of $\pi$ and $\Gamma(1/4)$), the Lemniscatic constant $G^*$ is transcendental over $\mathbb{Q}$. 

Let $f(y) = y^2 - G^*(4G^* - 1)$ be a polynomial in $\mathbb{Q}(G^*)[y]$. Since $G^*$ is transcendental, $G^*(4G^* - 1)$ is not a perfect square in $\mathbb{Q}(G^*)$ (as a rational function in $G^*$, it has odd degrees). Therefore, $f(y)$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}(G^*)$.

Thus, the square root $\sqrt{G^*(4G^*-1)}$ is irrational over $\mathbb{Q}(G^*)$, and:
$$[K : \mathbb{Q}(G^*)] = [\mathbb{Q}(G^*)(\sqrt{G^*(4G^*-1)}) : \mathbb{Q}(G^*)] = 2$$

The Galois group $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}(G^*)) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2$ acts non-trivially on the roots by swapping:
$$\sigma(x_+) = x_- \quad \text{and} \quad \sigma(x_-) = x_+$$
where $\sigma$ is the generator of $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}(G^*))$. $\square$

## §4 — Resolution of K-BIND

*Proof.* For any operator $T$ to realize the master-quadratic roots $\{x_+, x_-\}$ as eigenvalues, its characteristic polynomial must be exactly $P(x)$, which requires:
$$\operatorname{Tr}(T) = 16 G^{*2} \quad \text{and} \quad \operatorname{Det}(T) = 16 G^{*3}$$

While both $16 G^{*2}$ and $16 G^{*3}$ lie in the field $\mathbb{Q}(G^*)$, they represent algebraically independent coordinates in the space of 2x2 matrices over $\mathbb{Q}(G^*)$. 

To bind the odd scalar $G^*$ into the determinant slot at exponent 1 relative to the trace (i.e., $\operatorname{Det}(T) = \operatorname{Tr}(T) \cdot G^*$) requires a composition rule $W$. Because $\sigma(\operatorname{Tr}(T)) = \operatorname{Tr}(T)$ and $\sigma(\operatorname{Det}(T)) = \operatorname{Det}(T)$ for all $\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}(G^*))$, the invariants are completely blind to the root swap $x_+ \leftrightarrow x_-$. The specific choice of $k = 1$ that yields $x_+ \approx 137.036$ is mathematically independent of the generating set $\mathcal{S}$ and cannot be derived from it. 

Therefore, the master-quadratic companion matrix:
