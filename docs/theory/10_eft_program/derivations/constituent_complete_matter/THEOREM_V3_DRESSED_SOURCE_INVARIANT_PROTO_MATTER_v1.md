## 3. Why the result is proto-matter, not a particle

The family is an autonomous recurrent source-bearing pattern, but it has not
been shown to form from a generic finite seed. Its local basin is exact rather
than robust: removing one required packet record exits the family. Moreover,
the candidate source macro has no derived positive work ledger for activating
the eight field channels.

Therefore the theorem does not establish:

- formation or decay from the surrounding finite dynamics;
- a nonzero local perturbation basin;
- volume-stable metastability under interacting fields;
- multi-object scattering or exclusion;
- inertial mass, rest energy, or a dispersion relation; or
- particle charge/species identification.

The honest status is **invariant proto-matter**. It proves that the finite v3
carrier can support an exactly persistent relational object without a new
identity or history type, while sharply locating what stable physical matter
still requires.
