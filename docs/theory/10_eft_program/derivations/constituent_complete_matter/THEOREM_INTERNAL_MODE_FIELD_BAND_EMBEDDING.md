vanish by symmetry, destructive interference, a bound state in the continuum,
or a topological invariant. FTD-0662 supplies the complementary finite-volume
fact: the prepared internal doublet transfers energy into a dynamic residual
field with ordered near-to-far shell support in every registered arm. Complete
decoupling is therefore false for that prepared excitation and selected
action.
