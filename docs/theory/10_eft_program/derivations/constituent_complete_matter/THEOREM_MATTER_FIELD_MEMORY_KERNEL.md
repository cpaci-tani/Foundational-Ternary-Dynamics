# FTD-0667 — Matter--field memory-kernel theorem

**Status:** `[THEOREM — LINEARIZED BLOCK ELIMINATION] + [INTERPRETATION]`  
**Production impact:** none

## 1. Exact statement

Within any fixed differentiable chart/sector, write the linearized one-tick map
for complete matter variables `x_n` and complete field variables `y_n` as

\[
\begin{pmatrix}x_{n+1}\\y_{n+1}\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x_n\\y_n\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then, for every `n>=0`,

\[
y_n=D^n y_0+\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}D^{n-1-m}C x_m,
\]

and therefore

\[
x_{n+1}=A x_n+B D^n y_0
+\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}K_{n-1-m}x_m,
\qquad K_j=B D^j C.
\]

Thus eliminating the field produces an exact discrete memory law for matter.
The matter projection is first-order Markovian only in special cases, such as
`B D^j C=0` for every relevant `j` together with a fixed field preparation.

## 2. Proof

The field identity follows by induction. It is immediate for `n=0`. If it
holds for `n`, then

\[
y_{n+1}=C x_n+D y_n
=D^{n+1}y_0+\sum_{m=0}^{n}D^{n-m}C x_m.
\]

Substitution into `x_{n+1}=A x_n+B y_n` gives the matter equation and kernel.
No continuum limit, probability, damping ansatz, or spectral approximation is
used.

If two complete states have the same `x_n` but field difference `delta y_n`
with `B delta y_n !=0`, their next matter states differ. Hence no deterministic
first-order law on `x_n` alone can represent both histories.

## 3. Reversible transfer and revival

The full map may be invertible and energy preserving while the matter
projection loses amplitude and later revives. The exact two-coordinate
rotation

\[
T=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}
\]

preserves `x^2+y^2` and is invertible. Starting from `(x_0,y_0)=(1,0)`, its
matter coordinate follows `1,0,-1,0,1,...`: amplitude leaves the observed
coordinate and returns without information loss. Here `K_0=BC=-1`.

## 4. FTD scope

FTD-0665 measures nonzero pre-return matter-to-field transfer, which establishes
a nontrivial forward channel in the selected tangent neighborhood. FTD-0666
measures later matter revival, which establishes field/other-mode feedback at
complete nonlinear-history scope. The theorem shows why a matter-only action
need not be conserved even though the full state is exactly reversible.

It does **not** prove that the measured nonlinear return is generated by one
linear kernel, determine `K_j`, establish exponential decay, or license an
infinite-volume resonance. In an outgoing or effectively uncontained
environment, the local return blocks `B D^j C` may remain small for long times;
whether that occurs in FTD is the next empirical discriminator.
