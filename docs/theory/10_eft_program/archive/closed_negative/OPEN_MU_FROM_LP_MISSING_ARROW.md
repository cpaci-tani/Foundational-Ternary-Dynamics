## 1 · The problem (formal-semantic statement)

The L2 candidate identity (FTD-0094) reads, in CODATA form:

  2·m_e / α  =  16 G*²    (residual 68.77 ppm)

Under FTD's calibration

  mass-unit ≡ m_e / K_B = 1 MeV/c²,    K_B ≡ m_e (numerically, in MeV)

L2 simplifies to

  2 K_B  ≡  α · 16 G*²  =  α · (x₊ + x₋)        (calibration-invariant statement)
  K_B    ≡  8 α G*²                              (with α = 1/x₊ from FTD-0013)

The right-hand side is FTD-internal: every quantity there is computable from
Axiom-Zero invariants {D=3, ϖ, framework integers, c_lat=1/√3}. The
left-hand side, K_B, is presently **stipulative** — it is the dimensionless
number that makes m_e (in MeV) come out to 0.51100 by convention, because we
chose `mass-unit ≡ 1 MeV/c²` by external calibration.

This means L2 has the formal-semantic shape of a *coherence constraint* between
a stipulation (K_B) and a derivation (8αG*²). It is not a tautology; it is
not an empirical hypothesis; it is a **calibration-pinning identity** whose
status as a substantive prediction is conditional on the calibration choice
"1 MeV" being made for reasons logically prior to and independent of the
algebraic 8αG*² value.

If the mass-unit "1 MeV" was chosen for reasons independent of L2, then L2
asserts a non-trivial 68.77 ppm coincidence between two empirically anchored
numbers, and the coincidence demands explanation. If the mass-unit was chosen
*because* it gave the right value — even tacitly, even historically — then L2
is definitional and carries no empirical content beyond the original
calibration choice.

## 3 · Type-theoretic restatement

Let `MassUnit` be the type of mass-unit calibrations, parameterized by some
characterization μ that is independent of m_e. Then:

```
∀ μ : MassUnit.
    (2·m_e / α) [μ]   ≡   16 G*²    ↔    μ = μ_FTD
```

This reads: L2 holds, in the unit system μ, **if and only if** μ equals some
specific μ_FTD that is independently characterized. The L2 conjecture thus has
*two* parts:

  (i) μ_FTD is well-defined (the characterization exists) — this is what
      OPEN_MU_FROM_LP_MISSING_ARROW asks.
  (ii) The numerical value of m_e in μ_FTD-units, divided by α, equals
       16 G*² to ppm precision — this is what PROTOCOL_BCC_SUBLATTICE_SPECTRUM
       falsifies on the BCC spectrum side.

If (i) fails — i.e., if no independent characterization of μ exists — L2 is
permanently demoted to a calibration convention. The current 1-MeV
mass-unit becomes a fitting parameter, not a derivation.

The difference: length and time are *kinematic* dimensions that follow from
the lattice structure (sites, ticks, c_lat). Mass is *dynamic* — it appears
through the manifestation rule (genesis at |J| > K_GENESIS·K_B) and through
the master-quadratic chain. FTD-0059's proof technique (ring-of-invariants
algebra) does not directly apply because mass enters via threshold dynamics,
not as a basis vector of the dimensional ring.

**Open subquestion:** Can the FTD-0059 ring-algebra technique be extended to

permanently be tagged [PARAMETRIC]. If no, then μ_FTD might admit a
constructive derivation, and L2 has a path to [SMC]. **This subquestion is
the highest-leverage analytical move available** — it would either close
L2 negative cleanly, or open a constructive path forward.
