## What is learned

Periodic self-return does not explain the **pre-contact history through tick
80**. Over that history the complete difference field grows and spreads: its
radius-8 fraction falls to about 24 percent while its radial second moment
reaches about 494. Simultaneously the projected internal doublet remains
strongly oscillatory and turns upward after the tick-72 minimum.

That combination narrows the matter picture. The projected internal motion is
not merely a pulse that disappears into a returning box wave. A localized
constituent core continues to exchange energy with binding and a distributed
field reservoir while the complete state remains reversible. However, the
turning-point description is post-verdict inspection; because the registered
threshold was missed, it is not promoted to a constructive recurrence claim.

The threshold itself is exposed as volume-sensitive: at `L=33` the analogous
late minimum crosses `0.60`, whereas at `L=97` it bottoms near `0.631`. A fresh
out-of-sample campaign must use a phase/turning-point discriminator rather
than treating an arbitrary amplitude crossing as the definition of
recurrence.
