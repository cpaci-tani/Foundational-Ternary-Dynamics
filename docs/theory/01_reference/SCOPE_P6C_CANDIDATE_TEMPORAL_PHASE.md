## 1 · The candidate type

**P6C-T.** *Irreversible production events (tick-boundary collisions; and, in the extended form, genesis/annihilation vertices) carry a temporal phase: a sub-tick event datum τ ∈ (0, dt] determined by a fixed local rule R from the event's frozen inputs.*

Two forms are distinguished at registration, because they price into different classes:

- **T-rule (registered form):** the phase is set by one fixed, declared rule R (a law of the vertex). One-time structural adoption; **intensive** in the FTD-0509 currency — O(1) adopted structure regardless of trajectory length.
- **T-free (explicitly not registered):** each vertex carries an independent free phase. Per-event choice data; **extensive** in the FTD-0509 currency — O(N·log₂ k) bits over N events. By the FTD-0509 Outcome-B reading, extensive purchases are precisely the class the framework has always declined (FC-1, FC-2). T-free is therefore pre-emptively marked *decline-class*; this dossier proposes only T-rule.

## 2 · Why this candidate, why now

Three independent results triangulate the same vertex.

1. **Necessity of some purchase (FTD-0505).** The tick-boundary same-sign collision admits exactly three repair classes — capacity, range, or temporal phase — and the representation-only repair is `[CLOSED NEGATIVE]`. A constructive collision branch must adopt one. The phase horn is the only horn that leaves the ternary alphabet (P3) and the locality radius (P4) untouched: capacity expands the charge alphabet to ≥ 5 symbols, range adds an unforced exclusion radius with measurably distinct currents (minimum difference 0.0368304 across the selected family).
2. **Carrier-class alignment (FTD-0508 Corollary 4).** If the four framework walls unify, the transfer to the δ fiber must be carried by event data — operators (FTD-0244) and finite symmetries (FTD-0314/0326/0327) are closed. A vertex-attached datum is the minimal new event-data structure. This alignment is registered as *motivation only*: no claim that P6C-T reaches δ is made, and yield accounting (§5) counts it at weight 0.
3. **Native precursor (FTD-0323).** The substrate already owns a half-step arrow `∂_t^{1/2}` with a cyclotomic branch sign — an operator-level half-phase. T-rule would be its event-level counterpart. Suggestive placement, not evidence.

## 3 · Independence status (why this is an adoption, not a derivation)

The trilemma itself establishes non-forcedness: three repair classes exist, so no one of them is forced by the frozen register — choosing the phase horn is a selection among alternatives. Within the horn, rule R's form is likewise unforced until a narrowing theorem says otherwise. Both selections must be declared and priced. Conversely, if Stage-1 work (§4) proves some τ-rule *derivable* from the frozen ontology — that the vertex data already determines a unique consistent phase — then P6C-T is superseded by a derivation and leaves the register as a win, per the FC-W kill-condition pattern.

## 6 · Falsifiers and exits

- **Supersession (welcome):** a unique consistent τ-rule proves derivable from frozen vertex data — the phase was never an import; the trilemma's phase horn retires as content.
- **Closure negative:** every candidate R fails the conservativity/covariance/quotient gates — the horn closes, FTD-0505 sharpens to a dilemma (capacity or range).
- **Pricing out:** Stage 1 returns a family so large that log₂|family| makes y/c uncompetitive under ratified D6 — the candidate stays registered but unadopted, a priced no-buy.
