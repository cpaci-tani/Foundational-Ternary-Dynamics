## 2 · What the act-lens adds (and what it does not)

The act-lens is the reason FC-W is *honest rather than ad hoc*: a square root with two structurally-admissible branches (`+δ`, `−δ`) is precisely an **act** in the FTD-0325 sense — an unforced ℤ/2 branch-choice — exactly the kind of thing a substrate of forced magnitudes cannot perform for itself. So FC-W is the **second** act after FC-0's `i = √(−1)`: `i` is the act the substrate is *read as* taking (it builds the spine and `G*`); `δ` is the act the substrate *cannot* take (it selects the α-root). "The universe is chosen twice at the field level" (FTD-0325) is, in register terms, FC-0 and FC-W.

It adds **no new commitment, no derivation, and no promotion.** It does not make α `[DERIVED]` from P1–P5 (α remains *declared* via FC-W, never derived); it does not promote FTD-0013 (`x₊ ↔ 1/α` stays `[SMC]` — FC-W selects the algebraic branch, it does not prove the branch *is* the EM coupling); it does not touch FTD-0244, FTD-0242's closed-negative routes, or the engine.

## 3 · Why no second α-selection commitment is minted

The act-count arc's "declare the δ-branch" and the carrier arc's "adopt the external binding law W" select the *same surd by the same kind of move with the same payoff and the same kill-criterion*. The register therefore carries **one** α-selection commitment — **FC-W (FTD-0315), the constitution's FC-4** — and this document is its act-of-intent exposition.
