## 1 · Why a declaration is the honest option (and what it is not)

FTD-0318 sharpened MC-T4.3: the substrate **provably cannot** select the master-quadratic root `x₊ = 8G*² + 4G*·δ` by any native symmetry, because performing the `δ ↦ −δ` ℤ/2 requires moving an element outside `ℚ(G*)` while every native operator fixes `ℚ(G*)`. The surviving exit-(i) ("a 6th-postulate-class input forcing the operator," FTD-0242 §7) therefore cannot be a *derivation* — it can only be a **declaration** that the model realizes the branch. This is the same epistemic shape as FC-0: a theorem proves the fork is open (here FTD-0244; for FC-0, the spine-vs-ontology gap), and a commitment **picks the branch FTD asserts the world is** — *a declaration, not a derivation* (constitution §0, the Framework-Commitments register row).

**This proposal therefore makes α `forced-given-FC-4`, exactly as `D = 3` is `forced-given-FC-0`.** It is **not** a derivation of α, **not** a promotion of FTD-0013, and **not** a claim the substrate computes 1/137. Declining FC-4 leaves MC-T4.3 the open obstruction it is today and α at `[STRONGLY MOTIVATED CONJECTURE]`. The choice is the owner's; this is the drafted language.
