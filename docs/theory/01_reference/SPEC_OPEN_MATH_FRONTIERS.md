## F2 — Maximality / canonicality of ℚ(G\*) — **(c), deep external boundary**

**Question.** Theorem 9 proves only that ℚ(G\*) is a **π-free** subfield of ℚ(π, Γ(1/4)) (conditional on Chudnovsky). The audit **struck "maximal"** — it is unproven and, as literally stated, **false**: ℚ(Γ(1/4)) is a strictly larger π-free subfield by the identical Chudnovsky argument.

**What closing requires.** A genuine maximality statement would need a lattice argument over π-free subfields of ℚ(π, Γ(1/4)) — i.e. characterizing the transcendence-degree structure of all intermediate fields. That is a **Schanuel-conjecture-class** problem in transcendence theory; FTD cannot close it internally.

**What FTD *can* honestly state.** A **restricted conjecture**: ℚ(G\*) is maximal *among single-generator π-free subfields generated by a weight-0 Chowla–Selberg constant of the lemniscatic CM motive* — a precisely-scoped statement, marked **[CONJECTURE — restricted]**. The general case is **[OPEN — external]**. The AUDIT_W_CARRIER_NARROWING §3 (C3) result (every CM period of E under its order-2 maps lies in `Q̄(π,Γ(1/4))`) is a partial structural bound on the π-free "reach," not a maximality proof.

**Action:** drop "maximal"/"canonical" everywhere (done in `SPEC_ALGEBRAIC_SPINE.md` §9/§0/header); state the restricted conjecture here; do not re-introduce the bare word.

## F3 — Forcing of coefficient-16 and D=3 — **(c), the MC-T4.3 wall (a mapped boundary)**

**Question.** Two distinct objects both equal 16 (the master-quadratic coefficient and |Aut(E)|²) and D=3 solves `2^D(D−1)!=16`; but is the coefficient *forced* to be |Aut(E)|² (not |Aut| or |Aut|³), and is the dimension *forced* to be 3?

**Status (post-audit).** The **arithmetic** facts are `[THEOREM]` (|Aut(E)|²=16; `f(D)=2^D(D−1)!` equals 16 only at D=3, recomputed `f(1..6)={2,4,16,96,768,7680}`). The **forcing** is `[SELECTION]`, not `[THEOREM]`:
- The coefficient's *structural necessity* is the Tier-4 (OT-4.1) gap — true at value level, conjectural as necessity. FTD-0122 (k=4 from |ℤ[i]^×|²) is a **partial** unification (Roles 1+3), with Roles 2+4 a documented no-go.
- The D=3 *forcing* is circular: the LHS |Aut|²=16 is D-independent, and the RHS target is justified via |O_h|/3=48/3, which presupposes D=3.

**What closing requires.** A non-circular dimension functional (whose value at general D is **not** computed via |O_h|) **and** a structural-necessity proof that the EM-coupling polynomial's coefficient must be |Aut(E)|². This is the **same wall as MC-T4.3** (the α-readout operator-forcing obstruction): a 6th-postulate-class input would be required. Marked **[FOUNDATIONAL OBSTRUCTION — boundary]**, route-invariant (FTD-0242/0244/0313/0314). Not closeable internally without a new axiom.

## F5 — Unconditional transcendence of G\* (the Chudnovsky dependency) — **(c), permanent external import**

**Question.** A large fraction of the deep spine — ℚ(G\*) π-freeness (Thm 9), A_k transcendence (k≥4), and the **entire W-narrowing theorem (FTD-0314)** — is conditional on **Chudnovsky 1976** (algebraic independence of π and Γ(1/4)). Can it be made unconditional?
