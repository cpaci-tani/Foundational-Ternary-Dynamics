## §2 · Three readings of the qualifier

The clause underdetermines its object. Three readings are available; before 2026-08-03 they were extensionally identical for FC-W, which is why the choice was invisible. v1 of this document argued a forced two-way choice and was refuted for it; the choice is real and is the first half of the ruling.

**Reading (a) — the statement letter.** The gap-class is the conditional theorem `x₊ = 1/α given W`. It is checkable (arithmetic) and parameter-free (the master quadratic has none). FC-W discharges the qualifier today, trivially — and so does essentially any single-posit candidate, because conditional content is always parameter-free *given the posit*. Under (a) the qualifier excludes nothing: it is vacuous, and it reintroduces at the qualifier level the self-licensing that D6's second guard ("a candidate cannot pay for itself by licensing restatements of its own posit") rejects at the yield level. [DERIVED]

**Reading (b′) — the modal reading.** "Checkable" read as *capability*: the gap-class must carry a declared, non-self-supplied independent check route — a falsifier anchored outside the posit. This is not vacuous (a marginal candidate whose single gap-class is pure structure with no empirical bridge and no declared falsifier fails it), and it is not self-licensing (the discharge comes from the externally-anchored falsifier, not from well-formedness given the posit). FC-W's checkability prong under (b′) rests on **one** route: IMP-E1's falsifier (α measured to disagree beyond the tree-level tolerance), which is declared and external. (IMP-B1's falsifier — a native forced ℤ/2 carrier exhibited — is *supersession*-shaped, not check-shaped: if it fired it would derive the surd and strengthen the gap-class, so it cannot function as a route by which the gap-class could fail; it belongs to §3's supersession discussion, not here.) **And the second prong is impaired even on this one route:** "itself free of tunable parameters" must cover the check's own tolerances, and FTD-0791's registered finding is that both operative tolerances were set by the observed deviation — the falsifier's "tree-level tolerance" is post-hoc — with FTD-0803 adding that CODATA sits at 0.998 half-widths of that window, its edge. Under (b′) FC-W's discharge is therefore *conditional on repairing the tolerance declaration* (declaring the window in advance of any future comparison), not clean. [DERIVED from registered findings]

**Reading (b) — the achieved reading.** "Checkable" read as *achieved discrimination*: the gap-class must have passed a check that could have come out otherwise against a stated null. This is how the spec's own calibration *used* the qualifier — the FTD-0791 record states it directly: "FTD-0387's D7 prices the alpha-root as high-value *because* scan-rigid — that valuation loses its basis." Usage history favours (b). Under (b), on present evidence: the two numerical-uniqueness checks sit at their own nulls (79% family base rate; `N_null = 0.0014`); the one structural check (the registered CM domain) **was discriminating and passed** — but its base-rate determination was retrospective, its dual-match reading beyond the registered domain is a range artifact, and the physical target sits at the window edge. Whether that surviving item suffices for "high-value" is a genuine judgment call — it is exactly the second half of the owner's ruling, and this document does not foreclose it. [DERIVED; the (b)-application is the owner's]

**What selects among them.** (a) is eliminable on vacuity — that argument survived audit. Between (b′) and (b) the rules' text does not decide: (b′) is closer to the letter ("checkable"), (b) is how the calibration actually used the word. This is an interpretive ruling about a rule still at `[DRAFT awaiting ratification]`, i.e. precisely an owner sitting's business. What can be said without discretion: **under every non-vacuous reading, FC-W's discharge is weaker than the §3 text currently records** — (b′) leaves it conditional on a tolerance repair; (b) leaves it hanging on one retrospective, range-limited, edge-sited pass. [SYNTHESIS]

One further registered fact narrows the repair space: **no engine result under the current source configuration can discharge the qualifier for FC-W** — the engine runs on CODATA `ALPHA`, and a source-lint CTest fails the build if the derived root reaches a force path (FTD-0792). ## §4 · The ruling, structured

The D7 clause stands: an owner-declared `[AXIOM]` is not subject to disqualification by these rules; nothing below moves FC-W's tag. The ruling has two parts.

**R1 — fix the qualifier's reading** (a D6 matter, since it governs every future marginal candidate):
- **(a)** statement letter — available; vacuous per §2's analysis.
- **(b′)** modal (declared external falsifier route, tolerances declared in advance) — a real rule; weaker gate.
- **(b)** achieved (a passed discriminating check against a stated null) — matches the calibration's own usage; stronger gate.
- **(b-prereg)** — (b) sharpened to a decidable predicate: *the check's base-rate control must itself be preregistered* (null family and outcome thresholds declared before execution). Under this predicate, on the current record, all three of FC-W's checks fail it — including FTD-0321's, whose control was retrospective — so it is the strictest option, and it needs one declared sub-rule: whether a retrospective control can be cured (e.g. by preregistered replication on an extended domain). The null-family choice is itself a selection and carries its own F10 risk; preregistration of the family is the guard.

**R2 — apply the fixed reading to FC-W** (the deferred D7 decision):
- Under **(a)**: marginal-admissible, vacuously. The §3 sentence gets rewritten to say so explicitly — the recorded cost is that the qualifier then does no work anywhere.
- Under **(b′)**: marginal-admissible **conditionally** — the record must state the tolerance-prong impairment (post-hoc window, target at 0.998 half-widths) and the repair (declare the tolerance in advance of any future α comparison; CODATA currently knows α to 0.08 ppb, so the declaration is cheap and binding).
- Under **(b)**: the owner weighs the single surviving pass (registered CM domain: discriminating, passed, retrospective, range-limited, edge-sited) against "high-value." If judged insufficient: **FC-W is flagged sub-marginal on the record** — `[AXIOM]` standing untouched, grandfathered under the D7 non-adjudication clause — and the floor re-anchors: **matching FC-W's `y/c = 1` no longer functions as a passing precedent**; each marginal candidate must discharge the qualifier on its own showing, as §2's letter always required. The §4 illustrative table consequence: P6C-C, P6C-G, P6C-M lose the reading "marginal — same grade as FC-W"; each needs its own showing.
- Under **(b-prereg)**: the flag follows mechanically (no preregistered control passed), and every future marginal candidate inherits a decidable gate.

**Advisory (not a ruling):** the combination that converts this week into durable methodology is **R1 = (b-prereg)** with the cure sub-rule declared, **R2 = the mechanical consequence**, and the tolerance-declaration repair from (b′) adopted regardless — it costs one sentence in the spec and closes the post-hoc-window defect for good. Two live alternatives: **R1 = (b)**, R2 owner-weighed (minimal, honest, but leaves "high-value" a judgment call each sitting must re-make); and **R1 = (b′)** with the tolerance repair (a real rule, closest to the clause's letter — not recommended here only because it grades a gap-class by the existence of a falsifier rather than by anything the falsifier has yet discriminated, which is the standard the calibration's own usage history set). (a) is not recommended, for §2's vacuity reason. The reading choice itself is the owner's; draft r1 was refuted precisely for trying to force it.
