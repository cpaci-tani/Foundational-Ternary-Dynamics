# Audit — minimal relational matter ontology v1

**Ledger ID:** FTD-0669  
**Status:** `[FOUNDATIONAL SYNTHESIS — NO CLAIM PROMOTION]`

The synthesis defines a matter object as a localized invariant or metastable
family of complete manifestation--constituent--field states, modulo declared
translations, cubic transformations, and constituent relabellings. This is a
selected definition at the current research-ontology level.

The document does not claim that the five postulates force constituent
position, momentum, binding relations, or face/edge connections. It prices
those as the minimal extension presently sufficient for the constructive
engine evidence. It separates:

- primitive substrate types;
- selected constituent/connection/action types;
- exact content derived after those types are selected;
- emergent candidates still requiring stable orbit families and operational
  readouts.

No electron, photon, quantum state, spin, statistics, charge, mass, lifetime,
particle pole, or Lorentz claim is promoted. FTD-0675 retracts the
FTD-0666/0668/0670 modal return and turning claims because their displacement
projection omitted the mass metric. The relational synthesis remains
supported by exact common action, motion, state-only inversion, co-moving
dressing, pre-return outward field generation, and the exact matter-memory
kernel and FTD-0676's amplitude-stable reduced-coordinate transfer; it is not
supported by a demonstrated localized complete-state pole.

The synthesis is falsifiable through its ten operational object criteria and
seven escalation conditions. Failure of localization, state-only identity, or
two-object separability would close the current constituent pattern as an
adequate matter ontology and trigger a priced type extension rather than a
post-hoc force repair.
