**What FTD genuinely is not.** FTD is not a derivation of the Standard Model and not a "zero free parameters" theory. The lattice engine, run as a disciplined instrument through a multi-month EFT program, has produced **no derivation of physics** — it has produced a rigorous map of where discreteness does *not* reach, plus one genuine positive structural finding (L-invariant cluster counts).

**The defining feature.** The honest state of FTD is an *asymmetry*: an excellent epistemic apparatus and a real but small mathematical spine, with a sequence of honest negatives — not a derivation — connecting them to physics. The algebraic spine and the engine are two real pillars with no span between them, and the gap is structural (the engine stencil is `(SC+FCC)/2`, orthogonal to the BCC sublattice where `16G*²` lives), not merely unsolved. The framework's central physics question — the operational readout rule that would map the algebraically-distinguished root to a coupling matter actually measures (MC-T4.3) — is open, and the project's own diagnosis is candid that closing it may require extending the ontology.
