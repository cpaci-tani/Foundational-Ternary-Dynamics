## Findings

1. **Finite rigid smearing does not cure diagonal energy centering.** The
   mismatch factorizes as `B_M A M_d`. For any nonzero finite Laurent symbol
   `A`, the integral-domain property forbids exact cancellation of the
   nonzero edge or body factor.

2. **The axial exception is exact but does not extend to Moore diagonals.**
   `M_axial=0`, while edge and body symbols contain irreducible mixed-axis
   products. The observer confirms this across all 26 signed directions.

3. **Finite rigid smearing does not remove lattice self-pinning.** The
   Peierls coefficient is a nonnegative spectral integral and is strictly
   positive for every nonzero finite-support source.

4. **Neutral multipoles do not evade the theorem.** The registered axial
   dipole, body dipole, and plaquette quadrupole all retain positive
   centering mismatch on diagonal paths and positive Peierls barriers.

5. **Polarity reversal cannot cancel the barrier.** The coefficient depends
   on `|A|^2`; all polarity-mirror residuals are zero at printed precision.

6. **The direct observer independently reproduces the Fourier theorem.** All
   520 centering arms agree within `6.90e-17`; all 540 potential samples obey
   the exact quadratic law within `3.04e-17`.

7. **Smooth extension suppresses rather than solves the defects.** The
   binomial control has `Pi_i~1/(2N)` and exact relative centering norms
   falling as `O(N^-2)` in squared norm, hence `O(N^-1)` in relative L2
   amplitude.

8. **The suppression rate is not the earlier quadratic-coat rate.** The
   present CIC/Hodge barrier contains one factor of `1-cos(k_i)` and falls as
   `O(R_rms^-2)` relative to self-energy, whereas FTD-0555's quadratic-coat
   control contained two powers and fell as `O(R^-4)`.

9. **A continuous binomial envelope is not native ternary matter.** It is a
   selected comparison family generated by repeated local averaging. Its
   favorable scaling supplies no production mechanism that creates or
   preserves the profile.

10. **The entire finite rigid exact-cure branch is closed negative.** The
    remaining loopholes require changing the class: internal deformation,
    noncompact/band-limited support, integer hopping, or an energy-centered
    multistage action.

11. **Production remains unchanged.** No movement phase, force branch,
    toggle, default, scenario, renderer, or primitive state was modified.

12. **FTD-0580 resolves centering by leaving the rigid trilinear class, not by
    refuting this no-go.** Its endpoint chord is time-dependent morphology;
    the positive Peierls barrier remains.
