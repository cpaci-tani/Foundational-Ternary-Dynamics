## 0. Verdict

The physical intuition is sound but the current engine cannot yet instantiate it:

> A confining interaction can make a massive composite by trapping a finite, nonzero energy in a state whose total momentum vanishes. Gravity should couple to the same energy–momentum object; rest mass is its invariant, not a second substance.

The current engine has no single object playing those roles. Its colour force changes particle momentum, but the corresponding interaction energy is absent from the energy audit, energy ledger, declared Hamiltonian, and gravitational source. Cluster inertia consumes `M_REST` directly. The latency source independently consumes `M_REST·|s|` plus an optional ordinary-field proxy on CPU, while force-side gravity follows a separate ordinary-flux-density gradient. The GPU latency path does not implement the CPU field-energy option. RenderBridge and ParticleEngine also use different long-range confinement laws.

Therefore no current-engine run can demonstrate that *confinement energy became inertial mass and then sourced gravity*. Any such reading would cross between independently imposed bookkeeping paths.

This is a no-go for the present dataflow, not for confinement-generated mass in principle.

## 1. The bridge that would be required

For an isolated lattice excitation, a target-blind mass definition requires one conserved energy–momentum pair in a declared coordinate convention. In raw engine lattice units this is

\[
E^2=(M c_{\rm lat}^2)^2+c_{\rm lat}^2|\mathbf P|^2,
\qquad c_{\rm lat}=C_{\rm SPEED}=1/\sqrt3.
\]

In the centre-of-momentum frame, `P=0` and `M c_lat²=E`. A confining sector creates mass only in the precise sense that its dynamics admits a localized state with a nonzero, history-independent minimum of this same `E`. Gravity then couples to the corresponding stress–energy, not to a separately attached mass label. FTD-0401 subsequently proved that the current engine lacks the explicit raw-velocity and mass/rest-energy normalization needed even to instantiate this equation; its Causal Normalization and Mass-Role gate now precedes NCEMC.

At minimum, one common strong-sector Hamiltonian contribution `H_strong` must satisfy all three interfaces:

1. **Dynamics:** the colour force is generated by `H_strong` (a lattice work/gradient relation).
2. **Inertia:** `H_strong` contributes to the conserved `E` and `P` from which invariant mass is computed.
3. **Gravity:** the same contribution enters the gravitational source, with its momentum and stress terms when leaving the static weak-field regime.

If the force acts while `H_strong` is missing from either of the latter interfaces, confinement cannot be credited with the measured inertial or gravitational mass.

## 3. Scoped no-go

Assume a current-engine campaign reports a mass change after enabling colour confinement.

- If it reads cluster acceleration, the denominator is already `N·M_REST`; the mass was supplied to the integrator.
- If it reads CPU latency, the source contains `M_REST·|s|` and optionally ordinary `J/wave_vel` energy, not the strong interaction energy that generated the colour force.
- If it reads `EnergyAudit.total_energy` or the `EnergyLedger`, the active colour interaction has no potential term in the reported total.
- If it uses GPU `strong_energy`, that quantity excludes strong wave energy, is excluded from total energy, and is excluded from the GPU latency source.
- If it attempts the native invariant `E²=(M c_lat²)²+c_lat²P²`, RenderBridge has no complete total-momentum observable, its `E` omits the strong interaction, and FTD-0401 shows its `M_REST` roles are not normalized consistently.

Every available route therefore imports at least one of the desired conclusions through a separate path. No arrangement of current toggles closes the triangle.

## 4. The double-counting fork

The CPU latency source currently has the schematic form

\[
\rho_g=M_{\rm REST}|s|+\rho_{J,\dot J}.
\]

This is consistent only after the ontology of the two terms is fixed:

- If `M_REST·|s|` is an independent core energy and `ρ_J` is additional field energy, rest mass remains an imposed core property; confinement did not generate it.
- If `M_REST` is intended to summarize the confined field energy, adding both terms counts the same physical contribution twice.
- If mass is to emerge entirely from field dynamics, the independent `M_REST·|s|` source must disappear from the derivation path and the complete strong Hamiltonian must replace the incomplete proxy. That architecture is not implemented.

The audit does not choose among these readings. Choosing would be a framework decision, not a derivation.

## 5. Why confinement remains the right intuition

For intuition only—not as an FTD formula—a localized relativistic excitation often balances a localization term against a confining term,

\[
E(R)\sim A/R+B\sigma R.
\]

The competition selects a finite radius and a nonzero minimum energy. In a rest frame that minimum is a mass. The important feature is not the particular formula; it is the existence of a dynamically selected minimum that is independent of how the excitation was prepared.

FTD-0391/0399 found that the tested birth histories do not yet supply that invariant: retained energies were circumstance-dependent, and the larger-lattice three-history particlehood comparator failed its manifestation-portability gate. FTD-0400 shows that even a stable object would still lack the common energy–momentum/gravity bridge needed to interpret its confined energy as inertial and gravitational mass.

4. **NCEMC-4 — field-only gravity discriminator:** source the weak static gravitational potential from the same complete energy density with the independent `M_REST·|s|` term disabled. Beyond the static regime, include the corresponding momentum and stress components rather than `T00` alone.

6. **NCEMC-6 — calibration boundary:** success can derive a lattice-unit gap and dimensionless ratios. It cannot derive a value in MeV without the FTD-0096 calibration interface.
