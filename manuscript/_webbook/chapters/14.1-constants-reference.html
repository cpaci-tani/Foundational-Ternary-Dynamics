81.4.3 Proton Mass: Two Paths to the Same Number

The proton-to-electron mass ratio admits two independent derivations that converge through G*.

\[ \frac{m_p}{m_e} = 6\pi^5 = 1836.118 \quad \text{(0.002\% error)} \]

\[ \frac{m_p}{m_e} = \frac{N_{\text{eff}}}{\alpha} + T(10) = \frac{13}{1/137.036} + 55 = 1836.47 \quad \text{(0.017\% error)} \]

\[ 6\pi^5 \approx (G^*)^7 \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{N_{\text{eff}}}\right) = (G^*)^7 \times \frac{12}{13} \]

Both formulas converge because the lemniscatic constant G* encodes the relationship between circular geometry (π) and framework integers. The factor (1 - 1/N_eff) = 12/13 connects the pure-π formula to the integer-based one.
