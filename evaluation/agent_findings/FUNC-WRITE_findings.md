## Executive Summary

### S2: Clear Mathematical Exposition

### S3: Strong Internal Consistency
Cross-references between chapters are systematic and functional.

### S4: Calibrated Caveats and Disclaimers

## Critical Weaknesses Identified

### W1: Occasional Purple Prose [MODERATE]
Philosophical/cosmological sections slip into overly poetic language:
> "This is not failure. This is the pattern. This is home."

### W2: Inconsistent Citation Practices [HIGH]
- Some chapters use proper academic citations
- Others reference results without formal citation
- Placeholder citations present

### W3: Informal Register in Some Sections [MODERATE]
Occasional informal language: "The math works."

### W4: Scope Creep in Claims [HIGH]
"TOE completeness" and "100% framework completeness" are extraordinary claims requiring extraordinary justification.

### W5: Redundancy Across Documents [LOW]
Same derivations appear in multiple locations with slight variations.

### W6: Missing Error Analysis [MODERATE]
Systematic error propagation and uncertainty quantification absent.

---

## Recommendations

1. Citation standardization with BibTeX/CSL system
2. Register calibration - remove informal language
3. Claims hierarchy audit
4. Add error propagation section
5. Consolidate derivations to single authoritative source
6. Revise philosophical chapters as appendix
