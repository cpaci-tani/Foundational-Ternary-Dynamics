Push or Overlay?

When the panel is docked to the left or right, how should it relate to the 3D viewport?

A

Push — viewport reflows

The viewport shrinks to fit next to the sidebar. No content is ever hidden; panel and scene coexist. Camera aspect ratio changes when you toggle sides. Best when you're using panels + scene together for long stretches.

Sidebar
3D Viewport (narrower)
B

Overlay — floats above

The viewport stays full-width behind the panel; sidebar is a translucent glass panel over the scene (like today's bottom dock). Scene camera never changes on toggle; panel can be collapsed to a thin edge. Best when the scene is the hero and panels are reference.

3D Viewport (full bleed)
Sidebar (glass)
C

Hybrid — push on side, overlay on bottom

Left/right mounts push (clean viewport edges, no occlusion). Bottom mount overlays (keeps today's behavior so status bar + viewport don't get crushed vertically). One policy per axis.
