==============================================================================
INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================

Ergodic ratio = (within-seed temporal std) / (across-seed ensemble std)

  ratio ≈ 1: ergodic. Temporal sampling = ensemble sampling.
              Regime 4 entropy contribution similar to spatial.
  ratio < 1: cluster is 'frozen' in time (each seed converges to a
              specific N, doesn't fluctuate); ensemble variance comes
              from initial-condition divergence (regimes 1-3 dominate).
  ratio > 1: cluster fluctuates in time more than across seeds;
              regime-4 entropy dominates spatial entropy.

Autocorrelation reveals the timescale of temporal entropy generation:
  - Fast decay (ac drops to 0 within a few lags): high-frequency churn
  - Slow decay (ac stays > 0.5 for many lags): slow drift, low-freq dynamics
