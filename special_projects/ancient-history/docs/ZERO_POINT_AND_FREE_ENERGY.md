## Part III: The Casimir Effect—Proof of Concept

### 3.1 What It Is

Two uncharged conducting plates placed very close together experience attraction.

**Mechanism**:
- Vacuum fluctuations exist everywhere
- Between plates, only certain wavelengths "fit"
- Outside plates, all wavelengths exist
- Pressure imbalance → net force

This is **experimentally verified**. The vacuum is not empty.

### 3.2 Energy Extraction?

Can the Casimir effect extract usable energy?

**Problem**: To extract energy, you'd need to:
1. Let plates fall together (extracts energy)
2. Pull them apart (requires energy input)
3. Net: zero (or negative, due to losses)

**Unless**: You can change the vacuum energy density between cycles.

**FTD possibility**: If you can modulate vacuum flux density (via external fields, rotation, resonance), the Casimir force would vary, enabling net extraction.

### 3.3 Dynamic Casimir Effect

Rapidly oscillating a mirror can **create photons from vacuum**.

This is experimentally verified (2011, Chalmers University).

**Implication**: The vacuum CAN be made to release energy under the right conditions. The question is whether this can be scaled to useful levels.
