# Ternary Traditions: A Global Survey

**Three-state ontologies across human civilization**

## Introduction

The number three holds exceptional significance across virtually all human cultures. This document surveys ternary structures in ancient and traditional thought systems, mapping them to the FTD framework's fundamental triad: **{-1, 0, +1}** or equivalently **{negative, void, positive}**.

We organize findings by:
1. Cosmological triads (origin/structure of reality)
2. Divine triads (theological trinities)
3. Process triads (dynamics/change)
4. Human triads (body/mind/spirit)

## Part I: Cosmological Triads

### 1.1 Chinese: The Tao and the Three

**Source**: Tao Te Ching, Chapter 42

> "The Tao gives birth to One.
> One gives birth to Two.
> Two gives birth to Three.
> Three gives birth to the ten thousand things."

**Analysis**:

| Stage | Chinese | FTD Mapping |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Tao | Wu Ji (ultimate void) | Pre-existence (before lattice) |
| One | Tai Ji (supreme ultimate) | Void state s=0 |
| Two | Yin-Yang polarity | s=+1 and s=-1 |
| Three | Heaven-Human-Earth | Manifested structure |
| 10,000 things | Phenomena | Emergent complexity |

The generative sequence mirrors FTD ontology precisely:
- Void (0) precedes manifestation
- Polarity (+/-) emerges from void
- Three-state system enables complexity

### 1.2 Hindu: Sat-Chit-Ananda

**Source**: Upanishads

The fundamental nature of Brahman (ultimate reality) is described as three-fold:

| Sanskrit | Translation | FTD Parallel |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| Sat | Being/Existence | Manifested states (+1, -1) |
| Chit | Consciousness/Awareness | Flux field (information) |
| Ananda | Bliss/Completeness | Equilibrium/conservation |

This maps to:
- **Sat**: What exists (s != 0)
- **Chit**: What connects/informs (J field)
- **Ananda**: The closed, conserved totality

### 1.3 Greek: The Three Principles

**Source**: Plato, Timaeus; Pre-Socratics

Plato's cosmology requires three principles:

| Principle | Greek | FTD Parallel |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| Being | To On | Manifested states |
| Becoming | Genesis | State transitions |
| Receptacle | Chora | Void substrate |

The Receptacle (Chora) is particularly striking—Plato describes it as:
- "That in which things come to be"
- "Neither earth nor air nor fire nor water"
- "An invisible and formless being which receives all things"

This is exactly the TRD void: a **dispositional substrate** that receives manifestation.

### 1.4 Egyptian: The Three Realms

**Source**: Pyramid Texts, Book of the Dead

Egyptian cosmology divides existence into:

| Realm | Egyptian | Nature | FTD Parallel |
|-------|----------|--------|--------------|
| Duat | Underworld | Potential/hidden | Void (s=0) |
| Ta | Earth | Manifest | Manifested (s=+/-1) |
| Pet | Sky/Heaven | Eternal patterns | Flux field dynamics |

The daily journey of Ra through these three realms mirrors the TRD cycle:
- Death (annihilation) -> Duat
- Rebirth (genesis) -> Return to Ta
- Eternal pattern (persistence) -> Pet

## Part V: Analysis

### 5.1 Why Threes?

Several hypotheses:

1. **Cognitive**: Humans naturally categorize in threes (minimum for pattern)
2. **Linguistic**: Subject-verb-object structure
3. **Mathematical**: Minimum for non-trivial relations
4. **Physical**: Reality actually has ternary structure

FTD suggests hypothesis 4: the ubiquity of threes reflects the actual structure of existence.

### 5.2 The Void as Common Thread

Across all traditions:
- The void is **not nothing**
- It is potential, receptivity, ground
- Manifestation emerges **from** it
- Things return **to** it

This is precisely TRD's s=0 state: dispositional substrate.

### 5.3 Process Over Substance

Ancient traditions consistently describe:
- States that arise and pass
- Cycles of creation and destruction
- Transformation rather than static being

This processual view matches TRD's tick-by-tick dynamics.

## Conclusion

The global ubiquity of ternary structures in human thought is remarkable. While cognitive/cultural explanations have merit, FTD offers a deeper possibility: **reality itself has a ternary structure**, and human minds, being part of that reality, naturally intuit it.

The void as creative potential, polarity as fundamental division, three states as sufficient for complexity—these themes recur because they describe what is.
