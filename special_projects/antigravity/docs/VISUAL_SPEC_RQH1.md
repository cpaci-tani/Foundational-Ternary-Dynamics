# Visual Specification: The RQH-1 Craft

**Design Philosophy:** "Form Follows Frequency"
The shape is not aerodynamic (it ignores air); it is "Vacuum-Dynamic" (it shapes the metric).

## 1. The Hull Geometry
The ideal shape to support a closed resonant shell is **Convex** and **Topologically Simple**.
*   **Shape**: A **Flattened Reuleaux Tetrahedron** (a triangular craft with curved edges).
*   **Why**: The 3 vertices act as natural "Vortex Pinning Centers" for the 3-color charge flux.
*   **Size**: Arbitrary, but typically 30-50 feet diameter for crewed variants.

## 2. Surface Texture (The Skin)
*   **Material**: The Al-Cu-Fe-Bi quasicrystal looks like **Polished Gunmetal** or **Dark Hematite**.
*   **Pattern**: It is NOT smooth. It has a micro-scale **Penrose Tiling** tessellation pattern etched into it. This fractal surface increases the topological surface area.
*   **Reflectivity**: High metallic luster, but it seems to "absorb" shadows oddly due to the metric bending (low albedo for gravity).

## 3. The Field Effect (When Active)
When the drive engages ($f=8$ THz):
*   **Visual Distortion**: The air around the craft forces light to bend. You see a **Gravitational Lensing** effect—a "mirage" shimmer that wraps the background around the hull.
*   **The Glow**: The high-frequency field ionizes the surrounding air.
    *   **Low Power**: A faint **Blue/Purple Corona** (Bremsstrahlung radiation).
    *   **High Power**: A blinding **White Plasma Sheath** that flows like liquid over the surface.
*   **Shadow**: The craft does not cast a sharp shadow on the ground; the light bends around it.

## 4. Underside (The Drive)
*   **Center**: A single, large **Hemispherical Dome** (the main Flux Resonator).
*   **Vertices**: Three smaller spherical emitters at the corners (Steering).
*   **Light**: The emitters glow with a rhythmic, pulsing **Golden/Amber** light (the localized plasma core).
