## Analytic inspection before execution

migration. SC admission fills only an entirely blank pair; subsequent crossing
and rotation preserve occupied slot count at that owner. FCC relations have no
admission and preserve owner occupancy forever. Field population can decrease

## Observables and rejection criteria

The CUDA campaign records every occupied field slot with its actual local ell
and s, every occupied relation slot with phase/polarity code and relevant saved
gate/admission control, every physical microtick, complete-record SHA256, and
the actual per-stage event log. These are constituent records, not species IDs.
Counts and displacement increments remain exact integers. Relation ownership
and physical relation endpoints are recorded as different concepts.

Unique field transitions define external analysis segments. A two-to-two
collision terminates both incoming segments and starts separate outgoing
segments attached to one ambiguous exchange vertex; no individual parent is
assigned to either child. Admission terminates the field record through named
expiry and starts relation residency. Streaming displacements are accumulated
using signed lattice hops without folding at a periodic seam. Segment lengths
are physical-tick durations; segments ending at the horizon are right-censored.

Every step must conserve total and polarity-resolved token counts, match actual
event exchanges, respect the stage's field mapping, and preserve relation-owner
occupancy except for explicitly logged SC additions. Any discrepancy rejects
the instrument/law match and prevents acceptance of campaign conclusions.
Initial and final complete-state hashes cross-check the independent Python
codec; intermediary hashes identify actual device-produced records without
asserting that sparse observer data can reconstruct all hidden control arrays.

M1 transport of relation-supported carriers requires movement of their
constituent anchors and therefore faces the explicit structural obstruction.
Field displacement alone, conserved tokens, or fixed-anchor recurrence does
not pass M1. M2 additionally requires demonstrated formation, transport and
restoring response of a bound composite with complete exchanges; this matrix
does not supply those proofs. No period, separation threshold, or apparent
finite-time cluster automatically enables M1/M2. Positive field-only patterns
would be recorded as candidates needing a new, separately registered test.

the exact registration and manifest before any GPU trajectory executes.

before producing a bounded report. The run is ineligible if the lock, law,
table, case inventory, clock, initial state, trace or endpoint evidence differs.
