# Recovery successor requirements, version 1

Date: 2026-09-05. Proposal identity: `strict-recovery-requirements-v1`.
Status: **[PROPOSAL — PRICED REQUIREMENTS; NO SUCCESSOR LAW ADOPTED]**.

This is a design contract for a separately versioned research successor to
`phi-v2-staged-candidate-1`. It is not an implemented transition table, a new
canonical law, or a claim that its proposed types suffice for matter. Existing
candidate checkpoints and evidence retain their identities. The
[material audit](AUDIT_STRICT_MATERIAL_MACRO_RECOVERY.md) supplies the structural
obstructions; the [transport result](DERIV_STRICT_TRANSPORT_SECTOR.md) supplies
an exact positive sector that a successor must explicitly preserve or retire.
Canonical status remains governed by the project LEDGER and active constitution.

## Design decision that must precede implementation

Two useful research routes are distinguished here; they do not exhaust all
possible interpretations. Route A seeks interacting mobile collective patterns
in the existing field records or mixed field/relation records. A mobile phase
defect or excess pattern on an occupied background need not move an anchor's
token count. Such a pattern remains a current-law research possibility, requiring
its own observable, interaction and transport evidence. Route A first tests the
present law without adding reverse exchange or identifying the occupied anchor
set itself as a moving body. Route B treats individual anchored relation tokens
as transported, emitting material constituents. It needs mechanisms absent
from the present law: emission and transport of constituent support.
Requirements below are conditional on that interpretation; they are not a
theorem that every finite ontology needs these types.

A successor proposal must name its chosen route and completely enumerate its
state alphabet, legal schedules, local ownership, initialization, boundary
behavior and transition map before any success campaign. Every selected map,
additional bit and experimental calibration receives a price. There is no
target spectrum, force curve or named particle built into the transition.

## R1: bidirectional local exchange

**Required for relation-based emitting matter.** Specify finite transactions
that transfer token content both from field to relation records and back.
Each transaction lists source slots, destination slots, polarity, channel and
phase selection, capacity conflicts, accounting changes and completion tick.
One-way absorption alone cannot supply this mechanism: under the current law
the field count only decreases and occupied anchors never empty.

Calling a new emission transaction the inverse of absorption requires more
information than the present absorbed record contains. The many-to-one map
forgets the incident flag/channel information. An exact inverse needs retained
finite provenance sufficient to distinguish the complete preimages, including
any other erased context. If a fiber has M possible distinct inputs, selecting
that input later requires at least M distinguishable retained states (at least
ceil(log2 M) bits when using a binary encoding). No universal numeric M is
claimed until the full absorption map and its admissible domain are enumerated.

Alternatively, emission can use a newly declared deterministic selection map.
That is a priced new transaction, not recovery of an erased history. Its
orientation, phase and polarity dependence must be explicit and falsifiable.
External diagnostic logs cannot supply its missing ontic input. Reverse exchange
does not by itself establish reversibility of the complete law or its expiry.

**Fixed rejection tests:** enumerate every admissible exchange neighborhood and
destination conflict; reject unexplained per-polarity accounting changes,
duplicate consumption, duplicate writes, access to expired or external history,
or any purported inverse that fails complete-record roundtrip on its declared
domain. For a selected emission map, test the published map instead of asserting
a roundtrip that was never promised.

## R2: mobile constituents and observable lineage

**Required for identifying mobile composites.** Specify whether constituents
are field slots, relation slots, a finite composite record, or an observer's
identified pattern. A route identifying the occupied anchor set itself with a
translating body must supply a causal local transfer of occupied support between
anchors, or abandon that specific interpretation. A moving collective defect
in mixed records need not move that support; it instead requires a demonstrated
propagating observable and complete exchange accounting. Primary/reserve exchange
at one fixed anchor is not anchor transfer.

Keep constituent histories as external finite transition graphs unless the
proposed law actually needs identity records. At an ambiguous many-input
collision, retain the whole interaction vertex; do not assign individual
parents without a declared rule and its price. A bookkeeping identifier must
not become an unbounded ontic alphabet. If persistent identities are proposed
as ontic records, specify their finite cardinality, reuse and expiry rules.

**Fixed rejection tests:** account for every constituent at every intermediate
stage; reject unexplained births, losses and invented collision identities.
Test translated preparations, all admitted orientations and seam crossings.
A mobile-composite campaign must preregister size, constituent content,
localization criterion, lifetime horizon, perturbations, transport distance and
failure threshold. Mere stationarity, visual persistence, or recurrence of a
quotient is insufficient evidence for binding or a particle identification.

## R3: sustainable interaction density

The registered half-occupation preparation makes the current exactly-two
collision gate exceptionally rare. A new campaign must analytically price its
event probability and executable event budget before collecting evidence.
Changing preparation, occupancy capacity or collision eligibility is a declared
change, with a separately locked hypothesis. A favorable event count does not
establish equilibration, mixing or a continuum closure.

Before implementation, publish finite gate domains and the full action on every
admissible local occupancy. If a wider collision sector is proposed, specify
which finite conserved quantities survive and prove that its parallel writes
remain unambiguous. Do not introduce a fitted macroscopic relaxation time as a
hidden primitive clock. Any claimed relaxation time must be measured or bounded
for the registered state family, observable and norm.

**Fixed rejection tests:** reject a campaign whose declared event budget cannot
be reached within its frozen hardware/time limits; report the resulting
feasibility obstruction. Freeze wavelength, amplitude, orientations, error norm,
sampling error and physical-time horizon. Reject the claimed recovery if the
complete error budget fails; do not retune the preparation after seeing failure.

## R4: finite causal reservation when necessary

Competing cross-anchor exchange may need a request/reserve/commit protocol. This
is a possible implementation type, not a compulsory number of stages. Every
request, acknowledgment, reservation and cancellation then belongs to complete
finite state at a declared owner. Each propagation consumes a real microtick.
The neighborhood proof must cover these records, not only committed tokens.

Price the number of control states per owner, the maximum in-flight payload,
any finite timeout, the extra schedule phases and their effect on measured
transport. A request that loses arbitration must have a deterministic fate.
Expiry names the discarded information and its accounting consequences. A
periodic probe must separately document why wraparound does not contaminate a
claimed no-boundary observation interval.

**Fixed rejection tests:** reject any output/control dependency beyond radius
one in one microtick, duplicate reservation, deadlock forbidden by the declared
progress contract, or unaccounted timeout loss. Checkpoint and replay every
phase, including occupied reservations and concurrent conflicts. No worker,
GPU pass or renderer frame may collapse physical handshake stages into one tick.

## R5: upstream and physical gates

Before positive recovery experiments, freeze the proposed law and table hashes
and rerun complete alphabet/shape/ownership checks, structural causal proofs,
adversarial intermediate-record witnesses, exact accounting, expiry semantics
and checkpoint replay. Port only a candidate that passes those gates. Require
complete-state and event parity, serial/parallel determinism, stale-generation
rejection and lifecycle checks on every backend claimed in the result.

New state types invalidate previous closure assumptions until checked. Exact
block observations must retain new pending records and boundary transfers;
autonomous coarse evolution needs a new quotient proof or a declared approximate
domain and bound. Finite-ensemble experiments retain complete states and exact
multiplicities. A marginal projection cannot replace unresolved correlations.

Material promotion then requires the independent M2–M5 evidence in the material
audit: bound-state response and exchange; justified spectral/state/bonding
mechanisms; constitutive behavior with relaxation and finite-scale error; and
causal field/matter backreaction with one independently justified account of
inertia, material clocks and light propagation. Token conservation alone is
not physical energy, and an imposed Newtonian force is not a gravity recovery.

The fixed falsifier of each recovery claim is failure of its preregistered
observable, horizon or complete error budget under the frozen candidate. Failure
retains its evidence and yields a bounded obstruction or a newly versioned
proposal. Passing software gates authorizes experiments, not physical names.

## Deliverable required to advance this proposal

A design agent must next supply an explicit finite transition specification for
one selected route, an alphabet/price table with actual cardinalities, all
conflict cases, stage-support proofs and a locked small-sector experiment.
Independent mathematical, physics and implementation review precede adoption.
This requirements document deliberately does not provide an unspecified
"successor" boolean as a substitute for that design work.
